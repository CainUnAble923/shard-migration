Findings

1. **risk** - `server/customizations/Services/MiniChampionSystem/MiniChampionSpawn.cs:102` and `server/customizations/Services/Underworld/Navreys Lair/NavreysController.cs:92`: Generation checks or removes only its own controllers, leaving stock spawners able to produce reward bosses independently of wave completion, including FireElementalRenowned at the Crimson Veins controller's coordinates (`D:/UO/ModernUO-pinned/Distribution/Data/Spawns/post-uoml/termur/Abyss.json:212`, `:228`) and another Navrey beside her lair (`D:/UO/ModernUO-pinned/Distribution/Data/Spawns/post-uoml/termur/Underworld.json:566`, `:575`); importing those spawns alongside the controllers leaves an extra reward source that a solo player can farm without completing the mini champion waves. Reconcile the specific overlapping stock spawners and their existing creatures when enabling each encounter, preserve unrelated spawns, and add an integration test that imports the stock entries before generation and verifies that only the intended encounter owns the bosses.

2. **risk** - `server/customizations/Services/MiniChampionSystem/MiniChampSpawnInfo.cs:39`, `:72`, `:96`: If a save captures a boss death before the next one-second slice credits it, loading can discard its deleted creature reference while retaining Killed = 0, Spawned = 1, and Required = 1, leaving Done false and Respawn permanently blocked by MaxSpawned = 1, so the champion never reaches its restart. Reconcile missing creature references with the saved counters before resuming, or record deaths before saving, and add a round-trip case that saves between the boss death and its accounting slice.

3. **bug** - `server/customizations/Services/MiniChampionSystem/MiniChampionSpawn.cs:317`: Advancing a wave moves its surviving replacement creatures into `_despawns`, but `OnSlice` only calls containment checks on `_spawnList`, so players can lure those older creatures past SpawnRange + 10 and leave them outside the encounter indefinitely if the remaining waves are abandoned. Keep checking the map and range of living `_despawns` until encounter cleanup, and add a case that advances with survivors, moves one beyond the boundary, and verifies its return on the next slice.

4. **bug** - `server/customizations/Services/Underworld/ExperimentalRoom/ExperimentalRoomChest.cs:39`, `:57`: A player can obtain and complete multiple gems before redeeming any, then collect multiple rewards during the same 24-hour period, because activation does not reserve participation and both redemption paths accept completed gems even after the first deletion puts the player on cooldown. Reserve participation when activating a gem, bind redemption to that participation and its owner, and test that preparing another gem before the first redemption cannot buy a second reward during the cooldown through either chest interaction.

5. **bug** - `server/customizations/Services/Underworld/Navreys Lair/NavreysController.cs:297`: A save during rockfall persists Navrey's CantWalk = true, but loading only reconnects her controller and relights the pillars, leaving her unable to walk after the unsaved rockfall timer disappears until players trigger and finish another rockfall. Clear that temporary movement restriction on load or restore the remaining rockfall, and add a round-trip case taken during rain that verifies movement recovers without another pillar activation.

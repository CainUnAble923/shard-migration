# October 24 patch notes

Draft for the next package and server update.

## Launcher

- The launcher shows a progress bar while preparing the shard's art and maps. Updates now prepare these files before starting the game.
- A slow art rebuild can continue in the background while the game starts with the previous art. The completed art is picked up on a later start. Some startup checks can still cause delays.
- "Play Shattered Legacy" now connects to the test shard by default.

## Lumberjacking

- Stumps left by exhausted trees now sit at the base of the trunk instead of floating in the leaves.
- With the updated shard client, exhausted trees are hidden until their wood grows back. Some trees may still leave floating leaves.
- Using an equipped lumberjacking polearm on an exhausted stump now says there is not enough wood, instead of asking you to equip an axe.

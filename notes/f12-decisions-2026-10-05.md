Source: `notes\cc-P54-f12-xp-and-gain-paths.md`, "Decisions for Chase" (numbers below are P54's).

## Gain paths to 200

- **7. Worn bonuses:** yes. Gain tests use `Cap + (Value - Base)` (G and the five cap uses), so gear never stops gains early.
- **8. Hook G:** yes for Hiding, Stealth, Detect Hidden, Snooping, Poisoning, Healing, Veterinary, Begging, Forensics,
  Animal Lore (keyed on MinTameSkill), Musicianship, Tracking, Taste ID, Camping, Resisting Spells, Bushido.
  - **Detect Hidden:** gains only when something is found, and **also from treasure-hunting chest traps and other
    hidden traps** (Chase's addition), so it has a solo path.
- **8b / 13. Casting schools above about 120: NOT hook G.** Chase: a **Training Ward or a special dungeon quest line**
  (content gate). Design with the dungeon / League work. The Ward or quest only matters above stock's ceiling (about
  120), so OSI-range training is unchanged.
- **9. Clamps first:** yes. Fix Stealing's caught test now (trained skill, so gear cannot make a thief uncatchable);
  Hiding and guard-blindness clamps ship before any Hiding gain path.
- **10. Carpentry, Fletching, Tinkering:** yes, P61's rule (gain ceiling = next material's requirement, item window slid
  up, success stock).
- **11. Content skills** (Alchemy, Cooking, Tailoring, Fishing, Inscription): yes. Design top recipes, fish veins and
  orders with F-18 / F-20; ship the potion and fishing-rare caps first.
- **12. Imbuing:** **rework imbuing** (new feature; features chat to add it). Path past 140 could be a quest line or
  use in commissions. Until then Imbuing stays at 140.

## Levels and XP

- **1. Curve:** S2, a config table: P20's power curve to 100, flat XP per level above 100, never falling.
- **2. Pacing:** start with the recommended default: loop 0 about 3 h steady (8 h cautious), capstone about 334 h.
- **3. Echo top for levels:** keep x10.
- **4. Reserve credit:** raw XP from level 1 of the next loop, not multiplied by the echo.
- **5. Reserve after the capstone:** 0; post-capstone loops pay cosmetics only.
- **6. Full reserve:** XP lost, never refused; one message when full, reminder at most every 30 min, "Reserve: full" on
  the stat display.
- **15. XP for skill gains:** 2 x level XP per skill point, only for points above that skill's previous peak.
- **16. XP sources:** XP for the act (kill, craft, harvest, skill point, discovery), never for a currency turn-in or an achievement; kills credit everyone on the damage list.

## Echo and rate

- **14. Keeper echo:** keep the decided shape; add a per-use cap on echoed gain (default 2.0 points per use); echo multiplies the gain amount, never the chance.
- **17. Live gain rate:** **start live at plain stock speed (x1) and tune up in playtesting.** Test keeps its fast settings.

## What a path must actually do

Pinned `U/Skills/SkillCheck.cs:55-75,177-193` returns without a gain roll below the minimum or at/above the maximum. Direct checks similarly stop rolling at chance >= 1 (`:90-106,209-225`). Raising a stored cap or the gain rate does not reopen either case. Pinned `Gain` still enforces skill lock, Base < Cap and total-cap policy (`:243-285`); use it rather than assigning Base.

Our active Meditation calls `CheckSkill(Meditation, 0, skill.Cap)` at `C/Meditation.cs:116`, after valid mana/holding/armor/start checks. Its lesson is **a valid use plus an ordinary gain roll with headroom**, not replacing the effect's success formula everywhere. `C/ClusterFSkillGain.cs:210-216` compensates positive worn bonuses when a call's maximum equals the cap. Hook G (`:313-465`) adds a gain-only roll after the stock outcome, usually on `(row minimum, Cap + Value - Base)`, with content-tier exceptions for Lore, Arms Lore and Appraisal. `pastStockCeiling` defaults true (`:49,97`), but actual deployed settings were not read. Rate defaults are now two attempts and 1x amount, not P20's three and 5x.

For new paths, the acceptance boundary is **Base 199.9 -> 200**, including worn bonuses, then no gain at Base == Cap. Also verify representative lower loop caps and continuity from the old ceiling. Preserve successful use, resource costs, cooldowns, anti-macro keys, and one SkillUsed event per use. A window ending exactly at 200 can strand Base below 200 if it is checked against Value without bonus compensation.

Content ladders may have ceilings above 200: that is valid because Gain enforces the character's actual cap. Existing tier paths need not become equally trainable on every easy target. “To the cap” below means continuous earned access to 200; a fixed content ladder is not a promise of support for a future cap increase.

Size estimates are remaining implementation plus tests/build, not elapsed measurements: **0** = retain existing path, **S** = about one builder day, **M** = several builder days, **L** = a week or more/content integration. They exclude deployment and live/client verification. Multiple rows sharing a helper are not additive estimates.

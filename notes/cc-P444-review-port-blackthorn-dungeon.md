# P444: Blackthorn Dungeon port review

Reviewed October 22, 2026. Notes only. Finding paths are relative to the code work copy. Read both AGENTS.md files, the direction decision, task list, P196 notes, and the adjacent Codex review before reviewing canonical code and tests. The earlier review's findings are excluded below.

## Findings

1. **bug** server/customizations/Gumps/BaseRewardGump.cs:186: A player can open two Blackthorn purchase confirmations while holding enough points for one, then confirm both; each delivers its reward, and the second failed deduction is ignored by BlackthornRewardGump.RemovePoints (Services/Revamped Dungeons/BlackthornDungeon/Gumps.cs:21). Recheck and reserve points in the confirmed purchase path, refund on failed delivery, and test two pending 10-point confirmations against a 10-point balance.

2. **bug** server/customizations/Services/Revamped Dungeons/BlackthornDungeon/BlackthornSpawns.cs:144: XmlSpawner treats Width and Height as inclusive coordinate offsets, but the port uses them as exclusive rectangle sizes, removing the east and south edges from every positive-sized dungeon spawn rectangle. Use Width + 1 and Height + 1, including one tile for zero-sized Agent rows, and test literal outer-edge coordinates from the XML instead of deriving expected bounds from the port's table.

3. **bug** server/customizations/Services/Revamped Dungeons/BlackthornDungeon/Items/MinaxArtifacts.cs:102: Both the kill wrapper and invasion wrapper (:106) feed adjusted loot chances into the roll where ServUO supplies raw luck, increasing budget-upgrade and extra-property chances; at 1,000 raw luck, the extra-property check changes from 10% to 46.41%. Preserve the source's raw-luck input to the budget/property-count roll, separate it from any adjusted luck needed by the runic-property adapter, and test nonzero luck through both wrappers.

4. **bug** server/customizations/Services/Revamped Dungeons/BlackthornDungeon/Generate.cs:55: The final Felucca entrance stands at (1517,1420,9), although ServUO places it at z 12 and the reference map's walking surface there is z 12, so walking across that surface misses the one-high teleporter. Restore the entrance's z 12 position and test movement over that literal coordinate; P196's claimed destination correction reverses the meaning of the source's constructor and MoveToWorld arguments.

5. **risk** server/customizations/Services/Revamped Dungeons/BlackthornDungeon/Items/DecorativeShardShields.cs:284: The facing-choice response checks only that the deed exists and the saved house reference is non-null, then places the shield at saved coordinates on the player's current map (:305), so a stale response after changing facets or transferring the deed can place it outside the validated house or consume another holder's deed. Recheck deed possession, the original house and map, ownership, walls, and fit at confirmation, and test changed-facet and transferred-deed responses.

6. **risk** server/tests/Services/Revamped Dungeons/BlackthornDungeon/BlackthornDungeonVerification.cs:168: The hitching post is checked for placement and deletion, but no test calls its stable or claim paths, leaving fees, follower limits, rejected pets, and the conversion to PlayerMobile's stabled set unprotected. Add stable/claim round trips covering payment, full follower slots, and a rejected loaded pack animal, including a save/load between stabling and claiming.

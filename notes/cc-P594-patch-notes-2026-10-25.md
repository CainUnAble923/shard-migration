# October 25 patch notes

Draft for the next package and server update. Covers changes merged on October 25 so far. Availability on the running shard is unverified.

## Society of Smiths

- The Masterwork Hammer of Hephaestus is the hammer's third tier, with 800 uses, +15 Blacksmithy while held, and familiarity up to 5,000 for each metal. Society Masters with 15,000 standing and 100 Blacksmithy can upgrade a Reinforced Hammer at the guildmaster. The upgrade carries over its familiarity and requires payment in Seals, gold, and ingots.
- Successful crafts with familiar metals return some ingots, reaching a quarter of the cost at full familiarity.
- Hold the Masterwork Hammer near an anvil and forge, choose Craft This Order from its menu, and target a smith bulk order in your backpack. It makes the missing pieces through ordinary crafting attempts, using your skill, materials, and hammer charges. Pieces that meet the deed's requirements go into it.
- Large orders first take matching completed small deeds from the top level of your backpack, then craft what remains. See the partial-order warning below before using this.
- The run stops when the order is full, supplies or charges run out, you move or take damage, or you press Stop. Removing the hammer or deed also stops it.
- Update through the launcher to see the Craft This Order menu label.

## Bulk order rewards

- Members of an order-giving guild can see its payout in the deed window and tooltip. Society members see standing and Seals or cash-out gold, according to their completed-order setting. Other guild members see their guildmaster's gold payment.
- Open the deed window for a fresh quote after a trade or guild change. Tooltips can retain the previous holder's reward text.

## High Seas fishing

- Once fish mongers have been placed, find them at the docks of Britain, Moonglow, Trinsic, Vesper, Jhelom, Skara Brae, Papua, and the Sea Market in Trammel and Felucca. Bring your own boat close and double-click a monger to request a fish delivery order. Its shipping crate goes in your hold; fill it and sail to the delivery port. Destroying the crate forfeits the order.
- Delivery rewards can include bait, magic fishhooks, lava lobster traps, fishing guide books, Fishing power scrolls, and the Oracle of the Sea.
- Apply fish bait to a pole, or crab and lobster bait to a trap. It doubles the chance of its matching catch; enhanced bait triples it. A catch spends one use.
- Magic hooks attach to fishing poles and wear with use. Lava hooks allow lava fishing, junk-proof hooks reduce footwear catches, and dredging hooks give SOS shipwreck finds a barnacle-covered appearance.
- Lava fishing can bring up lava fish, stone footwear, stone pavers, and cracked lava rocks. Keep a cracked rock in your backpack and split it with an axe for a geode, shard, or lava rock. Wearing stone footwear prevents movement.
- SOS hauls can now include fish heads, oars, unfinished furniture, a yellow polka dot bikini, or a rare ship's strongbox.
- The Oracle of the Sea can locate a spot for Charybdis. Summoning there requires 100 Fishing, the Oracle in your backpack, and a pole with special Charybdis bait. That bait is currently missing from obtainable delivery rewards, so this route remains blocked.
- Charybdis now drops two to five fish pies. Most pies give a five-minute skill, combat, regeneration, or damage-reduction benefit. Their new oven recipes use the Cooking band II book, but missing ingredient vendors currently prevent ordinary baking.

## Known limits

- Craft This Order can consume a whole completed small deed even when a large order needs only part of it. Move small deeds you want to keep into a bag before starting a partially filled large order.
- Hammer exhaustion and charge regeneration still have known bugs. An empty hammer can retain its skill bonus, and regeneration timing needs correction.
- Fish-order accounting and magic hook application still have known item-handling defects awaiting fixes. Higher delivery reward tiers are also unreachable, so later guide volumes and special Charybdis bait are not available through these orders.
- Mento Seasoning, Samuel's Secret Sauce, and Dark Truffle have no vendor source. Fairy Salmon Pie's casting-focus benefit and Lava Fish Pie's soul-charge benefit are inactive.
- Corgul's island access is unfinished. This update does not open his encounter to players.

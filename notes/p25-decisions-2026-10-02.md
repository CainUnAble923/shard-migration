# cc-P25: Chase's decisions (2026-10-02)

Recorded by CC from Chase's reply to the P25 notes (`notes\cc-P25-stats-above-125-research.md`). Notes only; the
overseer moves these into `docs\` (agents do not edit `docs\`).

1. **A reset does NOT reset stats.** Stats carry across loops. This settles P25 Part 4, "design facts" item 1:
   - No stat write-down is needed in the reset path.
   - Pinned already behaves this way. Lowering `StatCap` never reduces raw stats (`SkillCheck.cs:313-333`).
   - Knock-on for `notes\f12-levels-loops-caps.md`: its line that the total stat cap, scaled by current level,
     "is what makes a reset feel like a reset" no longer holds. After the first loop, a reset character keeps full
     stats; the level-scaled total only limits *new* gain within a loop. The overseer should restate that section.
2. **Carry weight increases with Str, with no cap.** `customizations\ClusterFBackpack.cs:33-42` stays as built
   (`100 + 3.5 x Str` for humans, `PlayerMobile.cs:237`):
   - 800 stones at Str 200 and 1850 at effective 500, against OSI's 550.
   - This is a deliberate deviation. It should be registered in `docs\fidelity-principle.md`, where it is not
     today (grep 2026-10-02).
   - It drops out of P25 Part 5's builder list (row 8).
3. **No ceilings.** Reason given: players will face world bosses and champion spawns solo or in small parties.
   Asked which ceilings, Chase chose all four:
   - **No soft caps inside formulas.** Damage, swing speed, thrown range and spell damage keep paying linearly.
   - **No per-stat table tied to max level.** F-12's 125/140/155/170/185/200 column is dropped. Raw stats gain to
     the one global `stats.statMax`. That is 500 in both configs today; I assume it stays 500, since no new number
     was given.
   - **No effective clamp.** My "personal ceiling + 25" recommendation (P25 Part 3) is declined, and so is today's
     500. The patch would return `base.Str` with no `Math.Min`. That leaves `Mobile.cs:1868`'s 65000 as the only
     bound.
   - **No total stat cap.** F-12's 325..550 total and the +50 veteran reward are dropped. Today's
     `totalCap = 1500` (3 x 500) already never binds, so every stat can be maxed together.
4. **Self-bandage: no floor.** `Bandage.cs:493` stays as pinned: 1.0 s at Dex 200, instant (8 ms) from effective
   Dex 220. No pinned patch.
5. **Pets follow players.** Controlled pets keep gaining to `stats.statMax`, as they do today. No pet ceiling in
   `ClusterFSkillGain`.

## What these decisions remove from the F-12 build

- The 0x1A stat-lock handler from `arch-f12-stat-lock-verdict-2026-10-01.md`. There is no personal ceiling to
  enforce now, so it is not needed.
- The per-character effective clamp change to `patches\PlayerMobile-individual-stat-cap.patch`. That becomes
  "remove the min", or set `clusterf.statCaps.individualCap` very high with no code change.
- The `StatCapScroll` retune and the veteran +50 total.
- The `ClusterFStatCaps.Apply` per-level total.
- The P25 tests for the clamp, the bandage, pets, the total cap and the carry cap.

**Still in scope:** the meditation fix in our `Meditation.cs:83`. It is not a ceiling; at high Int the skill gets
*worse*, and that will be felt much more with no clamp (356 mana needed to start at Int 500, Med 120).

## Facts the builder must know with no clamp (P25, cited)

1. **The talisman and quiver stat-bonus leak** (P25 section 2.6, `AOS.cs:598` vs `:620`, not yet runtime-verified)
   is now unbounded. Today the 500 clamp is its only backstop.
   - With no clamp, repeated equip and unequip stacks Str, Dex or Int without limit until a restart.
   - It is a defect, not a ceiling, so fixing it does not conflict with decision 3. **Recommend fixing it.**
   - The fix makes the add and remove names match. It is a pinned patch in `AOS.cs`, or our own wrapper if one
     can be found.
2. **Status packet fields are 16-bit signed** (`OutgoingMobilePackets.cs:523-542`).
   - Any stat, pool or weight above 32767 shows garbled in the client.
   - Honest play cannot reach it: raw 500 plus about +375 offset gives about 875, and MaxWeight about 3160.
   - Only the leak in fact 1, or a GM, can reach it. That is another reason to fix the leak.
3. **Thrown range passes the 18-tile update range** (`Server/Main.cs:150`) from effective Str of about 300
   (Soul Glaive). At that point a gargoyle thrower can hit targets that cannot see them on screen. This is noted
   as a consequence, not reopened.
4. **Monsters and bosses were balanced against OSI player numbers** (150 effective, 150 HP). F-14's own-creature
   level field is the place to scale our bosses to these players; stock bosses will be much easier.

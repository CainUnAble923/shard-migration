# P608: guards that patrol and see

Design proposal, October 25, 2026. Design only. No code changes.

Recommend option C: placed guards, authored patrol routes, witnessed crimes, and physical pursuit. Build it in nine focused Golem prompts, with room for two more if map coverage or performance needs work. Start with a test-town pilot, then qualify New Haven separately before changing its enforcement. Towns remain guarded regions. A guard must see a crime or hear a report from a witness, then run to help. No guard appears at the criminal, teleports around a wall, or teleports home.

All new numbers below are proposed starting settings, not measured performance or approved balance. This note records review choices for later builder prompts. It authorizes no deployment. This is an intentional shard-specific departure from stock guards under Chase's P608 direction. The overseer should promote the approved behavior into the fidelity register before implementation ships; this task does not edit `docs/`.

## Law stays separate from enforcement

Keep existing guarded regions, criminal flags and timers, murder reporting, lawful aggression, town spell restrictions, and vendor-access rules. P608 changes how guards learn about an incident and get there. It does not turn a blind alley into a legal theft area, make a red character an automatic target under AOS, or declare every monster in Old Haven a criminal.

A valid crime can be unseen. Its ordinary criminal consequences still happen, but it creates no guard knowledge without a qualified observer. Guards do not acquire suspects by reading every nearby Criminal flag. Once told about an eligible incident, they may recognize that identified suspect when they actually see them again during the incident's lifetime. Guard knowledge is distinct from the legal flag and never grants permission to attack after the ordinary law forbids it.

Start with existing guard-eligible aggression, criminal action, and criminal assistance. Preserve Stealing's caught and criminal decisions. An uncaught theft does not become caught because P608 sampled a mobile nearby. When theft is caught, build witness evidence from the actual theft event and visible actor, not the stock eight-tile text broadcast. No new law for snooping, trespass, Den membership, low karma, or red names is implied.

Attribute pet and summon offenses to both the acting creature and responsible master through existing ownership and law rules. Seeing a pet does not reveal an unseen master's current location. Guards first stop the visible active threat. Player damage to someone inside a protected hub must be rejected through safety rules even if its source is outside the region; geography must not create a ranged-attack loophole.

## Posts, staffing, and route authoring

Each town/facet pair gets a versioned manifest: stable town and jurisdiction IDs, exact existing region references, posts, guard slots and roles, route IDs, waypoint coordinates including Z, waits, coverage priority, and allowed pursuit apron. Separate facets never share guards. Unguarded settlements remain unguarded. Do not populate a map merely because the expansion enables it.

Proposed staffing budgets follow. These are design allocations, not counts of existing towns or guards. Size depends on reachable street area and entrances, not region rectangle area alone.

| Town class | Post guards | Patrol guards | Total slots | Starting coverage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Small settlement | 2 | 2 | 4 | Main entrance and bank/square; one short loop with two staggered patrols. |
| Medium town | 4 | 4 | 8 | Main gates, bank, and square; two loops, two guards each. |
| Large town | 6 | 6 | 12 | Major entrances and service centers; three loops, two guards each. |
| Capital or spread-out city | 8 | 8 | 16 | Multiple banks/gates and districts; four loops, two guards each. |
| New Haven exception | 8 | 4 | 12 | Fixed service coverage plus short loops linking the bank, healer, trainers, town exits, and separate moongate region. |

Britain is a candidate for the capital budget, Minoc for medium, and a compact settlement such as Cove for small. These are provisional classifications pending actual route surveys. Do not copy one facet's itinerary blindly to another or claim the table proves coverage. Extra gates or a disconnected bank may require another post or a smaller enabled area before release.

In New Haven allocate post slots as pairs: bank/square, healer/trainer hub, eastern town exit, and moongate. Give two patrols each to two short service loops. Survey the actual doors and floors before choosing tiles. Use the existing BankAnchor and RegistrarAnchor as references, not as spawn tiles on top of service NPCs. Do not send these patrols through Old Haven's undead grounds or into Rootdeep.

The future seeder offers read-only status/dry run, create-missing, and explicit repair modes. Every slot has an ownership tag and persistent stable ID. Rerunning reconciles that slot rather than counting nearby mobiles or duplicating patrols. Adopt or retire legacy guard spawners only after an explicit inventory; never delete every WarriorGuard or ArcherGuard in a rectangle. Leave hand-placed, quest, faction, and mixed spawners alone and report conflicts. Preserve a guard's position while responding; apply route edits when it returns idle. Never seed on every call.

Route authors use staff-only waypoint placement and a connected sequence, then export the reviewed coordinates into the manifest. Existing WayPoint/NextPoint tooling can supply the editing workflow, with owned immovable markers or equivalent route data in the implementation. Put turns at corners, gates, door approaches, ramps, stairs, bridges, and floor changes, generally 8 to 12 tiles apart. Use separate one-way links where needed. Test every link in both its intended direction and return route. A loop can pause two to five seconds at a square; stagger departures so patrols do not overlap constantly. Loops should take roughly 45 to 90 seconds in ordinary walking conditions, measured later.

Validate legal map, exact Z, guard-zone membership, reachable ground, and jurisdiction at each point and along every link. Reject routes through private houses, locked doors, dungeon entrances, player travel gates, or unsafe floors. A guard may open an ordinary usable public door under normal movement rules. It cannot phase through walls or use a magical shortcut. Route data is authored and reviewed content, never a live all-town path search.

Killed guards are not a source of equipment, gold, skill practice, fame, karma, or quest progress. A missing slot can replace its guard at its own post after a proposed 60-second delay, at most one replacement per town per ten seconds. Replacement is never at an incident or on the suspect's tile. An obstructed or actively contested post waits and reports the gap. Never create unlimited avenge guards. Service-hub safety must still hold when every guard is dead or occupied.

## Seeing a crime and hearing a call

Use a shared witness service at the crime event, before callbacks can move, hide, or delete the actor. A record holds incident ID, kind, legal eligibility, town/facet, event location/time, culprit identity, victim, qualified witnesses, and their last observed culprit location. Server records supply these facts. Client text, a target serial supplied by a caller, a theft notice packet, or a current Criminal flag supplies no witness authority.

A guard or eligible civilian sees an actor only when alive, on the same map, within 14 tiles in UO tile range, able to CanSee the actor, and in actual map LOS. Check the relevant action location too where it differs from the actor. Z and walls matter. No facing cone in the first release; it would add uncertainty without a necessary stealth rule. Hidden or stealthed actors do not qualify until actually revealed. Staff access never becomes guard perception.

Direct guard observation immediately creates a response assignment. Civilian witnesses may shout once per incident using the same report path as players. Preserve recognizable guard-call speech and supply a simple report choice if several witnessed incidents are available. A living player can say or yell "guards" only about an incident they saw within the last 15 seconds. Default to the most recent eligible incident; never sweep every criminal near the caller. Being the victim alone is not proof that the culprit was visible. A victim who could not see the attacker may call for an investigation at the spot, but that gives no identity, remote target, or authority to strike a hidden player.

The report is heard only by actual guards within speech range: 15 normal, 18 yelled, one whispered. Use one delivery path for each speech event so region and NPC callbacks cannot dispatch twice. A guard can hear around a corner; hearing does not require visual LOS. Start with range and same jurisdiction/map, not a new acoustic wall simulation. Hearing a witness transfers their incident and last known location, not a continuous feed of the suspect's coordinates. A hidden witness may report an actor they actually saw; speech follows existing revealing behavior and never locates an unseen suspect automatically.

If nobody hears, say "No guard answers nearby." Do not spawn help or silently claim that guards are coming. Let the witness run to a post and call while the report is still fresh. For beginners, show where posts are and explain: "Guards respond to what they see and to nearby witnesses. The ruins are a training area outside town."

A dispatched guard can shout for at most two available nearby guards within 18 tiles. Relay the same incident ID with at most two relay hops and a five-second relay lifetime, once per guard. No town-wide alarm that knows everyone's location. Fixed hub sentries keep at least one guard at each protected post pair; patrols and the partner answer first. A severe attack can preempt a routine challenge or low-priority search, but must not empty every safe hub.

## Physical response, combat, and losing the trail

Use explicit states: Post/Patrol, Respond, Challenge, Fight, Search, Return, and Unavailable. A routine challenge can be interrupted immediately by a real offense. Assignment deduplicates incident IDs, picks an available guard with a plausible local route, and never constructs a guard as a fallback. If the closest guard cannot reach the site, try an eligible alternate under the same limits.

Patrol at about one tile per 0.4 seconds; response and pursuit run at about one tile per 0.2 seconds, with normal collision and turn costs. These are intended movement cadences, not merely the Running animation bit or an assumption about ActiveSpeed. Use the existing movement budget and point/path primitives; verify actual tiles per second in the eventual test environment. There is no catch-up burst after a delayed tick. Mounted suspects may escape on open ground. Posts, routes, and witnesses supply coverage rather than superhuman movement.

Run to the reported event or last observed spot. Pursue the mobile's current location only while the guard can actually see it. On lost LOS or Hidden, clear the live mobile movement goal immediately and copy the last seen point, including Z. This avoids PathFollower following a hidden mobile reference through walls. A report about an already hidden culprit prompts a search, not damage against that serial. Attacks always recheck visible target, legal harmful permission, distance, and LOS. Archers cannot shoot through geometry.

Search for up to ten seconds around that point, with at most two attempts to reveal within three tiles, no more than one every five seconds. Apply a contested Detect Hidden check against Hiding under the existing skill rules, bounded by range and LOS to each candidate. No passive reveal aura, map-wide scan, or targeting a hidden mobile to discover its location. Standard reveal messages apply on success. Keep Hiding's existing combat restrictions and Stealth movement limits; breaking pursuit does not give an automatic successful hide.

For an identified, legally eligible offender who is visible again, resume pursuit. A minor theft can escape through an unwatched alley if no witness or guard reacquires it. Knowledge does not remove the criminal flag or extend it. Initial reports expire after 15 seconds if no guard was assigned. An active assignment lasts at most 60 seconds from acceptance; losing the trail, expiry, suspect death/deletion, map change, or loss of lawful attack permission ends it. Duplicate calls cannot refresh that maximum. A genuinely new offense creates a new incident. Retain a short record for diagnostics only, with no later wanted status.

At a town boundary, guards may continue an already visible pursuit into an authored exterior apron, at most 20 tiles from that jurisdiction, for at most ten seconds after exit and within the overall pursuit limit. No fresh searches for unrelated criminals outside town. End pursuit at an unapproved region, dungeon, private interior, facet transition, or travel gate. Guards do not follow recall or teleport with a matching relocation. Crossing a seam inside the same authored jurisdiction does not evade the whole town, while crossing into a different town requires another local witness/report. Existing legal travel restrictions still apply to criminals.

For the first release, guards fight active legally attackable offenders using ordinary melee or ranged combat. There is no instant-kill timer. Start testing at roughly 1,200 to 1,600 hits, 65 to 70 percent resistances, 120 combat and resistance skills, and 25 to 35 base melee damage, with two respondents against an active attacker when available. These numbers do not prove strength against this shard's skill caps, gear, pets, or group tactics. Qualify against those builds. Strong is appropriate; unbeatable and omniscient are not requirements. Preserve normal LOS, targeting, and combat permissions, and exclude guards from taming, calming, luring orders, farming, and loot awards through explicit policies.

Do not introduce forced arrest, jail, confiscation, fines, or forced movement in the first release. A minor offender may stop resisting and choose a later authored surrender interaction, but that is a separate feature requiring rules for custody, escape, reconnects, stolen property, payment, and appeals. Current code's NonLethalAttack is not that feature. For now, stopping combat follows existing law and aggression, not a new gold payment that erases an offense.

After resolution the same guard walks to its home post or a reachable route entry. It does not disappear after forty seconds. If blocked, retry within the movement budget, try a reviewed alternate, then mark unavailable and report it. Never teleport a stuck respondent or snap it home during play. Any later staff repair is explicit, separate from crime response, and cannot duplicate the slot.

## New Haven, Young players, and Old Haven

Arrival time is not a sufficient beginner safety mechanism. Retain Trammel harmful restrictions, Young theft protection, and ordinary creature Young protection even when no guard saw the offense. P189's training and Rootdeep dungeon behavior remain separate from civic enforcement.

Before enabling walking guards in New Haven, qualify a protected service area covering the bank, healer, trainer hubs, town entrances, and moongate. Propose explicit prevention of player-origin harm to Young players there, including pets, summons, guild exceptions, fields, traps, delayed damage, and attacks crossing the boundary. Validate attribution on direct-damage paths rather than assuming CanBeHarmful covers them. This closes a real safety-policy gap and is a proposed additional rule, not a claim that P189 already implemented it. A legitimate training fight with stock undead outside town remains possible, including the usual loss of protection against that creature after attacking it.

Keep guards out of Old Haven training hunts and Rootdeep. Do not extend the New Haven guard zone across the ruins as a shortcut to safety, auto-kill the apprentice quest targets, or globally change dungeon Young rules. Teach the boundary when leaving town, and keep the return route readable. Verify P189 retirement actually happened on any future deployment: its source bounds still end at x 3680/y 2600, while the training extension reaches farther south/east. P447 records this gap and direct-trap/guild risks. Their fixes or explicit safety decisions must be resolved before the New Haven release; this design changes none of that code.

For protected hub tiles, require a reachable fixed guard and a measured 95th-percentile arrival within three seconds from hearing/seeing the incident. For ordinary covered streets, start with eight seconds. These are test targets, not promises about all town tiles. Maps and spacing must meet them without faster-than-declared movement. If a hub fails, fix the route/post layout or leave its existing mode until the complete candidate is ready. Do not let an experimental patrol toggle remove the only protection. Retain an obvious warning when Young status ends without renewing its clock or granting indefinite safety outside town.

## Persuasion, stealth, the Den, and bounties

Use [P606's guard contract](cc-P606-begging-skill-design.md): at 80 learned Base in the existing Begging/Persuasion slot, an eligible innocent traveler may answer an authored routine checkpoint challenge. Stand visible within three tiles and LOS, speak for two interruptible seconds, and resolve through P606's bounded effective-skill outcome calculation. Starting difficulty 80 is a tuning proposal. Success acknowledges passage at that checkpoint for five minutes. Failure keeps the ordinary authored challenge; it never itself creates a crime.

Allow one attempt per account/checkpoint/challenge opportunity, with P606's thirty-minute checkpoint/account training limit and at most one eligible practice event at terminal resolution. Ordinary patrol greetings, repeated failed clicks, manufactured crimes, and respawned guards teach nothing. Use stable checkpoint IDs across restarts and replacements. If P606 is delayed, patrols work without this adapter. Count the shared adapter once in implementation planning, not once for each task.

Talking a guard down means resolving a noncriminal misunderstanding or routine questioning. It cannot dismiss a witnessed theft, change Criminal or murder counts, interrupt an arrest order, postpone harm response, conceal a known fugitive, or bypass quest/faction restrictions. Recheck eligibility when speaking finishes. P606 explicitly recommends these boundaries; a later bribe or clemency feature would need its own decision. Ordinary travel to banks and healers must not acquire mandatory social-skill gates just to justify checkpoints. Failure leaves the same lawful route available unless separate existing content already restricts passage.

Hiding and Stealth supply real escape opportunities through unobserved routes and lost LOS. Guards do not detect guilt from a skill value, name, low karma, or guild membership. Existing reveals from theft and offensive actions still happen. A Den member gets no legal exemption and no automatic suspicion. The Den can offer jobs about observing patrol timings, scouting an unwatched route, or returning safely, using the same witness rules. Keep its stationary lookout and guildmaster useful, and do not camp their doors with special psychic guards. Practice targets remain in authored consent-based content; do not make harming beginners the best Den progression.

Add no bounty system in P608. The source already has optional murder bounties, but live enablement was not checked. If enabled later, preserve or adapt head turn-in at an appropriate post/contact while retaining existing ownership, expiry, and payout checks. Ordinary unseen theft creates neither a bounty nor a permanent town wanted ledger. A bounty is not a location beacon and does not waive LOS or attack permission. Broader town standing, disguises, bribery, and jail are separate work.

## Performance and failure limits

Use one town dispatcher and spatial queries near each incident or active guard, never a world-wide mobile scan. Capture sight evidence synchronously at the crime, then queue movement work. Stagger idle sensing to about once per second; while responding, visibility refreshes at most every 0.25 seconds and immediately before an attack. Budget event-time witness checks so a snapshot contains actual qualified witnesses rather than a list rechecked after the suspect escaped. If an event cannot be processed within its bound, retain only proven evidence and prioritize protecting the victim; do not invent witnesses to fill the gap.

Proposed initial caps: three assigned guards per incident, eight responding/searching guards per town, 32 across enabled towns, 64 queued incidents per town, and 128 across the pilot rollout. Deduplicate repeated reports; rate-limit callers to one report per two seconds. New harm outranks routine questioning and property searches, with a reserved severe-harm queue so call spam cannot displace attacks on beginners. These are tuning limits, not a claim that a queue can guarantee safety under unlimited load. Safety prevention continues independently.

Retain a persistent path per guard. Start with no more than two fresh path plans per town per second and 20 globally, plus a two-millisecond planning budget per dispatcher slice. Actual path creation, including PathFollower's blocked-step repaths, must consume the same budget. Limiting high-level AI calls alone is insufficient. A single synchronous path plan cannot be preempted by an outer stopwatch; measure worst-case searches and add bounded search work or defer the caller if needed. Reuse known route segments, spread replans across ticks, and avoid recalculating paths to an unchanged last seen point. Patrol planning yields first under load; active attacks and fixed post sensing remain prioritized.

After three failed attempts or five seconds without meaningful progress, stop replanning that blocked segment, retain the post gap/incident status, try one available alternate responder, and report the obstruction. Do not let players create endless route work by dropping objects or closing doors. Validate multi-level buildings, narrow doorways, bridges, dynamic blockers, houses, and field spells. Collision and safety-region rules stay in force; no magical rescue fallback.

Record incident-to-assignment and arrival times, witness source, lost-trail reasons, active counts, queue drops, path plans/failures/time, unavailable slots, and response coverage by post. Bound diagnostic retention and redact unnecessary player data. Benchmark an idle town, a crowded bank, simultaneous visible crimes, call spam, hidden suspects behind walls, and blocked routes in throwaway loopback-only test environments. Runtime budgets and player-capacity assumptions remain unverified until those measurements exist.

## Risks and mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation and release condition |
| --- | --- |
| Town feels unsafe or guard presence is too sparse | Survey bank/healer/gate coverage, reserve sentries, publish the difference between guarded streets and unwatched alleys, and verify arrival targets with ordinary novice movement. Keep Young harm prevention independent of the dispatcher. |
| Attacker kills a beginner before arrival | Reject prohibited harm at its source, including indirect paths and guild exceptions in the proposed Young hubs. Guard damage buffs do not solve the first hit. Resolve P447 safety risks before New Haven changes. |
| Players kite all guards away or kill the force | Keep a sentry per protected post pair, limit pursuit apron/duration and response slots, replace only at posts, and give no guard-farming rewards. Test a coordinated group with pets against both coverage and safety. |
| Guard sees through a wall or follows a hidden serial | Capture event-time visibility, require CanSee plus LOS plus range, replace live movement goals with last seen points, and LOS-filter reveals. Wall, floor, hide-after-report, and recall cases are release checks. |
| False reports target innocent players | Reports reference only server-authored witnessed incidents; no arbitrary accusation target. Revalidate law before every harmful act. Social failure or low karma never supplies evidence. |
| Guards stick on geometry or eat the tick budget | Author and walk routes, budget actual path creation, stop stalled segments, record failures, and assign a bounded alternate. No teleport fallback. |
| Guards clear Old Haven quests or alter other guard content | Scope dispatcher changes to opted-in town jurisdictions. Keep apprenticeship, dungeon, faction, quest, and hand-placed guards on their reviewed rules. Explicitly inventory legacy spawners before a town changes mode. |
| Reboot duplicates guards or grants another social roll | Stable owned slots/checkpoints and serialized durable social resolution keys. Discard transient pursuits on restart and resume a validated home/route state, without restoring an invisible target tracker. |
| Guard type changes break shard integrations | Audit all BaseGuard type checks, death credit, bounty turn-in, seeder ownership, and guard candidate exclusions. Preserve hidden metadata and existing lawful targeting through an adapter. |

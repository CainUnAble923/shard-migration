## In plain words

- **Wren's farmhouse is one whole house now (test shard).** It is OSI's own thatched cottage. The old one was the same
  cottage, but its pieces were left at the heights of a hillside in New Haven, so on the isle's flat meadow nothing
  lined up. The roof, walls, steps and door now match. The chimney is the hearth inside, the gray shed on the roof is
  gone, and the house has a bed, a table and chairs and its own sign. The farm field has one tidy fence all round, with
  the animal pen closed inside it and Wren's gate the only way in.
- **Each lesson has a proper signpost**, named "Lesson 5: The farm: ..." and so on, beside its stop instead of the blue
  rune on the ground. Double-clicking the sign starts the lesson, as the rune did.
- **The bank and the League lodge look like real buildings**: pitched roofs, windows, a bank sign, League banners, and
  furnished insides (a counter with Merrin behind it and vault chests; Elara's desk, bookcases, and a notice board you
  double-click to read about the League). Their doors fold back against the wall instead of swinging out. The bank
  lesson's arrow points at the bank door, then at Merrin once you are inside.
- **The dock to the moongate can be walked now.** You were right: the first planks were a step too high from the beach.
  A real character in TazUO got stuck there, and after the fix walked to the moongate and back. **For you:** walk the
  isle once more on the next test build (the farm, a sign or two, the bank, the lodge, the dock). Pictures:
  `notes\cc-P720-tools\shots\` (before-, after- and upgrade- pairs).

## Decisions and deviations

- **The farmhouse is OSI's cottage multi, not a hand repair of P663's pieces.** It is what P276 meant to build, and
  the multi is OSI's proven layout. New Haven's own farm (`TutorialFarmLayout`, the wing trials) is untouched.
- **Signs beside the stops, not on them.** The standing post is impassable. Putting it on the stop would block the road
  and the NPCs that share the stop, so the sign is the marker, one tile or two away.
- **The bank's door opens into the wall line (WestCCW).** I compared three ways by render: WestCW swings outside,
  which is what Chase disliked; EastCW swings in onto the carpet; WestCCW folds back in the wall line, which reads as
  hung. The lodge's door is the same.
- **P662's driver was changed in place** (`notes\cc-P662-tools\build_isle.py`, `isle-sets.json`, `checklist_isle.py`),
  because it is the isle's one source and `test_mapops.py` fact 7 reads its outputs. Its records were regenerated:
  `statics-placed.csv`, `land-summary.csv`, `plan-cells.json` (8,171 walkable tiles; the old file no longer describes
  the isle), `walk-report.json` and `build-report.json`. Every walk leg is unchanged except stop 7 to Merrin (5 to 10,
  round the counter), stop 8 to Elara (5 to 8, round the desk) and the yard's stop 6 to the echo (3 to 4, past the sign).
- **The Read tool was denied** (unattended run) for `TutorialFarm.cs`, `TutorialCorePlacement.cs` and one shot JPEG
  in `p720-proof`. I wanted to read them to edit them and to look at the shot. The edits went through scripts
  (`edit_farm.py`, `edit_farm2.py`, `edit_placement.py`, `edit_tests.py`), and the shots were copied to the scratchpad
  and read there. Nothing was skipped.
- **CHANGELOG** `2026.10.28.4` (main is at `.3`): six Changed lines and one Fixed. P721's unmerged branch also adds a
  section after `.3`; whichever merges second renumbers its section.

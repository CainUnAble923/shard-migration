# P672: quest seeds for Siege of Sosaria's named places

October 26, 2026. Draft for Chase to edit. Notes only.

Place names and map locations are credited to **Zigholtul**, creator of Siege of Sosaria Trammel Map 3.0. The [author-spots reference](sos-map-author-spots.md) supplies names and coordinates, not inhabitants or stories. The quest titles, new characters, occupants, and events below are Shattered Legacy proposals. They do not attribute a backstory to Zigholtul or borrow another shard's story.

There are 32 seeds below: the 16 requested landmarks, followed by 16 selected custom dungeons. This is a first selection, not a seed for every dungeon in the map reference. Coordinates refer to the author's map. A listed entrance or landmark is a planning anchor, not a verified spawn tile. Red Dagger Keep has only a region bounding box here. None of these notes decide P669's map choice.

The same hooks work if this becomes the main land or a separate expedition facet. On the main land, use local notice boards and League posts. As an expedition, start the same jobs from the existing League office and return reports there. Keep New Haven's tutorial and graduation separate from this draft.

## Reading the seeds

**Level** means a proposed current player level for encounter tuning, not a skill requirement or a verified creature rating. F-12's intended maximum is 50 at creation, 100 after graduation's first loop, then +5 per later loop to 200 at loop 21. The older six-step ladder in the skills design is superseded. A level band is a recommendation, not a new gate approved by this note. Content above 160 would use our own designed creatures.

**Systems** names the design the hook would use. All quests, new jobs, facet permissions, companion scenes, and rewards here are proposals. Full player XP and resets, humanoid companion hiring, and parts of high-skill training still need implementation. Bringing an existing companion or evo pet can add flavor, but no main route requires owning one. Skill activities should train through meaningful use, with difficulty matched to the intended band. Quest turn-ins do not grant free skill points.

**Facet** says whether a seed could support travel to Ilshenar, Malas, Tokuno, or Ter Mur. These would be permanent character travel permissions earned by a quest chain, retained through resets. A clue is not permission. A shared door or another player's completion does not grant personal credit. Exact travel rules remain for a build spec.

League side jobs do not replace promotion requirements or recover extra sword pieces. Preserve P593's Tamsin rescue, P601's feeding reveal, P616's Unbound and empty-world vision, and P621's permanent personal Mend or Sunder choice. Do not reveal those answers in an early side quest. Neither Keeper nor Breaker is required for a facet route. The Setting and the Unbroken epilogue remain reserved.

## Towns, forts, ruins, and islands

### 01. Valunstead: The Second Delivery

**Anchor:** bank, 484, 434, 15. **Level:** 10-25. **Systems:** League, Merchants' Exchange, loops. **Facet:** No.

League relief clerk Ada Fenwick lives above a storehouse whose households have signed for the same winter supplies twice. Find the missing cart, compare its delivery slips with the town register, and bring medicine to the families still waiting. Early dialogue treats this as a practical discrepancy; only characters past Chapter 1 get an optional exchange about remembered returns. Reward a useful League lead and the families' names in the player's journal.

### 02. Fort Whitestone: A Place at the Table

**Anchor:** 890, 1055, 32. **Level:** 20-40. **Systems:** League, Healers' Covenant, companions. **Facet:** No.

Quartermaster Hester Vale houses injured scouts and insists on laying a place for a patrol that never returned. Follow their discarded medical bundles, recover the patrol roll from a raider camp, and bring the surviving scout home. A traveling companion may read the names beside the player, but Hester supplies the same scene for a solo visitor. The fort becomes a recurring relief post rather than another conquered ruin.

### 03. Khalahandra Keep: The Unpaid Watch

**Anchor:** entrance, 2505, 1707, 50. **Level:** 70-95. **Systems:** League, Warriors' Brotherhood, loops. **Facet:** No.

Captain Della Marr's garrison has kept its oath while a false paymaster sells its relief supplies. Recover his account book and escort a real supply party through the keep's approaches. Characters returning in a later loop find Della's copy of their earlier report, an optional acknowledgment of lasting civic work. Completing the job records standing; it neither resets the character nor pays repeated first-completion rewards.

### 04. Fort Viridian: The Boundary Trees

**Anchor:** 1015, 1003, 0. **Level:** 25-45. **Systems:** Foresters' Union, Rangers' League, discovery ledger. **Facet:** No.

Forester Nessa Holt and her survey crew find guild boundary marks cut onto healthy trees outside the allotted grove. Track the cutters, identify which marks are forged, and replace only the damaged trail signs. The reward is a discovery entry and a sustainable work site, not permission to clear the forest. Logging and Tracking provide optional evidence routes, with a survey walk available to other builds.

### 05. Fort Stormhammer: A Shield Worth Keeping

**Anchor:** 1490, 1323, 2. **Level:** 40-60. **Systems:** Society of Smiths, Arms Lore, levelable gear. **Facet:** No.

Armorer Tobin Rell keeps repairing a recruit's shield because its maker never returned to collect payment. Recover the maker's pattern roll from looters, inspect the damaged fittings, and help equip the next patrol. A player can take an already sealed crafted shield on that patrol and earn ordinary combat item XP. The story values a maintained tool; it offers no automatic item levels or new Smith's Seal source.

### 06. Castle Dunbain: The Open Ledger

**Anchor:** 1333, 1377, 0. **Level:** 45-65. **Systems:** League, Thieves' Den, Merchants' Exchange. **Facet:** No.

Steward Corin Pell and hired guards occupy a castle whose relief accounts name villages that received nothing. Clerk Iris Dane asks for proof before the next convoy leaves. Enter the counting room through a forged work order or a guarded service route, recover the real ledger, and redirect the supplies. Lockpicking and Appraisal can expose extra evidence, while neither guild membership nor stealing from players is required.

### 07. Fort Silverblood: The Last Clean Bandage

**Anchor:** 1561, 2652, -5. **Level:** 55-75. **Systems:** Healers' Covenant, Order of the Alembic, League. **Facet:** No.

Healer Maeve Sutter tends soldiers poisoned by their own water barrels. Identify the tainted shipment, gather a safe reagent sample, and stop the seller before another patrol drinks it. Healing and Taste Identification offer practical investigation tasks; a field assistant covers missing specialties. The cure is prepared for this outbreak, giving the two guilds shared work without introducing a universal antidote or a new poison tier.

### 08. Red Dagger Keep: A Forged Passage

**Anchor:** region x 4406-4693, y 1944-2203; entrance unverified. **Level:** 75-95. **Systems:** Thieves' Den, Maritime Brotherhood, League. **Facet:** Malas route clue, not an unlock.

Smuggler Vera Flint's crew sells counterfeit League passage papers to families hoping to leave a dangerous coast. Recover the passenger list before a guard burns it, then get the stranded families to a real relief boat. The seized chart points to a memorial route beneath Ghuul Sanctuary. Its seal is a fraud, not a sixth sword fragment or an early explanation of Doom's dead.

### 09. Tigerclaw Fortress: The Borrowed Banner

**Anchor:** 2595, 1085, 8. **Level:** 100-120. **Systems:** Twin Paths Dojo, League, companions. **Facet:** Tokuno route clue.

Instructor Renna Ward has opened the fortress to displaced pupils, but its hired commander uses their banner to levy tolls. Recover the original charter, break his hold on the practice yard, and return the banner to the pupils. Bushido or Ninjitsu can provide optional training encounters; ordinary combat remains viable. A companion can practice beside the player without a forced branch choice. The charter identifies a lost Tokuno crossing survey in Whispering Hollow.

### 10. Dhampire Ruins: Names Beneath the Ash

**Anchor:** 1469, 2515, 14. **Level:** 60-80. **Systems:** Keepers of the Last Door, Forensics, League. **Facet:** Malas route clue.

Mortuary clerk Oren Pike shelters among ruined walls while grave robbers sell the inhabitants' nameplates. Match recovered plates to the burial register and stop the buyer from grinding them into false relics. Forensics and Spirit Speak reveal optional personal details; reading the register supplies the required evidence. The repaired list helps identify Ghuul Sanctuary's missing mourners. No sword piece lives here, and no side quest releases Mara's company before Chapter 3.

### 11. Camp Ironfang: Leave the Cages Open

**Anchor:** 1699, 2363, 5. **Level:** 30-50. **Systems:** Rangers' League, animal skills, companions and evo pets. **Facet:** No.

Trapper Garrick Venn's poachers hold pack animals and sell their tack as battlefield salvage. Stablehand June Mercer asks the player to mark a safe route, open the pens, and drive the captives to a recovery corral. Animal Lore, Veterinary, and Herding add care objectives. An evo pet may participate in the ordinary camp fight, but the rescue grants no evolution stage, replacement starter, or Bond Trial completion.

### 12. Ivanguard Manor: The Guest Who Was Never Paid

**Anchor:** 1773, 2683, 0. **Level:** 50-70. **Systems:** League, companions, Tailors' Circle. **Facet:** No.

Housekeeper Lysa Trent keeps the manor open while its owner withholds wages from a traveling guard. Recover the guard's stored equipment, settle the signed contract, and escort the household workers past hired enforcers. A player's companion may comment on fair terms and keeping a name after service. The rescued guard leaves under their own contract; the quest does not force a permanent hire or invent a free companion reward.

### 13. Mistveil Ruins: The Survey in the Fog

**Anchor:** 1177, 792, 0. **Level:** 35-55. **Systems:** League, Arcane Society, Cartography. **Facet:** Ilshenar route clue.

Surveyor Brin Alder is hiding from scavengers who have taken the brass markers from an old crossing survey. Find the markers in a readable sequence and rebuild the missing page of his chart. Cartography or magical inspection adds annotations, not free training on repeated clicks. The chart identifies a matching instrument in Azurite Caverns. It shows a possible road to Ilshenar without explaining the broken sword or opening travel by itself.

### 14. Glimmerwood: What the Grove Remembers

**Anchor:** west, 2123, 3330, 0; east, 2834, 3488, 6. **Level:** 120-140. **Systems:** Foresters' Union, Artificers' Order, persistent collections. **Facet:** Ter Mur route clue.

Grove keeper Mara Wren protects a stand of living trees from collectors stripping its survey tags. Recover the tags, record which trees survive, and compare their growth rings with a damaged stone rubbing. The grove ledger keeps that discovery through later loops. The rubbing describes a welcome marker for a Ter Mur crossing and directs the player to Fortress Calcifina. It is a travel clue, not the League crest's Setting.

### 15. Dragonstorm Isle: Before the Next Landing

**Anchor:** moongate, 3186, 3884, 17. **Level:** 110-135. **Systems:** Maritime Brotherhood, League, levelable gear. **Facet:** No; supports the Dragons Den expedition.

Harbor keeper Selda Rook and shipwreck survivors share the landing with drakes drawn to stolen eggs. Recover the brood tally from egg thieves and return the surviving eggs before escorting the next relief crew ashore. Already levelable crafted gear earns its normal combat XP during the fighting. Selda then identifies the Dragons Den entrance. The visible moongate is a map landmark, not proof of a working route to another facet.

### 16. Skypoint Islands: The Ship That Cannot Leave

**Anchor:** ship, 4357, 2349, 20. **Level:** 70-90. **Systems:** Maritime Brotherhood, Tinkers' Union, League. **Facet:** Ilshenar route finale, candidate unlock.

Pilot Anwen Dale's relief crew has a sound hull but a crossing compass that points into open water. Bring the survey from Mistveil and the repaired instrument from Azurite Caverns, then clear wreck scavengers from a safe embarkation site. Anwen conducts the first controlled passage to Ilshenar and records the player's return route. Later trips use the earned permission instead of demanding another compass or another loop.

## Selected custom dungeons

### 17. Agurast Ascension: One More Flight

**Anchor:** entrance, 1560, 2624, 1. **Level:** 150-175. **Systems:** League, loops, companions. **Facet:** No.

Former League surveyor Sella Vane and her exhausted crew keep extending their climb because each landing promises a final answer. Reopen their marked retreat, recover their survey, and convince Sella to descend with her living crew before confronting the creature blocking the return. A companion may remember the expedition on a later visit. This is a late side story about choosing to stop, not a replacement for Tamsin's rescue or the Setting's loop floors.

### 18. Amul Seketsi Royal Tomb: The Wrong Heir

**Anchor:** entrance, 4197, 1445, 0. **Level:** 125-150. **Systems:** Keepers of the Last Door, League, Appraisal. **Facet:** No.

Registrar Edda Marlow studies the tomb while relic dealer Halden Sorn presents a forged inheritance and sends mercenaries to empty it. Compare the succession tablets, expose the false seal, and stop the tomb's awakened custodian without destroying the records. Appraisal and Forensics uncover optional details. The title and tomb remain Zigholtul's place names; this proposed claimant and dispute make no assertion about an author-written royal history.

### 19. Azurite Caverns: The Needle Under Stone

**Anchor:** entrance, 4282, 562, 2. **Level:** 60-80. **Systems:** Miners' Compact, Tinkers' Union, League. **Facet:** Ilshenar route component.

Prospector Ellis Ward has found the instrument named on Brin's chart, lodged behind a flooded work face occupied by crystal-fed cave creatures. Survey a safe approach, recover its scattered bearings, and repair it on the field bench. Mining and Tinkering offer specialist work; Ellis can perform the repair after the player secures the parts. Deliver the compass to Skypoint. It is travel equipment, never a blade piece or a device for resetting characters.

### 20. Black Widow Pit: The Silk Account

**Anchor:** entrance, 2126, 3439, 10. **Level:** 65-85. **Systems:** Tailors' Circle, Order of the Alembic, League. **Facet:** No.

Tailor Delia Moss's gathering crew has been driven into a webbed side chamber by giant spiders. Retrieve their marked bundles, prepare a temporary clearing wash, and lead the survivors out before confronting the nesting matriarch. Tailoring and Alchemy turn the salvage into a practical delivery order. Any specialist silk and its recipe would need a later material spec; this seed does not promise a new top-tier cloth or automatic mastery gain.

### 21. Bleak Wind Tunnels: A Lamp for the Return

**Anchor:** entrance, 474, 3300, 1. **Level:** 85-105. **Systems:** Rangers' League, Camping, companions. **Facet:** No.

Trail keeper Hollis Beck has lost contact with a survey party after scavengers stripped its route lamps. Relight the marked junctions, establish a sheltered rest point, and escort the party through a wind-haunted passage. A companion can carry a spare lamp as dialogue flavor; Hollis provides it for solo players. The rest point is a quest shelter, not an early grant of the skills design's high-skill dungeon Camping benefits.

### 22. Dragons Den: The Stolen Brood

**Anchor:** entrance, 3194, 3818, -19. **Level:** 160-185. **Systems:** League, Rangers' League, levelable gear. **Facet:** No.

Brood thief Varric Pell has barricaded himself among the dragons with the missing egg shipments from Dragonstorm Isle. Follow Selda's tally, recover eggs from his warded stores, and survive a confrontation with an original guardian designed for this band. The finish rewards the rescue and an expedition record. Combat may advance already sealed gear normally; the dragon hoard does not hand out a levelable artifact or a free jump to item level 200.

### 23. Fortress Calcifina: An Answer in Stone

**Anchor:** entrance, 3051, 156, 2. **Level:** 135-150. **Systems:** Artificers' Order, Arcane Society, League. **Facet:** Ter Mur route finale, candidate unlock.

Stonewright Arda Quill is trapped with a crossing marker that fortress scavengers have wired into a defensive engine. Bring Glimmerwood's rubbing, free Arda, and disconnect the stolen fittings before the original stone guardian wakes. Reassemble the marker as an invitation, then establish a safe arrival and return point with a proposed gargoyle contact in Ter Mur. Neither Keeper nor Breaker gets to claim ownership of that crossing.

### 24. Fungully Grotto: The Sample That Breathes

**Anchor:** entrance, 1714, 1066, 5. **Level:** 25-45. **Systems:** Order of the Alembic, Taste Identification, League. **Facet:** No.

Field brewer Mina Bell shelters above a grotto where stolen culture jars have turned a gathering site into a choking fungal bed. Identify the leaking jars, gather uncontaminated samples, and clear a route for the missing pickers. Taste Identification uses a safe prepared sample rather than asking the player to eat unknown mushrooms. Orla's guild can post the follow-up brewing order without making a new Herbalism subsystem a prerequisite.

### 25. Ghuul Sanctuary: Room for the Names

**Anchor:** entrance, 1350, 1500, 20. **Level:** 90-110. **Systems:** Keepers of the Last Door, League, Spirit Speak. **Facet:** Malas route finale, candidate unlock.

Memorial keeper Rowan Pike tends a passage occupied by name thieves and their bound shades. Bring Dhampire's corrected register and Red Dagger's passenger list, match the missing people to the memorial, and defeat the collector preventing their families from crossing. Restoring the memorial reveals a stable road to Malas. This is a separate local case, with no Crossguard, feeding reveal, or release of Doom's company outside its League chapter.

### 26. Hammerhill Sewers: What the Water Carries

**Anchor:** entrance, 1553, 1017, -10. **Level:** 15-30. **Systems:** Custodians, Tinkers' Union, League. **Facet:** No.

Drain tender Elsie Crow and her cleanup crew have been forced out by scavengers selling stolen pump parts. Trace the overflow, recover the parts, and open a safe working route through the vermin nests. Civic cleanup and a separate League repair delivery give different kinds of players useful work. The cleanup portion uses the Custodians' own progression; it does not invent a Custodian requisition or pay League Renown for every discarded object.

### 27. Haven Catacombs: The Unclaimed Tool Roll

**Anchor:** entrance, 3578, 2589, 0. **Level:** 20-40. **Systems:** League, Society of Smiths, Arms Lore. **Facet:** No.

Repairer Cora Venn has been searching burial workrooms for her mother's marked tools while grave scavengers strip their fittings. Find the roll by comparing maker's marks, restore a damaged stair brace, and bring Cora home with the record of who maintained the tunnels. This is an independent local job. The map name does not make these catacombs the P36 tutorial dungeon or move Chapter 1's Pommel chamber here.

### 28. Mongbat Hideout: A Coat Full of Receipts

**Anchor:** entrance, 1769, 1148, 0. **Level:** 10-25. **Systems:** League, Tracking, Merchants' Exchange. **Facet:** No.

Courier Nellie Hart has taken shelter just inside a cave after mongbats stole her mail pouch for nesting cloth. Track the scattered scraps, retrieve the deliveries from three marked nests, and escort her past the roost's largest scavenger. Receipts identify who should receive the recovered goods, giving Appraisal an optional investigation beat. Her pouch contains ordinary household business, so this early job can help citizens before teaching the League's hidden history.

### 29. Oboru Burial Ground: The Song Left Unfinished

**Anchor:** entrance, 1810, 1056, 0. **Level:** 70-90. **Systems:** Bards' Consortium, Keepers of the Last Door, League. **Facet:** No.

Mourning singer Iona Bell has returned to record names that tomb scavengers chiseled from the stones. Gather the surviving inscriptions, protect her while she records them, and silence the grave caller using their fragments to lure mourners below. Music and Spirit Speak offer optional witness accounts. Every build can complete the memorial, with no compulsory bard success, deliberate death, or claim to have ended Britannia's loop.

### 30. Passage of Lost Souls: The Road Home

**Anchor:** entrance, 4589, 3288, 0. **Level:** 175-200. **Systems:** League, loops, companions, Keepers of the Last Door. **Facet:** No.

Wayfinder Tess Marlow guards a procession whose route signs have been turned inward by a creature that feeds on fear of departure. Rebuild the route from the travelers' accounts and escort them past its final barrier. Characters past Chapter 5 may give a Keeper or Breaker response, with equal access and the same rescued people. An optional companion remembrance survives a later reset. No scene reverses the player's choice or supplies the Ring.

### 31. Stone Burrow Mines: The Weight of the Claim

**Anchor:** entrance, 1908, 1766, 15. **Level:** 100-125. **Systems:** Miners' Compact, Society of Smiths, levelable gear. **Facet:** No.

Mine recorder Amos Flint has evidence that a contractor moved claim markers after a collapse and trapped the original crew behind his guards. Survey the true work face, stabilize its supports, and recover the sealed claim book. Material-matched Mining and repair tasks can train through use; already sealed crafted gear can grow during the rescue fighting. The payout is guild work and a recorded deposit, not unrestricted Celestial ore at a lower skill tier.

### 32. Whispering Hollow: The Answering Voice

**Anchor:** entrance, 1915, 1332, -55. **Level:** 110-125. **Systems:** Arcane Society, Bards' Consortium, League. **Facet:** Tokuno route finale, candidate unlock.

Acoustician Fenna Reed follows an old crossing signal while a cave creature copies her crew's voices to lead them off the marked route. Bring Tigerclaw's charter, compare its survey rhythm with the echoes, and restore the real signal after confronting the mimic voice. The repaired crossing establishes passage to Tokuno. Fenna supplies the required rhythm, so no bard build is mandatory. This echo is a navigation signal, not the Crossguard's feeding or a new sword fragment.

## Optional facet routes

These are planning options for Chase, not a decision to close existing facets. Each route finishes on the Sosaria map, so its prerequisite never lies inside the facet it unlocks. Safe destination tiles, map availability, travel restrictions, and return handling need verification before implementation.

| Facet | Quest route | Suggested finale band | If Chase instead chooses a loop milestone |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ilshenar | Mistveil Ruins -> Azurite Caverns -> Skypoint Islands | 70-90 | Loop 1, permanent maximum 100 |
| Malas | Dhampire Ruins and Red Dagger Keep -> Ghuul Sanctuary | 90-110 | Loop 3, permanent maximum 110 |
| Tokuno | Tigerclaw Fortress -> Whispering Hollow | 110-125 | Loop 6, permanent maximum 125 |
| Ter Mur | Glimmerwood -> Fortress Calcifina | 135-150 | Loop 11, permanent maximum 150 |

The loop column is an alternative to quest-gated travel, not another prerequisite added to each chain. Under that option, the quests still offer stories and guild work. These proposed milestones follow F-12's corrected ladder. They are not tied to League rank, and they do not grant the current levels in the adjacent column.

For quest gating, save the travel permission permanently when each character completes the finale. A reset must not strand that character away from housing, guild contacts, or a return point. Keep scroll sources on the starting land available so earning a later loop cannot depend on a facet that the same loop would unlock. Guild-specific skill tasks should have supplied NPC assistance or a general adventuring route for required travel chains.

Chapter access needs a separate rule: provide reachable League staging markers on the starting land for eligible private trials. Chapter 3 must reach its private Doom trial without first demanding public Malas access, and Chapter 5 must reach its private Exodus trial without first demanding public Ilshenar access. A chapter visit grants neither public facet permission nor the side chain's rewards. Keep the chapters' existing rank and sequence rules; adding travel gates must not create a circular prerequisite or change the planned story locations.

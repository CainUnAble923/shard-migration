# League Chapter 3: One More Victory

**Draft for Chase to edit. P601, October 25, 2026.**

Verite Citizen trial. Blade piece: **the Crossguard**. Place: **a private trial copy of Doom's Gauntlet in Malas**. Chapter question: **What does the loop want?**

This is a playable story proposal based on [the League storyline](../docs/league-storyline.md) and the voice of [Chapter 2's draft](cc-P593-league-chapter-2-draft.md). All new names, dialogue, encounters, timing, and reward handling below are drafts. They are not implemented features or approved canon. Proposed length: about 35 to 45 minutes, solo or with a party. Exact placement, difficulty, effects, and trial-map support need implementation review and a client walkthrough.

## What the player takes away

The pieces of the broken sword do more than trap people in a loop. They feed on their return. Every reset strengthens them. Doom keeps its heroes fighting because heroes keep coming back.

The player learns this by meeting people who have already won, and are still here. Taking the Crossguard lets those dead rest within this party's trial. The public dungeon continues as usual.

The Unbound, the vision of a world without the loop, and the Mend or Sunder choice belong to later chapters. Here the player leaves with a piece of the sword and a reason to question what recovering it will do.

## Setting and cast

Use the Gauntlet's familiar circuit: its side rooms and central fight, with a quiet place to speak between encounters. Stock stone, doors, pentagrams, human ghosts, and existing Doom creature art are enough. No new art is required by this draft. Room labels below are staging names, not verified coordinates.

At first the place sounds occupied. Buckles click. Someone checks a blade. A woman tears cloth into bandages. Nobody speaks until the player approaches.

**Elara Voss, the League Registrar.** She sends the player after the next piece. The Despise account has given her evidence that people can be caught inside a smaller loop. She has not yet learned what the pieces gain from it.

**Mara Fen, a dead healer.** Proposed new NPC. She wears an ordinary traveling coat and carries a satchel with one clean bandage left. She remembers tending the same companions after the same battles. She believes that one complete victory will finally let them leave.

**Dain, a dead shield bearer.** Proposed new NPC. He waits at the first room's door, checking a split shield strap. His repeated fall makes the feeding visible without requiring the player to die.

**The other dead.** Unnamed members of Mara's company, seen resting or preparing at the remaining doors. Give them brief, ordinary actions. One tests a bowstring. One counts supplies. One sits with boots off, rubbing feet that no longer touch the floor.

**The Crossguard.** Black metal held above the central floor, its two arms reaching toward the side rooms. Pale lines link the company to it. These are proposed story effects. The piece has no face and gives no speech explaining itself. Its clearest answer is what happens when someone returns.

## Beat 1: A piece that answers

Offer this as the Verite chapter after Chapter 2. Characters already at or past Verite can return for the story and rewards under the existing waiver decision. This does not add a retroactive rank requirement or replace the separate promotion job.

At the field office, the player sets the Grip beside the Pommel. The two pieces pull together, then turn across the desk toward Malas.

**Elara:** "They have been quiet since Despise. What changed?"

**Player:** "I put them together."

Elara lays a map beside them. The pieces turn again when she moves it.

**Elara:** "Doom. People go there to fight their way around the same halls. Some have made a trade of it."

**Player:** "Another expedition?"

**Elara:** "No missing register this time. I want you to look at what is happening in front of everyone."

Optional question, **"What should I bring back?"**

**Elara:** "The next piece, if it is there. And an account of what holds it. We know the sword broke time. We still do not know why time keeps doing the same thing."

Accept text:

> Follow the blade pieces to Doom. Find what holds the Gauntlet's dead to their circuit. You may bring your party.

A League trial marker at the real dungeon entrance offers entry to the private copy. Travel inside that trial brings the party to the Gauntlet staging area. Do not require a public Gauntlet clear, a rare drop, or a golden skull payment to start this chapter. That access is a proposal for the story trial only.

## Beat 2: Ready for the next round

Mara is folding the last bandage. Dain stands at the first door.

**Mara:** "Hold still. I can get another strip from the lining."

**Dain:** "Save it. The strap will last one more room."

They stop when the party approaches.

**Mara:** "You are living. Good. We could use another pair of hands."

**Player:** "How long have you been fighting here?"

**Mara:** "We have nearly finished. Dain holds the door, we clear the rooms, then we meet in the middle. After that we go home."

Inspection of Dain:

> His shield has been repaired through the same holes until the leather is mostly stitching. Behind it, you can see the stone through his chest.

Selecting **"Show Mara the blade pieces"** makes the Crossguard answer with a dull light above the central floor. The lines to the dead become visible for a moment.

**Mara:** "That thing. It shines when we come back. I thought it was showing us the way."

Objective:

> Accompany the company through the first room. Watch the Crossguard.

## Beat 3: A victory already won

Begin a short, trial-only fight using the first Gauntlet room's creature theme. Dain takes his place in a separate, visible scene at the doorway. His story actions do not depend on escort AI or on the player keeping him healed.

As the room clears, Dain falls to a scripted shadow at the threshold. The fight is already over. Mara kneels beside him and reaches for the bandage.

**Mara:** "We cleared it. Dain, we cleared it."

The bandage touches his hand. A pale line pulls tight between him and the Crossguard. His shape disappears, then reforms at the door with the broken strap in his hands.

The Crossguard grows brighter. A gap along one arm fills with black metal. Show both changes clearly, with text as well as an effect.

> Dain returns to his place. The Crossguard's broken edge grows whole.

For a brief transition, the company resumes its opening positions. Mara folds her clean bandage.

**Dain:** "Save it. The strap will last one more room."

**Player:** "He said that before."

**Mara:** "I know. Please do not ask me how many times."

This is the one required story rewind. The player stays alive, keeps equipment and ordinary progression, and retains the cleared room. There is no second combat clear or deliberate player death needed to continue.

Journal update:

> The company returned, and the Crossguard grew stronger. Speak to Mara before starting another round.

## Beat 4: What the bandage cannot mend

In a safe conversation area, Mara opens her satchel. Inside are folded scraps bearing the same short instruction in her handwriting.

**Mara:** "I put a note where I would find it. Then I found another underneath."

Readable scrap:

> We won. All the rooms, and the thing in the middle. Dain reached the door. It brought him back before he could step through. Do not tell them another victory will do it.

**Player:** "You have already finished the circuit."

**Mara:** "Yes. I read that, and then someone calls for me. I have one bandage. There must be someone I can still help."

Selecting **"Watch the line when Dain returns"** replays a short journal illustration of the witnessed scene, without another reset. The light runs from Dain into the Crossguard. It does not run from the piece to him.

**Player:** "It is taking something from him."

**Mara:** "Every time he comes back."

**Player:** "And from the others."

**Mara:** "I kept telling them to get up."

**Player:** "You did not make it hold them here."

Mara looks toward Dain, still working at the strap.

**Mara:** "Then help me tell them they can stop."

The feeding is the chapter's reveal: the dead returning makes the piece stronger. It does not imply that healing, resurrection, normal dungeon visits, or the player's legacy resets incur a new gameplay penalty. No real character statistic is drained. The scene gives evidence of the story's wider rule that every reset feeds the pieces.

## Beat 5: Finish their watch

The party completes the remaining side rooms. Each has a short combat encounter using that room's existing Doom theme, followed by a quiet interaction with a member of the company. The first room remains cleared and its interaction is available immediately.

After each fight, a pale line still holds the waiting dead to the Crossguard. Fighting alone has not loosened it. The player selects **"Ask Mara to speak to them"** at the marked doorway. Mara appears beside that witness for the scene, so the chapter does not depend on protecting a fragile escort.

At Dain's door:

**Dain:** "Is it time?"

**Mara:** "Your watch is finished."

**Dain:** "Who takes it after me?"

**Player:** "We will get the others out."

Dain sets the shield against the wall. He steps away from the door. His line slackens, and one light on the Crossguard goes dark. He stays beside Mara, still bound to the place until the piece is removed.

Keep the other exchanges brief. Proposed examples:

**The archer:** "I have enough arrows for the next room."

**Mara:** "Keep them. Come and sit with Dain."

**The supply keeper:** "If we leave now, we waste everything we carried in."

**Player:** "You carried it far enough."

**The tired fighter:** "I took my boots off. Give me a moment."

**Mara:** "Take it. We can wait."

Every side room gets a visible completion mark and a journal entry. These are interactions any character can perform. No Spirit Speak requirement, guild gate, spelling quiz, simultaneous switches, or sacrifice of a blade piece is needed. A solo player can complete every step; a party can share the fights and conversations.

Objective after the doors are quiet:

> The company has stood down. Enter the central hall and free the Crossguard.

## Beat 6: The last call to arms

The company waits at the edge of the central hall. Mara has stopped tearing her coat.

The Crossguard sounds like metal struck against stone. Dain reaches for his shield, then lowers his hand.

**Dain:** "That was always the signal."

**Mara:** "Let it call."

A trial guardian rises beneath the piece, using the Dark Father's existing appearance. This is a proposed story encounter, not a promise that the stock Dark Father is balanced for a Verite solo character. Choose its combat numbers and ability set in the implementation prompt.

Ready prompt:

> The Crossguard will try to call the company back into battle. Defeat its guardian and release each binding when it appears. Begin when your party is ready.

Fight proposal: a clear central encounter with a visible heavy-strike warning. At two health thresholds, the guardian becomes briefly protected and the Crossguard pulls a line toward a member of the company. A marked binding appears near the hall's edge. Any party member can use it to release the line. The guardian pauses its attacks during this interaction, so a solo player has time to respond.

First binding prompt:

> The Crossguard is calling Dain back. Release the binding before returning to the fight.

**Dain:** "I put it down. I am not taking it up again."

Second binding prompt:

> The Crossguard has caught Mara's bandage. Release her from the call.

**Mara:** "I have nothing left to mend here."

She opens her hand. The bandage falls without reaching the floor.

Ignoring a binding leaves the encounter paused with an explicit objective. It does not kill a witness, erase a room, or create another rewind. These interactions and protections are new draft mechanics, not existing Gauntlet abilities.

When the guardian falls, the Crossguard drops low enough to reach. The room remains quiet. Nobody resumes a starting position.

## Beat 7: Leave it quiet

Select **"Take the Crossguard"** to break the remaining lines and record personal recovery for eligible participants. The company can finally leave the circuit.

Inspection:

> The metal is warm where the dead were bound to it. You fit the Grip between its arms without fastening them together. The warmth moves toward your hands. For a moment, you want to turn back and fight the hall again.

The player lowers the pieces. The feeling passes. No hidden choice or permanent penalty follows this inspection.

**Player:** "It still wants us to go back."

**Mara:** "Then keep walking."

Dain picks up his shield. This time he carries it at his side. The dead pass through the exit together, their shapes fading beyond the threshold. Mara waits until the last has gone.

**Mara:** "Will you tell someone we finished?"

**Player:** "I will tell the League."

**Mara:** "Good. I would hate to be sent back for leaving a report unwritten."

She smiles once and follows them.

Leave a moment of stillness before offering the return. No new wave spawns in the completed trial. The dead are released here, for this party. This is not a claim that every soul in Doom has been freed, and it changes no public spawn or other party's chapter.

Objective:

> Return to Elara Voss with the Crossguard and Mara's account.

## Beat 8: What we have been gathering

At the office, Elara waits for the player to finish speaking before she writes.

**Elara:** "Mara Fen. Dain. Their company. Released from the circuit."

**Player:** "They had won before. The piece brought them back anyway. It grew stronger when they returned."

Elara lays the Crossguard beside the Grip and Pommel. She leaves space between them.

**Elara:** "We thought the pieces were holding time where it broke."

**Player:** "They are feeding on it."

**Elara:** "On us."

Optional question, **"Does the sword want this?"**

**Elara:** "I cannot tell you whether metal wants anything. I can tell you what you saw it do. It held them, brought them back, and took something each time."

**Player:** "Then why keep gathering it?"

**Elara:** "Because leaving it there would have left them there. And because we need to understand what broke before we decide what to do with it."

She writes the last line of the report.

**Elara:** "They finished. I will make sure that part is clear."

End with the recovered pieces quiet on the desk. Do not show the unlooped world, name a rival faction, or ask the player to choose the sword's fate yet.

## Reward and completion handling, proposed

- **The Crossguard:** a unique story keepsake and the third recovered blade piece. Suggested item text: "Taken from Doom when the company laid down its arms. The metal is still warm." No combat stats, automatic reforging, or new reset bonus in this chapter.
- **Chapter journal:** Mara's note, the witnessed feeding, and the company's release remain readable. Inspecting the recovered pieces does not consume them.
- **Chapter completion:** record Chapter 3 after the company is released and the player reports to Elara. Apply the existing Verite trial check where applicable. The ordinary promotion job, standing, and Renown requirements remain separate.
- **Ownership:** each eligible participant gets personal recovery and completion credit, with one earned Crossguard per character. Helpers can accompany friends without receiving duplicate pieces. Avoid last-hit, first-click, or shared-loot competition.
- **Other payouts:** leave XP, Renown, and gold amounts for the build prompt. The trial offers no promised stock Doom artifact roll or repeatable artifact farming route.

As in Chapter 2's draft, prefer a durable recovery record with a replaceable keepsake. A full backpack should leave the reward available from Elara. Losing a keepsake should not block the next chapter or create a second earned reward. League custody instead of personal originals remains an editing question, not a settled item design.

## Retry and party notes for the eventual build

Keep the objectives explicit: meet Mara, witness the first return, read her note, clear the side rooms and stand down the company, defeat the central guardian, recover the Crossguard, report to Elara. All required dialogue can be revisited.

The party needs one short story rewind. Actual player defeats and disconnects should not strengthen the guardian, replay the reveal, or take away the company's released-room progress within a continuing trial. A failed fight restarts that encounter. If the trial expires or is abandoned, reentry can restage unfinished scenes, but durable recovery and completion records must survive. Do not award farming loot from repeated or reset scenes.

Keep witnesses safe from combat and prevent them from wandering into another room. A solo player uses the same bindings as a party. Do not require a tank, a healer, or a minimum party size. Difficulty scaling, participation range, regrouping after death, and trial persistence need a later build spec.

Return characters safely to the real entrance when they leave or an instance closes. Preserve earned recovery if a connection drops before the office report. Characters following the story for their own credit complete Chapters 1 and 2 first; friends at other stages may help. No scene changes normal legacy progression, public Doom, or another trial's state.

# October 25 patch notes, follow-up

Draft supplement to [the earlier October 25 notes](cc-P594-patch-notes-2026-10-25.md), covering later merges through P603. This interval adds documentation and preparation for future work. No additional game-code changes merged in the checked snapshot. Running-shard availability and publication of these drafts remain unverified.

## High Seas corrections

- Current boat delivery orders can award only first-tier rewards: some bait, lava lobster traps, Fishing Guide volumes 1 or 2, Fishing power scrolls, or plain fishing poles. Hooks, the Oracle of the Sea, and Charybdis bait are outside the reachable rewards. Repeating orders or raising fish-monger standing does not unlock them. This corrects the earlier notes' reward list.
- Lava-pole fishing and the Oracle summoning mechanism are built, but their equipment still needs staff supplies. Normal access to Charybdis remains blocked.
- Accepted fish orders currently have no usable player cancellation action. An unfinished shipping crate can prevent dry docking. Choose orders carefully and ask staff for help if you cannot finish one. The earlier description of destroying a crate was not a usable player cancellation route.
- The later review found further unresolved fishing item-handling and delivery checks. No repair merged in this interval. Corgul's player access and normal fish-pie ingredient supplies also remain unfinished.

## Guide drafts

- A new [Hammer of Hephaestus guide](wiki-drafts/hammer-of-hephaestus.md) covers the first free hammer, tiers T1 through T3, familiarity, upgrades, recharging, automatic deed filing, and Craft This Order. It preserves the warnings about whole small deeds being consumed, hammer recovery, and outdated payout tooltips. T4 remains unavailable.
- The [High Seas guide](wiki-drafts/high-seas-fishing.md) now explains the merged bait, hooks, lava fishing, SOS finds, delivery orders, Oracle, and fish pies. It distinguishes built features from equipment and encounters players can actually reach.
- The [Rangers, Custodians, and Healers guide](wiki-drafts/rangers-custodians-healers.md) now explains the existing elixir of rebirth sale and use. It also describes Tracking Ping as a proposal. This guide update adds neither a new elixir sale nor a working Ping ability.

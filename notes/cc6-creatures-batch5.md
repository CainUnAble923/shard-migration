**The breath, read in both trees rather than taken from the earlier notes.** ServUO's `DragonBreath` is a
`SpecialAbility` whose numbers come from a `DragonBreathDefinition` chosen by creature type (`Services/Pet Training/
SpecialAbility.cs:860-1010`): the default is fire (0.16 of current hits, 100% fire, 30-45 s between breaths, 1.0 s
stall, 1.3 s effect delay, item 0x36D4, sound 0x227, animation 12), and the second definition, `Uses = {
SkeletalDragonRenowned, SkeletalDragon }`, is the same with 100% cold and effect hue 0x480. Pinned `FireBreath.cs`
carries those exact fire numbers line for line, `ColdBreath : FireBreath` overrides `ColdDamage => 100`,
`FireDamage => 0`, `BreathEffectHue => 0x480`, and stock `SkeletalDragon.cs:60` and `Drake.cs:56` declare them
through `GetMonsterAbilities()`. **CC4 Shame already made this mapping** (`notes/cc4-shame.md`, "Pet Training
abilities") and batches 2, 3 and 4 did not read it: `Slith` (D-55), `ToxicSlith` (D-56), `FireDaemon` (D-62) and
`CrystalHydra` (D-68) all take the default fire definition and were dropped as P6 losses. So `SkeletalDragonRenowned`
declares `MonsterAbilities.ColdBreath`, `Grim` inherits `FireBreath` from pinned `Drake`, and the four earlier files
are Q-054 (§5, §8) — not edited here, because that is four earlier ports and a `docs/patch-backlog.md` row.

What the mapping does not reproduce, so it is said once: ServUO triggers a breath **on think**, 10% per think while
off cooldown, and it costs 30 mana (`SpecialAbility.cs:176-185`, `CheckMana`, `TriggerChance` default 0.1); pinned
triggers on a **combat action** (`BaseWeapon.cs:893`, `BaseRanged.cs:74`), 50% while off cooldown, no mana. Both then
wait 30-45 s. Range is 12 there and `RangePerception` (18 for a renowned) here. In a fight the cadence is close; a
mana-starved creature breathes here where it would not there. CC4 accepted the same difference.

**Grim, reviewed against the PINNED `Drake`, not ServUO's** (AGENTS.md, the carrier-child rule applied to a stock
parent): the two Drakes agree member for member — random body 60/61, sound 362, the stats, tameable at 84.3 with 2

was lost by the parent being stock rather than ported.

**Subclassing a stock generator-serialized type follows batch 4's rule exactly.** `Grim : Drake` carries its own
`[SerializationGenerator(0, false)]`, is `partial`, and the generator gave it its own `(Serial)` constructor and version
slot after `Drake`'s — the test checks the attribute with `inherit: false` and the constructor's `DeclaringType`, as
batch 4 did for the executioner. `BaseRenowned` is `abstract partial` with the attribute (the `BaseSetArmor` and
`PeerlessKey` shape) and each of the thirteen carries its own; the test pins all fourteen. Stock `Saliva : Harpy` is
the same shape, so nothing here was new to the generator.

**One member added over ServUO's, and why:** ServUO leaves `BaseRenowned.m_DamageEntries` null until `OnBeforeDeath`
creates it, so `RegisterDamage`/`AwardArtifact` cannot be driven outside a death. The field is initialised at
declaration here as well, and `OnBeforeDeath` still replaces it exactly as ServUO does, so the death path is
byte-for-byte ServUO's and a test can also exercise the weighting directly. Not player-visible.

**Not deviations, recorded because they read like typos and are ServUO's (for `docs/bug-list.md` §3, all of the
"missing" kind, all copied and pinned by the test where the test can reach them):**

- **The recipient roll is strict, so a roll equal to the total gives the artifact to nobody.** `AwardArtifact` draws
  `RandomMinMax(1, totalDamage)` and then hands out on `runningTotal > roll`; when the roll is exactly the total, the
  last entry fails the test and the loop ends. A lone damager who did exactly 1 damage therefore **never** receives
  (`RandomMinMax(1, 1)` is 1, and 1 > 1 is false); with real totals it is one chance in the total. The artifact is left
  constructed and un-parented (ServUO's leak too: an item at no location in the save). Build C of this batch went red
  on it once in 100 draws at damage 3100; the test now pins the 1-damage case deterministically. Missing, not broken:
  a drop that occasionally does not roll.
- **Eligibility sizes the roll and nothing else.** `AwardArtifact` filters the *total* through `IsEligible` (player,
  alive, within 32 tiles, room in the pack) but walks the **whole** table to pick the recipient, so a dead player, or one
  40 tiles away, still receives when they are the only damager, and an ineligible entry early in the table can absorb a
  roll sized for the eligible ones. Pinned: 200 kills by a player 40 tiles away, 31 artifacts (the same 15%). Generous
  rather than missing; copied.
- `PixieRenowned.SetStr(-350, 380)`: `RawStr` clamps at 1 on both emulators, so about half of these pixies have 1
  Strength; `SetInt(700, 8500)`; Karma **+7000** (an innocent that hands out artifacts). `RakktaviRenowned` and
  `TikitaviRenowned` `SetHits(50000)` and `VitaviRenowned` 45000-50000 — the ratmen are the tankiest things in the
  Abyss by a factor of twenty. `Tempest.ControlSlots = 2` on a creature nothing can tame.
- `Flurry`'s corpse is "The remains of Flurry" with a capital T; `Tempest`'s and `Grim`'s are lower case;
  `Mistral`'s is "a mistral corpse".
- ServUO's `Grim` re-declares Drake's breath; `SkeletalDragonRenowned` sets `Hue = 906` on a body whose stock
  counterpart is unhued.
- The gargish cloth artifacts do not swap graphic by gender (§2): ServUO's clothing bases do (Arms, Shirts,
  OuterLegs, Pants — 4 files), pinned ModernUO's Type1/Type2 armour does not, and CC9's `SpinedBloodwormBracers` did
  not reproduce it either. Cosmetic; if it ever matters it is ~6 lines each in `SpinedBloodwormBracers`,
  `MantleOfTheFallen`, `SummonersKilt` (an `OnAdded(IEntity)` override on `Mobile.Female`), and a decision about
  whether the shard wants any gargish gear to swap when its stock gear does not.

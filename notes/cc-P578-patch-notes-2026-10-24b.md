# October 24 patch notes, continued

Draft for the next package and server update. These changes supplement the earlier October 24 notes.

## Rootdeep Burrows

- The entrance in Old Haven's ruined bank is now a wide burrow surrounded by dirt and rocks. Step into its dark mouth to go down. The rope ladder back up leaves you outside the bank's broken south wall.
- The new entrance needs the matching map update.

## Clean Up Britannia

- Cleaning up no longer gives civic waste bundles. Civic Contracts now count Clean Up points earned after you accept them and pay the same gold as before. Turning one in leaves your store points available to spend; those earnings cannot also finish another Civic Contract.
- Hand leftover civic waste bundles to the Sanitation Warden for 10 Clean Up points each. If you already hold a Civic Contract from before this change, open its Active page before redeeming bundles so those new points count toward it.
- Your trash bag has two new right-click options: Clear a corpse and Clear corpses nearby. Any player who owns a trash bag can use them. Join the Custodians at a Sanitation Warden for a free bag.
- Clearing pays Clean Up points and deletes the corpse with everything still inside. It asks for confirmation when loot remains. Player corpses are protected, and other players' monster loot rights are respected.
- Update the player package for the new menu labels.

## Miners' Compact

- Stone Contracts appear after you learn stone mining. Garrett's Learn Stone Mining page sells Mining For Quality Stone from Journeyman rank for 50 Mining Vouchers. Reading it requires 100 base Mining. Stone Contracts you already hold can still be turned in.
- The two raw ore contracts are now mining quotas: Mine Iron Ore asks for 100 iron ore; Mine Dull Copper Ore asks for 50 dull copper ore, Journeyman rank, 65 Mining, and a reported Dull Copper discovery. Only matching ore mined after accepting counts. You keep the ore when you finish.
- Contracts held before the change can still be completed with their original iron ore turn-in, including the old 150-iron Ore Refinement Run.
- When restoring Jacob's Pickaxe, the Liaison can cover missing Mining Vouchers for 100 gold each. Your existing vouchers, materials, and normal gold price still apply. The page shows the prices before you confirm.

## League Register

- Blaze repair remains free and now repairs only gear you are wearing. Remote Repair Deeds still cover worn, carried, and banked gear.
- Field repair deeds and kits must still be usable and in your backpack when you select the item to repair.
- The League Field Satchel accepts filled guild satchels and lets resources reach the satchels inside. A refused insertion costs no merge fee, and moving a satchel around inside is free.
- The Field Satchel counts as one item only when directly in your own backpack. Its contents count in bank and house storage, and their weight still counts in your pack.
- Registrar purchases and removing a guild from your Register now ask you to make room if delivery fails, without taking payment or dropping the replacement at your feet.
- Turning in ready orders through the Register now finds completed smith deeds inside bags and books in your backpack.

## New Haven tutorial, on the test shard

- Each account receives one starter pet, from either the Farmhand or graduation. Skipping and remedial training do not give a second pet.
- An unchosen graduation pet stays available after closing the window, logging out, or a server restart. Rook offers the choice again.
- Other characters on the account can read Rook's page, but the original trainee keeps control of lessons and rewards.
- The Farmhand's bandage supply is limited to three stacks of ten per account. The patch-up lesson requires a successful bandage heal.
- Players outside the tutorial cannot attack its creatures.

## Crafting and rewards

- Abandoning a smith commission through the guild book now starts the same wait as abandoning it at the guildmaster. Small and large commissions share the wait, five minutes by default, before another request of either size.
- Fletching, carpentry, and tinkering bulk orders now reject shop-bought weapons and clothing.
- Levelable gear no longer earns experience from killing pets.
- First-time boss loot boxes now trigger for qualifying participants.
- Eligible boss and paragon loot can now receive the rare Self Repair roll.
- Kills finished by your pets, summons, or creatures you provoke now count toward your League bestiary. Killing a tamed pet no longer pays Butcher's Eye.
- Some hidden achievements now check their conditions more carefully.

## Known limits

- Nearby corpse clearing scans again when you confirm. Stay at the same spot and check nearby loot before accepting. Bonded pet corpses can also be cleared, so keep them away from this action.
- A heavily filled League Field Satchel can still be refused when you return it to your backpack. Reduce its contents before moving it back.
- Keep room in your backpack when asking the Farmhand for bandages: failed delivery can leave them on the ground and still use an allowance. Deleting an active trainee can also leave the account's tutorial unable to continue on another character.

# P535: skills design versus the build

Checked October 24, 2026. Notes only.

Main has the shared gain machinery, material-based crafting paths, several high-skill effects, Pet Training, and Appraisal. It does not yet deliver the skills design as a whole. Higher training content, many mastery rewards, and the player level and reset system remain unfinished. P191's rate change is present in source. P283 and P282 are research and planning inputs, not implementations of their proposed content or progression.

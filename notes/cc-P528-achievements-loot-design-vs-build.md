# P528: achievements, accessory slots, and loot boxes compared with main

Reviewed October 24, 2026. Notes only. Code HEAD and local main were both
`f2853e86ca96c89d8f97a6c3a17c9fd2661ad41d`; the design and notes checkout was
`99f25629d390acc690296e2cd8edc168a4338523`, also equal to its local main. No remote
refresh was attempted. These findings describe the supplied source, not the running shard.

The achievement grant system, most event hooks, player toe rings and extra bracelets, fixed
box contents, safe opening, and server fanfare are built. The largest gaps are boss-box
delivery, achievement replay on later loops, companion accessory integration, unfinished
reward items, and sources waiting for player progression or League chapters. Existing
implementation reviews also identify correctness work in otherwise built features.

This is an internal design comparison. The detailed tables include hidden achievement
conditions for implementers; they are not player guidance. No AP ceiling or title count is
published here. Keep hidden achievements and their titles secret in player material.

## Baseline and reading the tables

Design inputs are `docs/feature-queue.md:3190` (F-40), `:3216` (F-41), and `:3228`
(F-42 context), the completed P79, P231, P262, and P263 prompts, and these design files
under `D:/UO/cc-prompts/design/`: `f-exploit-achievements-accessory-slots.md` and
`accessory-slots-spec.md`. Implementation notes P79, P231, P262, P263, P355, P361,
and P366 explain earlier decisions and merge accommodations; current source decides status.

Chase's October 23 answers in `docs/feature-queue.md:2018` take precedence over older
League proposals. In particular:

- Publish no AP total or title count. Player guides may name visible titles and must keep
  hidden ones secret.
- Loot-box bands keep their existing grouping. Do not move them to the named chapter
  milestones merely because chapters use those milestones.
- The Renown shop, Hall page, and backpack purchases are delivered. Box placeholders do
  not mean the shop itself is missing.
- Warehouse and Featherweight rewards are dropped. Clockwork Scavenger remains planned;
  it is not an implemented box reward.

**Built** means the implementation and an ordinary entry point are present. **Partial**
means the table names both the implemented part and the remaining work. **Not built** means
the reviewed source and caller search did not provide the designed behavior. A test-only
override or a staff grant is not an ordinary entry point. **Deferred** identifies deliberate
later work rather than a failed phase-one requirement.

Sizes estimate remaining implementation work: **none** means no identified gap; **S** is
a local rule, hook, or content change; **M** touches several paths or needs focused
integration; **L** needs a subsystem or substantial progression/content work. They are
judgments, not measured schedules. Shared dependencies and estimates must not be added.

`ShatteredLegacy/server/patches/`, and `T/` is `ShatteredLegacy/server/tests/`.

# ServUO port list, rebuilt 2026-10-13

ServUO content still worth porting given the shard's current direction. Replaces the "Content ported by us"
area table and the 2026-09-19 "Content backlog" and "Deferred" sections on the status page. Machine-readable
twin: `port-list-2026-10-13.json` (same folder), which the unnumbered prompt `cc-P-port-list-target.md` turns into
`tools\port-list.json` and wires into `progress.py`.

## Why rebuild

- `tools\port-triage.json` is not a ServUO port list. It is the 2026-09-06 triage of our own `server/customizations`
  (111 files) for the migrate-to-ServUO plan that was not taken; `docs\port-triage.md` already says OBSOLETE. Nothing
  in it applies now. The prompt moves it to `tools\obsolete\`.
- The status page headline ("27% of candidate content ported, 551 of 2,045") divides by everything ServUO has that
  ModernUO lacks. The page itself says the pool "is not a target", and the direction note says the work moved from
  porting to building our own systems. A denominator full of PvP factions, a player bazaar and a paid store shell
  cannot tell Chase anything.
- The newest overseer passdown on disk is 2026-10-05. Since then: one character per account, two players, no
  donations, TazUO assistants kept, loot boxes, recipe gating, the Alembic guild, Artificers' imbuing (P82), Herbalism,
  WoW and Diablo creature art (renamed), extra facets as new maps. The list below is judged against that.

## Method (re-derive before trusting)

ServUO pub57 `Scripts\Services`, `Scripts\Items`, `Scripts\Mobiles`, listed 2026-10-13. A file is a candidate when its
stem is not in `tools\modernuo-stems.txt`; done when the stem exists anywhere under
`D:\ShatteredLegacy\server\customizations`. Same proxy as `progress.py` (filename stems, not behavior). Each candidate
lands in the first system whose path prefix matches. Every one of the 2,753 candidates lands somewhere; none is left
unassigned. `Quests\`, `Skills\` and `Spells\` are not scanned yet (Skill Masteries lives there; see Later).

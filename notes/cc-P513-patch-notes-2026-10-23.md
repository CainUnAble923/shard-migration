# October 23 patch notes

## Pets

- Animal trainers now sell elixirs of rebirth to bring dead bonded pets back to life.
- Rangers' League members can buy an elixir from their guildmaster at a discount.
- To use one, wait for your pet's body to fade, stand beside its ghost, and use the elixir from your backpack.

## Player guides

- The next wiki update adds guides for the Society of Smiths, Artificers' Order, Order of the Alembic, Miners' Compact, Foresters' Union, Rangers' League, Healers' Covenant, Custodians, and Thieves' Den.
- It also adds a new players' corner with an FAQ and glossary, crafting and High Seas fishing guides, a dungeon guide, and a guide to the Halloween event in Sorcerer's Dungeon.
- Existing guides for the League, achievements, loot boxes, traps, bulk orders, materials, mining, imbuing, and New Haven have been revised for that update.

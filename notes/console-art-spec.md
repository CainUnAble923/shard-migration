# Shard Console art spec (cc-P132)

For Chase, to hand to an AI image tool one piece at a time. The console (`D:\ShatteredLegacy\scripts\Shard-Console.ps1`,
the Enhanced look) draws a stand-in for every piece today; each file you drop into `D:\ShatteredLegacy\scripts\console-art\`
replaces its stand-in the next time the console opens. Nothing here was generated by the console or by the agent.

## The shared style (put this paragraph in front of every prompt)

> Ultima Online Enhanced Client interface art. Dark slate and basalt stone with a faint grain, gilded bronze and
> worn gold trim, carved runes and sigils as ornament, a cool low-key light from the upper left with one warm bronze
> accent. Palette: slate 40,42,47; near-black backing 14,15,18; bronze trim 220,178,98; parchment-pale text 228,222,210;
> glow colors: green 120,255,150, amber 255,200,90, red 255,110,100, grey 130,130,140, the Golem's bronze glow
> 255,196,96. Clean, legible, no text in the image, no photoreal metal, no lens flare. The background is transparent
> (alpha), not black: the piece sits on dark stone that varies, so leave nothing opaque outside the object. Centered,
> with a 2 px margin of transparency on every side.

Every piece: **PNG with transparency** (static), or for an animated piece a **horizontal sprite strip PNG** (all frames
the same width, side by side, frame 1 on the left) plus a line in `console-art\art.json`; an animated GIF is accepted
but second choice (its transparency is on or off, so it leaves a hard halo on the stone). Make each piece at the two
sizes listed (the small one is what the console shows at 100%; the double one keeps it crisp at 150% and for later
use), and save the small one under the slot's file name. Keep each file under its budget (KB).

Text contrast is not the art's job: every word the console draws sits on a plate, a backing or a page the console
colors itself, and the textures above are kept low-contrast so the words on them stay readable (the console checks the
lightest and darkest pixel it draws text over).

## How to drop them in

1. Save each file under its exact slot name in `D:\ShatteredLegacy\scripts\console-art\`.
2. For a sprite strip, add its frame count and speed to `scripts\console-art\art.json`:
   `{ "slots": { "crystal-green": { "frames": 12, "fps": 8 }, "golem-running": { "frames": 8, "fps": 6 } } }`.
   A GIF needs no entry.
3. Close and reopen the console (it reads the folder at start). The startup log (`D:\UO\logs\shard-console-startup.log`
   and the console's own `scripts\shard-console.log`) says `console-art: N of 20 slots have art` and names any file it
   refused and why (wrong type, over budget, unreadable, a strip whose width is not frames x height); a refused slot
   keeps its drawn stand-in, and nothing pops up.
4. P133 wires the spinning and glowing animations once the strips exist; until then the first frame of a strip is shown.

## Findings

1. **risk**, `server/customizations/Regions/RootdeepVault.cs:152` and `server/customizations/Mobiles/Burrows/VaultCreatures.cs:282`: the cleared flag prevents reentry but does not end tutorial eligibility, so a trainee who stays inside can repeatedly kill the respawning Overdraft for its pouch, Dawnstone ingot, bandages, and trinket. Separate reward eligibility from permission to remain long enough to loot and leave, and test a credited trainee killing a second Overdraft without crossing the doorway.

2. **bug**, `server/customizations/Regions/RootdeepVault.cs:182` and `:234`: credit seals the doorway immediately on the kill, before the player uses the crawlway, so reconnecting before looting ejects the player and blocks access to the wreckage; a credited Young player who dies before leaving likewise cannot return through the doorway to recover their own corpse. Track successful departure separately from boss credit, preserve unfinished loot and corpse recovery, and test reconnecting and dying between the kill and the crawl.

3. **bug**, `server/customizations/Regions/RootdeepVault.cs:163` and `:177`: boss credit resolves a pet's current `ControlMaster`, so a trainee whose only damage came from an unbonded pet that died before the boss loses credit when deletion clears that master, despite the damage entry retaining the original responsible player. Use the recorded responsible damage entries, with player deduplication and tutorial eligibility checks, and extend V3 to kill the attacking pet before the Overdraft.

4. **risk**, `server/customizations/Regions/RootdeepVault.cs:191`: the crawlway search is centered on the boss's death position with a radius of 24, so killing a boss lured into the reachable Deposit Hall at `(5143,1133)` misses the mouth at `(5160,1159)` and grants credit without opening the promised exit. Resolve the seeded crawlway at its canonical mouth independently of the corpse position, and test a kill there as well as in the Strongroom.

5. **bug**, `server/customizations/Mobiles/Burrows/VaultCreatures.cs:139` and `server/customizations/ClusterFRootdeepSeeder.cs:104`: the Hoarding Rat creates ordinary Penny Rats without spawner ownership, a tracked pack, or cleanup, so killing each respawning leader while leaving its pack alive accumulates extra rats beyond the intended pack size. Track and clean up the leader's reinforcements on death/deletion, and extend `server/tests/Mobiles/Burrows/BurrowsCreaturesVerification.cs:437` through multiple leader deaths instead of manually deleting the summoned rats before deleting the leader.

6. **risk**, `server/customizations/Mobiles/Burrows/WarrenCreatures.cs:162` and `:253`: the Burrow Mother's live brood list and lifetime counter are unsaved, while her spawned grubs are ordinary world mobiles, so saving and restarting during a fight loses ownership and resets both caps; surviving old grubs then escape her death cleanup and new grubs can stack on top. Persist the brood references and count, or explicitly reconcile surviving brood on load, and test a save/reload with live brood followed by another spawn attempt and the mother's death.

7. **risk**, `server/tests/Regions/RootdeepVaultVerification.cs:331` and `server/tests/Items/StarterResourcesVerification.cs:207`: the interior checks assert zone rectangles and force map readiness, while the Dawnstone test calls `MutateResource` directly, so these tests can pass without proving that players can walk the finished room connections or mine an actual Dawnstone target. Add an applied-map interior route check covering the Vault gaps, boss rooms, crawlway exit, and return route, plus real harvesting on a reachable mineable tile with shared-bank depletion and an outside-region control.

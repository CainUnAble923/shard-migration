## Questions and recommended answers

These are recommendations for review, not newly accepted decisions. The IDs match P260's For Chase list; P392 carries them as U47-U60.

| Question | Recommended answer and reason |
|---|---|
| Q1. May reagent and decorative plants cross? | No. Keep separate pollen lanes and average only inside the reagent ladder; stock enum averages would otherwise select unrelated plants. |
| Q2. Is strain tier separate from plant hue? | Yes. Save strain separately and derive its displayed metal hue; keep stock color semantics for dyes, baskets, and decorative breeding. |
| Q3. May reagent plants yield indefinitely while tended? | Yes. Set yield by type, strain, and skill and measure the bulk targets. Leave stock lifetime limits intact. |
| Q4. Do beds regain upkeep? | Reagent beds get slow upkeep and explicit grace. Stock raised beds retain their current no-upkeep behavior. This is narrower than changing every P128 mound. |
| Q5. How does the skill ship, and is it selectable at creation? | Extend P239's existing skill-file override writer, handle ungrouped skills for both new and saved profiles, and allow a starter skill only when its useful starter kit is included. |
| Q6. Which skill qualifies an Alembic promotion? | The higher base value of Alchemy and Herbalism. The general best-owned-skill calculation is already built; add Herbalism to the appropriate ownership path. |
| Q7. What happens to stock reagent pickups? | Keep them and add herb patches. P260 proposed replacing them, but no accepted replacement decision was found. This recommendation matches P392 U53 and preserves existing content. |
| Q8. When is graveyard digging built? | After the first working farm/foraging release, as a separate new gathering action. Current vendors and creature drops cover the interim; future League hunts are not a built substitute. |
| Q9. What happens to the familiar on house collapse? | Preserve a deed in durable owner claims storage. Do not rely on a house moving crate that may disappear with the house. Define transfer and redeeding in the same prompt. |
| Q10. How are yard items counted? | A separate small house-size budget, with normal storage rules on containers. Keep the limit visible and enforce it during placement and reload. |
| Q11. What survives plot expiry? | Warn first, end tenancy and crops, and recover owned items through the bank or durable claims storage with an overflow path. Do not silently delete kit or bins. |
| Q12. Should growth use a minute sweep? | Yes for reagent scheduling, with persisted due times and tests for restart and duplicate sweeps. Preserve stock bowl pace and behavior. |
| Q13. Which Blood Moss plant art ships first? | Reuse a stock fern. It avoids a new art dependency; later custom art goes through Chase's request/approval workflow. No art was generated or requested for this research task. |
| Q14. Where do players get the bed deed? | A Carpentry recipe through the Foresters. Alembic handles seeds and farm consumables; both guilds then have useful roles. |

## What to change in the next build prompt

P260 remains a useful architecture survey, but its phase-one draft should not be issued unchanged.

- The skill-file writer is now present. Extend `Build-Skills` instead of creating a second writer. It currently renames populated records and skips empty ones, so adding Herbalism is still real work. `0004-world-map-fog-of-war.patch` and `0005-ui-skins-appearance.patch` already occupy the numbers after P260's listed client patches.
- Multi-skill promotion is already generic. The missing work is ownership and policy wiring, not a new promotion algorithm. Keep `GatedSkillsOf` consistent with whether Herbalism processing actually has a craft menu.
- Account data is now `CurrentVersion = 22` at `C/ClusterFAccountData.cs:503`, not P260's 20. Do not reuse its proposed v21 for herb discoveries. Select the next version when implementing against the then-current tree.
- Loop recording has advanced since P260: graduation records a real loop and grants its reward. Skill resets and peak memory still have no implementation. Neither claim should be broadened into saying all loop work is absent or all of F-12 is built.
- P260's phrase that `PlantResources` has four rows describes its output kinds, not its actual mappings. Read its type/hue entries directly; none yields a reagent. Fertile Dirt on beds is already implemented and does not need another port.
- Include seed-box and seed-stack identity when adding strain state. The current box merges type/hue matches without a strain key (`C/Items/Functional/SeedBox/SeedBox.cs:331`). A new strain field alone is insufficient to preserve distinct seeds.
- Carry forward the mound-emptying gap from P260. The existing bed tests do not exercise the emptying gump; a new regression should be seen failing before its fix. This task records the source mismatch and changes no code.
- Do not call player-only plant actions with the familiar as though it were a co-owner. `RaisedGardenPlantItem.IsUsableBy` checks the acting mobile against house ownership. Provide an owner-authorized worker path with equivalent house/plot checks.
- The town farm needs its own surveyed site. P260 points to New Haven's Alembic post; current F-49 placement data also has a test-hall site in Papua. Neither source defines public plots or proves world placement.
- Give the complete design a home in the phase plan: bundle processing, bed-wide tending, seed vault storage, seed sources, and post-grace strain loss need explicit acceptance checks. They should not disappear behind the words reagent plants or familiar.

Recommended order is the server skill and client entry (M), reagent plants/breeding/yield (L), then reagent-bed scheduling/care (M) and wild foraging/ledger (M). Garden kit (L) and yard/public plots (L) follow; split yards and tenancy into separate prompts. The familiar and automation (L) follow working yards, care, and bins. Higher-skill herb content, small side-use recipes, and harvest contracts can then arrive separately. F-12 memory stays in its own workstream. No completion percentage is assigned: the reusable foundations and missing game systems are not equal units of work.

## Findings

1. **bug: Generated dining deeds can be impossible to fill.** `server/customizations/Services/BulkOrders/CraftBOD.cs:371`, `LargeTinkerBOD.cs:52`, and `CraftBulkEntries.cs:664`: the first dining entry, ButcherKnife, permits exceptional and metal requirements for the whole list, but SpoonLeft, ForkLeft, KnifeLeft and Plate have neither property, so their small deeds cannot satisfy those large deeds; the bribe checks in `BulkOrderSystem.cs:379` and `:390` make the same first-entry assumption. Require every entry, including its makeable twin, to support each requirement before rolling or bribing it, and add a test that fills generated dining deeds with actual crafted items instead of prefilled small deeds.

2. **bug: Turning in a deed does not restore an offer in the 2016 cache.** `server/customizations/Services/BulkOrders/BulkOrderVendors.cs:291` and `:366`: a player who exhausts a craft's offers and completes an order still waits six hours, because the new context is never credited; the six town vendors inherit an empty successful-receive hook, pinned smith/tailor hooks clear only their old timers, and non-shopkeeper guild masters call `TurnIn` without that hook. Replenish the matching craft in the shared successful turn-in path, following ServUO `Scripts/Services/BulkOrders/BulkOrderSystem.cs:224` and `Scripts/Mobiles/PlayerMobile.cs:4152`, and test exhausted caches at both town and guild givers against the [official caching rule](https://uo.com/wiki/ultima-online-wiki/gameplay/crafting/bulk-orders/).

3. **bug: Resuming a large deed's pending reward loses most of its bank value.** `server/customizations/Services/BulkOrders/BulkOrderVendors.cs:167` and `BulkOrderSystem.cs:719`: Claim Rewards always offers 2%, while the original large-deed turn-in offers 20%, so a 210-point large order banks 4.2 instead of 42 after reopening the choice or reconnecting; this defect is also present in ServUO `Scripts/Mobiles/NPCs/BaseVendor.cs:292`. Persist the pending bank value or deed size alongside its points, restore the correct amount when reopening, and add a large-deed close/reopen and save/load test.

4. **bug: The Foresters and Alembic masters still redeem orders while bulk orders are disabled.** `server/customizations/Services/BulkOrders/BulkOrderGivers.cs:139` and `:116`: their drop handler checks the expansion but ignores `ContentFeatureFlags.BulkOrders`, and an already-open craft-choice gump also reaches `Act` after that flag is turned off. Apply the feature flag to both paths and test a filled deed and an outstanding choice after disabling the feature, matching pinned `Mobiles/Vendors/BaseVendor.cs:1089`.

5. **risk: The cooking apron improves unrelated crafts.** `server/customizations/Services/BulkOrders/Items/MasterChefsApron.cs:38` and `server/patches/CraftItem-chefs-apron.patch:6`: the helper supplies its bonus to every craft, including blacksmithing, and `server/tests/Services/BulkOrders/BulkOrderCraftsVerification.cs:762` explicitly expects that behavior, while the [official cooking reward description](https://uo.com/wiki/ultima-online-wiki/skills/cooking/cooking-bulk-orders/) limits the bonus to cooking. Confirm the historical rule and restrict the call by `CraftSystem.MainSkill == SkillName.Cooking`, or record an approved deviation, then replace the blacksmith positive assertion with a negative one and verify cooking separately.

6. **risk: Some point tables disagree with the official tables.** `server/customizations/Services/BulkOrders/CraftRewardCalculators.cs:215` and `:306` copy ServUO's bonuses of 300 for wood furniture and 250 for composite longbows, so normal wood orders of 10 pay 310 and 260 points, while the official [carpentry](https://uo.com/wiki/ultima-online-wiki/skills/carpentry/carpentry-bulk-orders/) and [bowcraft](https://uo.com/wiki/ultima-online-wiki/skills/bowcraft-and-fletching/bowcraft-bulk-orders/) tables give 210 and 360 respectively. Reconcile these values with a dated OSI reference, correct or document them, and add explicit point expectations for both families.

7. **risk: Reward prices also carry unexplained differences from the official tables.** `server/customizations/Services/BulkOrders/BODRewardCollections.cs:208`, `:177`, `:263`, `:115`, `:124`, `:128`, and `:131` list the Shadow Iron mallet at 450, Mechanic title at 225, barrel at 625, and smith maps at 600/850/950/1050, while the official [carpentry](https://uo.com/wiki/ultima-online-wiki/skills/carpentry/carpentry-bulk-orders/), [tinkering](https://uo.com/wiki/ultima-online-wiki/skills/tinkering/tinker-bulk-orders/), [cooking](https://uo.com/wiki/ultima-online-wiki/skills/cooking/cooking-bulk-orders/), and [smith](https://uo.com/wiki/ultima-online-wiki/skills/blacksmithing/blacksmith-bulk-orders/) tables list 500, 325, 750, and 700/950/1050/1150; the mallet is purchasable now, while the other listed rows remain placeholders. Check a dated OSI table before changing prices, record any deliberate differences, and pin the resolved prices in tests before enabling the placeholders.

8. **risk: Jewelry offers grow a permanent cache.** `server/customizations/Services/BulkOrders/LargeTinkerBOD.cs:47` creates a fresh gem-specific array for each jewelry offer, and `CraftBOD.cs:352` retains that array and its entries forever as a dictionary key even after the offered deed is rejected or deleted. Cache filtering before adding gem labels, or use a bounded key based on the original list and gem, so repeated offers reuse the same entries.

9. **risk: The reward-list test cannot catch changed prices.** `server/tests/Services/BulkOrders/BulkOrderCraftsVerification.cs:745` compares the sorted price sequence with itself, and the remaining assertions check counts, construction and selected placeholders, so a wrong price, hue or reward mapping still passes. Replace the self-comparison with independent expected tuples for each craft, including price, identity, hue and charges, and show each new assertion failing against its intended defect first.

10. **risk: The new book tests do not exercise withdrawal or sale transactions.** `server/tests/Services/BulkOrders/BulkOrderCraftsVerification.cs:497` and `:501` call `Reconstruct` directly, so they never check that withdrawing or buying one of the new deeds removes exactly one entry, rejects a stale second response, or preserves a large jewelry deed's gem through the actual transaction. Add those cases through `BOBGump.OnResponse` and `BODBuyGump.OnResponse`, plus an outstanding combine target after moving a deed into trade, with every new test seen failing first.

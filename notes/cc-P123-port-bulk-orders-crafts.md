## Deviations and decisions for Chase

1. **No exceptional or material a deed's item cannot carry (a change from ServUO, argued).** ServUO reads exceptional and
   material through `IQuality` and `IResource`, which ServUO's food, furniture and tinker parts implement. Pinned's do not:
   on this shard only weapons, armour, clothing, instruments, tools, books and quivers have a quality, and only weapons,
   armour, clothing and jewellery a resource. ServUO's rolls would ask for exceptional cooking (82.5% of large cooking
   deeds) and wooden furniture that no craft can make. So a deed asks for exceptional only of a type with a quality, and a
   material only of a type that records one (`CraftBOD.CanBeExceptional` / `CanUseMaterial`, also in the bribe rules).
   Cost: cooking deeds never earn the +200 exceptional points, and most furniture deeds never earn wood points. Porting
   quality onto food and furniture is its own job (it touches the craft system), which I recommend as a follow-up if Chase
   wants the full cooking economy.
2. **Gold-table typos corrected (`fix`, fidelity principle).** ServUO's fletching, carpentry and tinkering gold tables have
   cells with a slipped digit (60000 for 6000; 1250, 1350, 1750 for 12500, 13500, 17500), each marked "ServUO:" in
   `CraftRewardCalculators.cs`. A 5-part regular yew fletching deed of 10 would pay sixty thousand. For the deviation
   register (agents do not edit `docs/`).
3. **Large lists keep only what the craft can make.** Pinned's craft systems make no magical shortbow, elven composite
   longbow, necromancer spellbook, parasitic or darkglow potion, and no plain spoon, fork or knife (the left-hand ones fill
   those entries, as ServUO's tinker table says). A large deed fills only from small deeds and no small deed is offered for
   an item nobody can make, so those lines are left off their large deeds (`CraftBOD.Convert`). ServUO's gold tables have no
   2-part row; a list shortened to 2 pays the 3-part row.
4. **Bakers give cooking deeds** (P118's table, Chase's answer); ServUO's Baker gives none.
5. **The non-shopkeeper guild masters** (Foresters, Alembic) offer, take and claim rewards but take no bribe (ServUO's bribe
   is a shopkeeper's and its state is kept per vendor). The Foresters' master asks "Carpentry or Fletching" when a member
   has both skills.
6. **Shopkeeper guild masters show Bulk Order Info** although they do not sell (the shard's guild masters have no shop);
   ServUO's smith guildmaster turns its shop on instead. This also fixes P122's Tailors' Circle guild master, whose members
   had no way to ask in game (P122-3's last step).
7. **The book filter's numbers** keep pinned's 0..16 for saved filters (above); the smith metal filters now name their craft.
8. **Kept from ServUO as they are:** the wood deed lines are EA's bare "Oak", "Ash", ... (there is no "made with oak" line
   in the client); ServUO's carpentry and tinkering gold index reads one row short of its labels; skill is the skill's Base,
   as P122's rolls read it; ServUO's graphic hues in the deed gump are not carried (pinned's gumps draw none).

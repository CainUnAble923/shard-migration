# League Chapter 4: The Last Morning

**Draft for Chase to edit. P616, October 25, 2026.**

Blaze Citizen trial. Blade piece: **the Blade**. Place: **a private trial copy of Ice Dungeon**. Chapter question: **What happens if the loop breaks for good?**

This is a playable story proposal based on [the League storyline](../docs/league-storyline.md), with the voice and continuity of [Chapter 2](cc-P593-league-chapter-2-draft.md) and [Chapter 3](cc-P601-league-chapter-3-draft.md). New names, dialogue, scenes, encounters, timing, and reward handling are drafts for Chase to edit. They are not implemented features or approved canon. Proposed length: about 35 to 45 minutes, solo or with a party. Exact placement, difficulty, effects, and trial support need implementation review and a client walkthrough.

## What the player takes away

The League is not the only group that understands the loop. Some former members have decided that no one should live inside it, even if breaking free ends Britannia. They call themselves the Unbound. Their leader once did the work the Registrar now asks of the player.

The Blade shows a world where the loop has ended. It is quiet because there is nobody left to make a sound. The player brings that knowledge back along with the fourth piece.

Recovering the sword no longer means simply repairing what broke. The player now has a reason to ask what should be done with it. The permanent Mend or Sunder choice belongs to Chapter 5. No conversation here makes that choice, joins a faction, or awards its lasting effects.

## Setting and cast

Ice Dungeon begins with noise: boots scraping, tools striking, water running beneath the frozen floor. The Unbound have entered ahead of the player. Their lamps mark a route toward a black shape in the deepest ice.

Use a short route through existing dungeon space: an entry shelter, a cut through the ice, a broken crossing, and the deep chamber. These are staging labels, not verified map locations. The vision uses a small, empty copy of a familiar New Haven street and the League field office. Stock scenery, human NPCs, lamps, tools, ice, and existing effects should carry the chapter. No new art is required by this draft.

**Elara Voss, the League Registrar.** Practical as before, but this report is personal. She knew the Unbound leader when he was a League champion. She does not conceal that connection or pretend he was always a villain.

**Calder Rusk, former League champion and leader of this Unbound expedition.** Proposed new NPC. He wears a field coat with the League badge carefully unstitched. He checks other people's lamp wicks before his own. He still helps people; he has stopped believing that helping them back onto the same road is enough.

**Nell, an Unbound cutter.** Proposed new NPC. She has marked each tool handle so nobody can mistake her kit for theirs. She believes Calder, and understands what destroying the pieces might cost. She gives the player someone to meet besides a faction leader and armed guards.

**The other Unbound.** Former League members carrying practical expedition equipment. They are organized and dangerous. Their objective is to get the Blade out and destroy it, not to win a dungeon race for sport.

**The Blade.** A long section of black metal frozen upright below the chamber floor. Both ends are broken. This is the sword's fourth piece, not a complete weapon; its Point remains missing. Near it, the sound of running water stops before the water does.

## Beat 1: A name Elara knows

Offer this as the Blaze chapter after Chapter 3. Characters already at or beyond Blaze can return for the story and rewards under the existing waiver decision. The chapter does not replace a promotion job or add a requirement to ranks already earned.

At the field office, Elara has set a letter beside the recovered pieces. The Crossguard turns toward it.

**Elara:** "A supply clerk brought this in. Lamp oil, rope, cutting tools. Enough for an expedition into Ice Dungeon. The buyer used an old League account."

**Player:** "Someone looking for the next piece?"

**Elara:** "Calder Rusk. He used to bring people back from places we had given up searching. I knew his handwriting before I read the name."

The letter is brief:

> Elara,
>
> Close the account after this. We will not need supplies for a return journey.
>
> Calder

**Player:** "He does not expect to come back."

**Elara:** "He expects there to be nothing to come back to. He calls his people the Unbound. They were League once. They know what the pieces do. They mean to destroy them."

**Player:** "Even if the world ends with them?"

**Elara:** "Yes. He believes keeping it here is worse."

Optional question, **"What made him leave?"**

**Elara:** "We brought a village through a bad winter. Food, medicine, every guild that could spare a hand. Calder stayed until the last roof was sound. Later he found the same people waiting for the same carts. They thanked him for coming so quickly. None of them remembered him."

**Player:** "And you asked him to help again."

**Elara:** "Yes."

She folds the letter along its old crease.

**Elara:** "Reach the Blade before he breaks it. If he will speak, listen. I would rather hear his reasons than make them up for him."

Accept text:

> Follow the pieces into Ice Dungeon. Reach the Blade before the Unbound destroy it. You may bring your party.

A League trial marker at the real entrance offers entry to the private copy. The Unbound expedition, altered passages, and recovered piece exist only inside this trial.

## Beat 2: Someone has gone ahead

At the entry shelter, the player finds a lamp, empty supply sacks, and a strip of cloth bearing the outline of a removed League badge. A chalk arrow points down the passage.

Inspection:

> The lamp has been trimmed and filled. Someone left it where the next person would see the turn.

Farther in, Nell kneels beside a broken tool. Calder stands beyond a narrow ice shelf with the rest of the expedition.

**Nell:** "The last chisel. I told you that seam was stone."

Calder looks up as the party arrives. The three recovered pieces answer the Blade with a low sound.

**Calder:** "Elara found someone else."

**Player:** "She said you would know why we came."

**Calder:** "Then she has started telling the truth sooner. That is something."

**Player:** "You mean to break it."

**Calder:** "It has had long enough to let us go."

He gives Nell his lamp and sends her through with the others.

**Calder:** "You have seen what it takes. Ask yourself what you are carrying it home for."

The Unbound close a worked barrier across their cut through the ice. Their lamps remain visible beyond it. A nearby side passage offers the player's route.

Journal update:

> The Unbound are cutting toward the deep chamber. Follow the side passage and find a way ahead of them.

## Beat 3: A race through the ice

The race is staged through visible expedition progress, not a hidden countdown. Calder's people move after the player completes marked objectives. Reading, accessibility needs, defeat, and a lost connection cannot silently destroy the Blade or fail the chapter. The fiction stays urgent because the player sees the Unbound getting closer at each crossing.

**First crossing: the cut.** A short encounter with trial-only ice creatures blocks the side passage. Use existing creature appearances, with the actual roster and Blaze solo balance chosen later. Afterward, any character can lower a loose ice shelf to make a crossing. This is an explicit interaction, not a Mining or Strength check.

Across the gap, the Unbound free their cutting frame from its runners and carry it by hand. Calder takes the heavier end when Nell cannot lift it. The player reaches the lower passage as their lamps disappear above.

**Second crossing: the fallen pack.** The two routes meet at a broken ledge. Nell has slipped and is holding a rope. Her marked tools lie out of reach. Calder braces the other end across the gap.

**Calder:** "There is a cleat beside you. Put the rope over it."

Selecting **"Secure the rope"** lets Nell climb onto the ledge. The interaction is the route forward, available to any solo character. There is no timed refusal, morality score, or hidden faction commitment.

**Nell:** "Leave the tools. I can make the chamber without them."

**Player:** "You know what he means to do there."

**Nell:** "Yes. He told us before we came."

Calder waits until she is standing.

**Player:** "You would end her world too."

**Calder:** "She knows. So do I."

Nell picks up the lamp the player passes back to her. The Unbound continue through the upper route. Their cutting frame catches on the ledge; the player gains a shorter way into the deep chamber while they free it.

**Final approach.** One short fight guards the player's descent. Beyond it, the Blade is visible in the floor. The player reaches its exposed upper break first. Calder's expedition enters from the other side as the ice begins to answer the recovered pieces.

No special guild, rare material, simultaneous party action, or death is required to win the approach. The competition brings both groups to the same moment with different intentions.

## Beat 4: The fourth piece

The Blade's upper edge shows through a crack in the ice. Calder's people set down the cutting frame. He approaches without drawing a weapon.

**Calder:** "Set the others beside it. See what it has kept from us."

**Player:** "You have not seen it either."

**Calder:** "I have seen enough of what we have now."

Ready prompt:

> Touch the Blade with the recovered pieces nearby. Your party will enter a story vision and return here afterward. This does not reset your character or decide the sword's fate.

The player's recorded recovery of the earlier pieces should allow the scene even if a loose keepsake was lost. Do not require surrendering or consuming those items.

Selecting **"Touch the Blade"** brings frost across the screen's edges, supported by text rather than an effect alone.

> The water stops. Your breath hangs in front of you. Then even that is gone.

Calder reaches toward the Blade as the scene changes. He witnesses the same vision, giving him something specific to answer afterward.

## Beat 5: The last morning

The party stands on a familiar New Haven street. Calder is a short distance away. The ice and expedition are gone.

The doors are open. There are no voices, animals, footsteps, or forge sounds. Nothing comes to attack. Let the player walk before presenting the first interaction.

Journal text establishes what the Blade is showing:

> Britannia after its final return. The loop has ended. No one is coming back.

This is the chapter's required vision of an unlooped world that ended, not a second reality the player can loot or settle. It does not establish that Chapter 5's Sunder outcome must destroy Britannia. The player has seen the cost of the loop ending outright; they have not been shown every possible future or the result of a choice they have not made.

Use a brief route with ordinary objects, echoing the human details in Despise and Doom.

**The market table.** A bowl sits under the edge of a cloth. There is no food left in it, only dust. Beside it lies a folded list.

Readable list:

> Oil. Flour. Thread. Ask whether the blue cloth has come in.

**Calder:** "They expected another day."

**Player:** "So did everyone we found in Doom."

He stays at the table for a moment.

**The cold workshop.** A chair has been repaired with a piece of mismatched wood. A needle is still pushed through the cuff of a coat.

Inspection:

> Someone had nearly finished. There is enough thread for the last few stitches.

**The field office.** Elara's place is empty. The register lies open. Its last entry is unfinished. Do not show a dead Elara, fabricate a personal death for the player's character, or turn this into a prophecy about a named NPC.

At her desk, select **"Wait for the next return"**.

Hold a short pause that can be advanced, with no precision timing or long forced wait. The light stays where it is. The register does not change. Nobody resumes an opening line.

> Nothing begins again.

**Calder:** "I thought I would be glad to see it quiet."

**Player:** "Are you?"

**Calder:** "I do not know."

He touches the back of Elara's empty chair.

**Calder:** "I remember her staying late to write the names we brought back. She would not leave one for the morning."

**Player:** "You can still speak to her."

**Calder:** "In the world we came from."

All three stops receive journal marks. No combat interrupts the silence. No specter explains the vision or reveals the Point, Exodus's plan, or the Setting.

Objective:

> Return to the Blade with what you have seen.

The black shape of the Blade appears in the office doorway. Selecting **"Return to the ice"** brings everyone back to the deep chamber. Any participant can review the vision through the journal later. A party should not be forced to wait for every member to read every optional line before leaving.

## Beat 6: What he will do with it

Water runs beneath the floor again. Nell asks Calder what happened. He answers her before addressing the player.

**Calder:** "It showed us an end. Empty houses. Nobody waiting for the next turn."

**Nell:** "And the people?"

**Calder:** "Gone."

**Player:** "You would still break it?"

**Calder:** "I have watched people die who had already died in my arms. I will not call that mercy because it happens twice. Or a hundred times. Yes. I would rather it end once."

**Player:** "You saw what we would lose."

**Calder:** "I saw. I am not asking you to pretend it costs nothing."

He orders the frame brought forward. This is where his conviction becomes an immediate threat. The player must stop him from striking the Blade, without being asked to endorse the loop forever.

Player response:

**Player:** "We have not found the whole sword. You are asking everyone to pay before we know what else can be done."

**Calder:** "And you are asking them to wait."

Ready prompt:

> Stop the Unbound cutting frame and recover the Blade. This confrontation does not choose Mend or Sunder.

Fight proposal: Calder and a small guard hold the approach while the frame prepares a strike. Calder uses an existing human appearance and readable weapon attacks. The encounter is sized later for a Blaze solo character, with party scaling. Nobody must defeat a stock boss or keep a friendly healer alive.

At two clear stages, the frame's operator withdraws and a marked brace becomes usable. Calder pauses his attacks while any character releases it. The first interaction lowers the arm; the second removes the striking head. Use plain objective text and an effect so the machine's status is readable.

> The arm is raised. Release the marked brace to lower it.

These stages are paced interactions, not a real deadline. Missing the prompt leaves the encounter at that stage with a readable objective. It does not destroy the quest piece, kill the party, or force a chapter restart. No specialist skill or simultaneous pair of switches is required.

End combat when Calder is defeated and the frame is disabled. His defeat is nonfatal. His guards retreat; Nell helps him stand. The player has stopped this attempt to destroy the piece, without a forced execution or a false promise that the Unbound have abandoned their cause.

**Calder:** "You have bought more time. Be careful what you spend it on."

**Player:** "Elara will hear what you saw. And what you said."

**Calder:** "Then give her my words. All of them."

He leaves with the expedition. Nell takes the damaged tools. Calder takes the lamp. Keep his survival and retreat as visible scene outcomes, not an unexplained disappearance at zero health.

## Beat 7: Lift it clear

With the frame disabled, the earlier pieces pull toward the Blade. The ice parts along its length. Select **"Recover the Blade"** to lift it out and record personal recovery for eligible participants.

Inspection:

> The metal is cold enough to hurt. For a moment you see the empty street along its edge. Then your reflection moves, and you hear the others breathing.

The Grip, Crossguard, and Pommel fit beside it, with the Point still absent. Do not assemble, reforge, or destroy the sword here.

Journal update:

> The Blade showed a world with no return. The Unbound would accept that end. Carry the fourth piece and your account back to Elara.

The return route is clear. The sound of the dungeon comes back fully: water, shifting ice, boots on stone. There is no required story rewind in this chapter. Its contrast with Despise and Doom is the terrible absence of one.

Offer a safe return to the real Ice Dungeon entrance. Neither the Unbound's retreat nor the Blade's removal changes the public dungeon or another party's trial.

## Beat 8: Leave the page open

At the field office, Elara listens to the full account before reaching for the register.

**Elara:** "Calder saw it too?"

**Player:** "He said he thought he would be glad to hear it quiet. He still meant to break the piece."

**Elara:** "Then I cannot say he does not understand."

**Player:** "Do you?"

**Elara:** "I understand the people who were waiting for our carts. I remember how pleased they were to see us. Calder remembers that they had forgotten. Both things happened."

The player lays the Blade beside the other pieces. Elara moves the letter away so the metal does not cover it.

Optional question, **"Is that what happens if we destroy the sword?"**

**Elara:** "You saw a world where the loop had ended. We need to take that seriously. You did not see every way this sword might be broken or repaired. I cannot promise they all lead to the same place."

**Player:** "You asked me to bring back what broke time. What will you ask me to do with it?"

**Elara:** "I do not have that answer yet. When we have the last piece, I will not pretend this was only a repair job."

She writes Calder's name and leaves space beneath it.

**Elara:** "For now, you came back. You brought the piece. And you heard someone I had stopped hearing. That belongs in the report too."

End with ordinary noise outside the office. Someone is calling for a delivery. A door closes. The register stays open.

## Reward and completion handling, proposed

- **The Blade:** a unique story keepsake and the fourth recovered piece. Suggested item text: "Lifted from the deepest ice. Along its edge, an empty street waits for a morning that will not come." No combat stats or completed weapon in this chapter.
- **Chapter journal:** Calder's letter, the Unbound's stated purpose, and the vision's three stops remain readable. Replaying a journal scene changes no character progression or faction standing.
- **Chapter completion:** record Chapter 4 after personal Blade recovery and the report to Elara. Apply the Blaze trial requirement where applicable, retaining the separate promotion-job, standing, and Renown rules and the existing chapter waiver.
- **Ownership:** each eligible participant earns personal recovery and completion credit, with one earned Blade per character. Helpers can accompany friends without duplicate piece rewards. No last-hit, first-click, or shared-loot race.
- **Other payouts:** leave XP, Renown, and gold amounts to the implementation prompt. No Keeper or Breaker title, cosmetic, skill floor, echo bonus, or faction membership is awarded here.

Follow the earlier drafts' proposal for a durable recovery record and replaceable keepsake. A full pack leaves collection available from Elara; a lost item does not erase earned story progress or block later chapters. The question of League custody versus personal originals remains open for Chase's edit across all chapters.

## Retry and party notes for the eventual build

Objectives stay explicit: follow the expedition, secure Nell's rope, reach the Blade, witness the empty world, stop the cutting frame, recover the piece, report to Elara. Required conversations can be revisited. A player can disagree with Calder in optional dialogue without changing eligibility or deciding the later permanent choice.

The race advances at story milestones. Actual elapsed time, death, and disconnects do not let the Unbound win offscreen. A failed fight restarts that encounter without replaying completed conversations or the vision within a continuing trial. Calder and Nell cannot be accidentally killed before their scenes. Reset encounters should not award farming loot.

Keep the vision safe and short, with a clear return interaction and text supporting every important effect. Party members travel together; journal entries preserve the essential scenes for anyone who missed a line. No player must sacrifice an item, choose a class, or wait alone in a separate room. Exact participation range, scaling, scene advancement, and regrouping rules belong to the future build spec.

On abandonment, disconnect, or trial closure, return characters safely to the real entrance. Preserve durable recovery and completion records even if the player disconnects before reporting. An expired unfinished trial may restage scenes on reentry. Characters earning their own chapter credit follow Chapters 1 through 3 first; friends at other stages may help.

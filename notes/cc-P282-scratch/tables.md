# P282 generated XP tables, 2026-10-21

Research assumptions, not measured gameplay. C=24; exponent=1.5; XP/kill=5*creature level.
Steady=120 kills/h at own level; cautious=60 kills/h at 75% of own level (floor 1).
No global loop bonus. Echo=min(10,1+0.5*loop), below previous peak only.
Hours are active XP-earning hours; calendar days divide hours by 1, 3 or 6.
Tables exclude scroll acquisition delays, travel, training downtime and noncombat XP.
Main tables enter each loop at level 1 with an empty reserve; cumulative includes loop 0.
Band A->B includes transitions A through B-1. At echo E divide base band time by E
only below the old peak; bands.csv gives every reachable band in every loop/style.
Full-bank tables charge capped farming to the arriving loop, before its reset/re-climb.
The final arrival at 200 includes no outgoing bank. No echo on bank fill or release.

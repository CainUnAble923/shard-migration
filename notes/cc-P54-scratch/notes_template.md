### A.2 The model

Script: `cc-P54-scratch\xp_ladder.py` (deterministic; a port of cc-P20's `xp_curve.py`). Inputs as decided: the
ladder 50, 100, then +5 to 200 at loop 21; level resets to 1 every loop; the base echo x1.5 at loop 1, +0.5 per loop,
x10 from loop 18; the reserve. Modeling choices, mine:
- "Levels already reached" in loop N = levels below the previous loop's cap.
- P20's +20 percent XP per loop is dropped: the echo replaces it.
- **Reserve:** filled at max level at the base rate, and credited as raw XP from level 1 at the start of the next loop
  (not multiplied by the echo again). Main rows assume **no** reserve credit (a player who resets as soon as they hold
  the scroll); "w/ res" shows the loop if the last reserve was full.
- **Profiles.** A: 120 kills an hour at own level. B: 60 kills an hour at 75 percent of own level. XP per kill = 5 x
  creature level (P20).
- **New profile C (crafter/gatherer):** 720 actions an hour (one every 5 s, P20's use rate), 0.5 x level XP per craft
  or harvest at its own level, plus XP per skill point gained (A.2c). Hours scale exactly: B takes 2.67x A's hours,
  C 1.67x before its skill pool.
- **Gain rates for C.** "test" = the test shard (`Configuration-test\modernuo.json:114-116, :187-188`: ClusterF 3
  attempts, x5 amount, Test Center GainFactor x5). "live" = the live config (`Configuration\modernuo.json:107-109`, Test
  Center off). "plain" = pinned alone. The skill pool is bounded (each skill only climbs to the cap), so the rate
  changes how fast the pool is spent, not its size.

### A.5 Edge checks

{{GEN:Edge checks: the reserve}}
1. **The reserve cannot carry a character past a step it has no scroll for.**
   - It is only credited after a reset, and the reset is what needs the scroll.
   - It is 25 percent of the XP of the next loop's new levels, so even spent from level 1 it can never reach the new
     cap. The table shows the most it covers: levels 1 to 53 at loop 1, 1 to 25-38 later. Since it can never reach
     the cap, nothing needs clamping.
   - **One gap:** at loop 21 there is no next cap, so 25 percent of nothing is 0, and a capstone character has no
     reserve. Post-capstone loops (F-12: allowed, cosmetic only) then bank nothing. Decision 5.
2. **XP at max level with a full reserve.** Proposed: **lost, never refused.** The kill or craft still happens and
   pays its loot, currency and skill gain; only the XP is dropped.
   - The player sees one message when the reserve fills, in the System's voice ("Your reserve is full. Further
     experience is lost until you loop."), and a reminder at most once per 30 minutes of play while full.
   - The paperdoll or stat display (F-12's chosen place) shows "Reserve: full".
   - Do not convert overflow into Renown or anything else: that is the double pay A.1 rules out.
3. **The echo cannot make a level re-climb free.** At the x10 top the loop-21 re-climb still costs a tenth of the raw
   XP: 844k earned XP against 553k for the five new levels (S1), and 12.3 of 16.6 h of loop 21 in the S2 default. So level paces
   every loop, as F-12 (2026-10-03) requires. **Skills are different:** Part C shows the Keeper skill echo is instant
   at live rates. If skill points pay XP, a Keeper's instant skill re-climb would also become level XP; hence the rule
   in headline 5 (skill XP only above the previous peak).

### B.2 The shared hook G (ours only, `C\ClusterFSkillGain.cs`)

`ClusterFSkillGain` already sits between every gain call and pinned (`:36-47`), and sees skill, target, min and max.
The thief/healer and animal/bard recounts proposed this independently; I checked the API they rely on.

**When it rolls.** All five conditions must hold:
- the caller is a player;
- Value >= the call's stock max (pinned "no challenge", `SkillCheck.cs:67`, `:189`);
- Base < Cap;
- the call matches a row in a table;
- the row's floors pass.

**What it does.**
- Returns the stock result unchanged, then calls the public `SkillCheck.CheckSkill(from, skill, amObj, chance)`
  (`:108`) with chance = (Value - min) / (maxEff - min).
- `maxEff = Cap + (Value - Base)`, so worn bonuses do not cut it short.
- For location rows, amObj is the same `Point2D` pinned builds (`SkillCheck.cs:72`), so anti-macro still applies.
- Calling `CheckSkill` directly, not the base handler, keeps `SkillEvents.SkillUsed` from firing twice.
- It runs inside `RunBoostedCheck` (`:97`), so ClusterF's attempts and amount apply.

**Fidelity: below each call's stock max it is bit-identical to pinned**, by construction. Nothing an OSI player would
notice below 100 or 120 changes, because every row's floor is at or above the stock max (100, 120 or 142).

**Rows, keyed by (skill, check kind, stock min, stock max, target type).** The keys are literal pinned numbers, so a
verification test must assert each pinned line still holds them, or an upstream bump breaks a row silently. The
group files list the exact pairs.

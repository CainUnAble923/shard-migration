# League Chapter 2: The Last Return

**Draft for Chase to edit. P593, October 25, 2026.**

Bronze Citizen trial. Blade piece: **the Grip**. Place: **a private trial copy of Despise**. Chapter question: **Who has noticed the loop before?**

This is a playable story proposal based on [the League storyline](../docs/league-storyline.md), especially Chapter 2 and the decided rules. All new names, dialogue, encounters, timing, and reward handling below are drafts. They are not implemented features or new canon. Proposed length: about 25 to 35 minutes, solo or with a party. Exact rooms, route, combat numbers, and trial-map support need implementation review and a client walkthrough.

## What the player takes away

The League has sent people after the broken sword before. Some understood the loop well enough to leave warnings. They still failed to bring everyone home.

The player succeeds by listening to one of those people and helping her finish an expedition that should have ended centuries ago. The Grip comes out with her. She lives.

This chapter does not explain what the pieces gain from the loop, introduce the Unbound, or offer the later Mend or Sunder choice. Those reveals belong to later chapters.

## Setting and cast

Despise is wet stone, low ceilings, and water striking the same puddle at the same steady pace. A League trail has been cut into the rock: a circle with a short line through it. The markings are old enough to have softened at the edges. The blood beneath them is still red.

Use a short route through existing Despise rooms: an entry landing, three expedition stops, a dry camp, a narrow return passage, and an exit threshold. These are staging names, not claims about particular map coordinates. Reuse stock scenery, human NPCs, books, corpses, chest graphics, and available effects. No new art is required by this draft.

**Elara Voss, the League Registrar.** The existing Registrar opens and closes the chapter. She is practical and careful with the truth. She knows an expedition disappeared. She does not know it is still trying to return.

**Tamsin Reed, expedition leader.** Proposed new NPC and the last survivor. A patched field coat, a cracked Wisp Orb, and a small iron chest are all she has kept. She can remember her companions' habits more easily than their names. At camp she is always packing for the return journey.

**Orren, surveyor; Mina, smith; Bram, guard.** Proposed expedition members, dead along the route. Each left a journal. Give each a trade and a small human detail, rather than making every journal a lecture about time.

**The expedition chest.** A guaranteed scripted mimic containing the Grip. Tamsin recovered the piece earlier and stored it here. She carries the chest, so the Grip is both in her keeping and inside the mimic, as the storyline requires. Its origin as a mimic remains unexplained here.

## Beat 1: A report that never came back

Offer this as the Bronze chapter after Chapter 1. Players already at or above Bronze can return for it under the existing chapter-waiver decision. This draft adds no retroactive rank requirement and does not replace the separate promotion job.

At the field office, the Pommel turns in the player's hand toward Despise. It stops when pointed that way. Elara places an old expedition record on the desk.

**Elara:** "It has been doing that since you came through the door. Despise, unless my map has moved again."

**Player:** "You sent someone there before."

**Elara:** "The League did. A surveyor, a smith, a guard, and their leader. Their departure is in the old register. Their return should be on the next page."

**Player:** "And the next page?"

**Elara:** "Blank. I would like to put something on it."

Optional question, **"What do you expect me to find?"**

**Elara:** "Their records, if any survived. Anyone still living. And whatever the Pommel is trying to find. In that order."

Accept text:

> Follow the Pommel into Despise. Find the League expedition and learn what happened to it. You may bring your party.

An expedition mark at the Despise entrance offers entry to the private trial. Nobody needs to hunt for a rare chest or change the public dungeon to begin.

## Beat 2: Three names on the way down

The Pommel warms at each expedition mark. Each stop contains a body and a readable journal. Reading records the entry in the chapter journal, so a loose book cannot be lost or taken by another player. Short excerpts carry the necessary clues; longer pages can be optional.

**First stop, Orren.** His measuring line is neatly coiled beside him. The date in his journal is more than two centuries earlier than the current League register. Avoid a specific calendar year until Chase sets one. His body looks a few weeks old.

Inspection:

> The water has not worn through his coat. His hands still hold the end of the line. You check the journal's date again.

**Orren's journal:**

> Tamsin says we leave at first light. There is no first light down here, so I have drawn a sun on the wall. Bram says it is a poor likeness. He is right.
>
> We found an older League account in the chest. A collapse beneath New Haven. Someone gets through it and takes a black pommel from a sealed room. The account has no name. It describes the stone falling, and then falling again.

This recalls Chapter 1's established premise without inventing a particular puzzle solution for that chapter.

**Second stop, Mina.** There is a leather repair roll beside her and black dust on her fingers.

**Mina's journal:**

> The piece is a sword grip. Something has broken through both ends. Whatever blade struck Mondain's Gem did not survive the blow whole.
>
> I wrapped it twice and put it in the iron chest. Tamsin carries it now. I tried to scratch my name inside the lid. It was already there, under a coat of rust.

**Third stop, Bram.** His shield leans toward the passage, as though he sat down while watching it.

**Bram's journal:**

> We reached the way out. I could smell rain. Then Tamsin was packing at camp again, and Orren was asking where I had put his line.
>
> I wrote their names on this page. If I forget, I can read them. Orren. Mina. Tamsin. Mine is Bram.

Two short fights against trial-only cave guards interrupt the investigation, with safe reading space after each. Proposed enemies are ordinary Despise creature types selected and sized later for a Bronze solo player, with party scaling. No specialist skill or particular guild is required to read the evidence.

Objective after all three stops:

> Find Tamsin Reed at the expedition camp.

## Beat 3: The woman who is always leaving

Tamsin stands beside a cold fire, packing the same folded blanket. The chest sits at her feet. Her cracked Wisp Orb glows faintly beside it.

**Tamsin:** "You found the marks. Good. Have the others caught up?"

**Player:** "I found their journals."

**Tamsin:** "Keep them dry. We leave as soon as everyone is ready."

**Player:** "Tamsin, how long have you been here?"

**Tamsin:** "Three weeks. We have enough food for another four days."

Her answer matches the freshness of the bodies, not the dates on their pages. She does not recognize the passage of centuries.

Selecting **"Show her the Pommel"** makes the chest rattle once.

**Tamsin:** "That belongs with what we found. You can have our piece when we get out. Help me finish the return. I cannot leave them here again."

Do not offer a false choice to kill or rob her. The trial is to help her finish.

### The Wisp Orb tie

Inspecting Tamsin's cracked orb plays a brief proposed expedition echo. A pale shape follows her old footsteps to the chest, then turns back toward camp. It repeats a fragment of her command:

> "Stay close. We are going home."

**Tamsin:** "We used an orb to guide a creature through the passages. I kept its anchor on the chest. Wherever I carried it, the creature followed."

**Player:** "It still follows that route."

**Tamsin:** "So do I."

The current Wisp Orb source has possession, anchor, leash, and aggression behavior. This cracked expedition orb's recorded echo is a proposed quest scene, not an existing orb ability. It introduces that dungeon system through the expedition's history without requiring possession, an aligned orb, or a karma change. A neutral-karma character can finish the chapter. Do not route this trial through the public Despise boss sequence or conscription.

## Beat 4: The first return

Select **"We will walk out together"** to begin the escort. Tamsin picks up the chest. She follows at a forgiving distance and waits at marked stops if the party falls behind.

She pauses where each companion died. The player selects **"Read the name from the journal"** at each stop. There is no spelling quiz or timed dialogue.

**At Orren:** "He measured everything twice. Said the second measurement was for the fool who trusted the first."

**At Mina:** "She fixed my buckle before we left. Would not take a coin for it."

**At Bram:** "He wanted the first watch. Said he slept better once the rest of us were snoring."

During the escort, a trial-only ambush blocks the narrow return passage. Tamsin sets the chest down and takes cover while the party clears it. She does not wander into combat or require the player to be a healer.

At the threshold, daylight shows beyond the stone. Tamsin stops.

**Tamsin:** "There should be four of us."

**Player:** "We have their names. We can tell the League."

**Tamsin:** "I know. I know I said that before."

The chest latch clicks. Water falls upward for one brief moment. With a short transition, everyone stands back at the cold camp. Tamsin is folding the blanket.

**Tamsin:** "You found the marks. Good. Have the others caught up?"

Journal update:

> The return has repeated. Your notes remain. The chest moved first. Show Tamsin what you remember.

This is the chapter's one required story rewind. It does not reset skills, equipment, rewards, or the character's normal legacy progression. Cleared encounters stay cleared. The player does not have to repeat the investigation or escort fights to discover the next step.

## Beat 5: Open it here

At camp the player selects **"We reached the exit. The chest brought us back."** Tamsin looks at the blanket, then at the journals.

**Tamsin:** "Orren. Mina. Bram."

**Player:** "You remembered."

**Tamsin:** "For a moment. Keep the pages where I can see them."

She kneels beside the chest.

**Tamsin:** "I thought if I got the piece home, it would mean they had not died for nothing. Every time we failed, I tried the same way again."

**Player:** "Then we open it here."

**Tamsin:** "Yes. Before I forget why."

She takes the cracked orb from her belt. Its pale echo stops circling the chest and settles by the journals. This is a story gesture, with no change to ordinary Wisp Orb rules. The Grip is the time anchor in this scene; the orb illustrates how long she has followed the same command.

Ready prompt:

> Tamsin will open the expedition chest. Defend her and take back the Grip. Begin when your party is ready.

## Beat 6: The chest that will not let go

The mimic opens when Tamsin lifts the latch. Its lid bears the scratches Mina described. Inside, wrapped in rotten leather, is the Grip.

**Tamsin:** "Mina put that in my hands. You are going to give it back."

Fight proposal: a clear, soloable mimic encounter, with a short lid-slam windup shown by text and an effect. Players can move away before the slam. At two health thresholds it closes its lid and becomes briefly protected while a visible latch appears on its side. Any party member can use the latch. Tamsin holds it open, ending the protection so the fight resumes. No lockpicking check, rare item, or simultaneous party interaction is needed.

First latch prompt:

> The lid is closing. Release the side latch so Tamsin can hold it open.

**Tamsin, first latch:** "I have it. Get the piece clear."

**Tamsin, second latch:** "Orren. Mina. Bram. I am still here."

These are short encounter pauses, not another forced rewind. Ignoring the latch leaves a readable objective rather than starting an endless damage race. Tamsin cannot be killed while holding it. The fight ends with the mimic collapsing into an ordinary broken chest and the Grip exposed inside.

This story mimic is guaranteed and separate from F-32's rare dungeon-chest mimics. It grants no guaranteed PetMimic, tame opportunity, or repeatable rare loot. Its exact combat behavior is new draft material and needs later implementation and balance work.

## Beat 7: Four names return

Select **"Recover the Grip"** at the broken chest. This records provisional recovery for each eligible character on the shared trial. Tamsin must still cross the threshold before the chapter can be completed.

Inspection:

> The leather has worn smooth where a thumb would rest. Black metal shows through at either end. Near the Pommel, the piece grows warm. For a moment you feel a hand close around yours, then let go.

**Tamsin:** "I thought I had to bring them back the way they were."

**Player:** "Tell the League who they were."

**Tamsin:** "Will you walk with me? I would like to reach the door while I still know their names."

The last walk is short and clear. At the threshold Tamsin stops once, says the three names, and steps through. This time the water keeps falling. The chest stays broken.

**Tamsin:** "I can hear birds."

Return the party to the real Despise entrance. Tamsin survives outside the local loop. This does not free the whole dungeon, resurrect her companions, or end Britannia's larger cycle. Her proposed appearance at the field office is visible as this character's chapter outcome, without forcing a public-world change for everyone else.

Objective:

> Return to Elara Voss with Tamsin's account and the Grip.

## Beat 8: The next page

At the office, Elara puts a fresh page beside the old departure record. Tamsin reads the names from the journals while Elara writes.

**Elara:** "Orren, surveyor. Mina, smith. Bram, guard. Tamsin Reed, expedition leader."

**Tamsin:** "Returned. Write returned by my name."

**Elara:** "Returned."

Optional player question, **"How many expeditions did the League send?"**

**Elara:** "I do not know. Some registers begin halfway through a sentence. Some list the same people twice. Until today, I thought we had lost the other pages."

**Player:** "They wrote about the chamber under New Haven. Before I went there."

**Elara:** "Then you have their warning. I wish someone had been able to carry it back to them."

On placing the Grip beside the Pommel:

**Elara:** "Two pieces of the blade that struck the Gem. The sword broke, and time broke with it. Others found the same trail before us. Now we have one of them here to tell us where it led."

**Tamsin:** "And where it stopped."

Elara closes the old register. Leave the remaining pieces and Chapter 3's question for later.

## Reward and completion handling, proposed

- **The Grip:** a unique story keepsake and the second recovered blade piece. Suggested item text: "Recovered from the League expedition in Despise. The leather remembers the hands that held it." No weapon stats, combat bonus, or automatic reforging in this chapter.
- **Chapter journal:** Tamsin's account and the three journal excerpts remain readable. The Grip and Pommel can be inspected together for the warmth scene without consuming either one.
- **Chapter completion:** record Chapter 2 after Tamsin exits and the player reports to Elara. Apply the existing Bronze trial requirement where applicable. Do not award a second promotion or remove the standing and promotion-job requirements.
- **Ownership:** each eligible participant gets personal recovery and completion credit, with one Grip reward per character. Helpers may accompany a friend without receiving duplicate blade pieces. No last-hit or chest-loot race.
- **Other payouts:** leave XP, Renown, and gold amounts to the eventual implementation prompt. The Grip and completed story are the specified rewards here.

Prefer a recoverable story entitlement over a loose item that can block later chapters. If a pack is full, record the earned reward for collection from Elara. Replacing a lost keepsake should restore that character's existing piece, never create a second earned reward. These are handling proposals for Chase to review.

## Retry and party notes for the eventual build

The journal objectives are explicit: find the records, meet Tamsin, escort her, investigate the rewind, defeat the chest, bring Tamsin out, report to Elara. Dialogue choices can be revisited. The trial needs no guessing about where progress stopped.

On defeat or leaving early, allow a retry without consuming the Pommel or losing recorded journals. Tamsin takes cover during ordinary fights; if the whole party falls, pause or restart the current encounter rather than treating her as dead. The story rewind happens once per attempt, and repeated combat attempts do not replay it. Do not award farming loot from reset encounters.

Disconnects and an abandoned trial return characters safely to the real entrance. Preserve durable milestones already earned, especially Tamsin's exit and personal Grip recovery, so a lost connection before the office report cannot lose the reward or award it twice. Party members who have not completed Chapter 1 can help, but follow the story in order for their own completion credit. Exact sharing range and reentry rules belong to the future build spec.

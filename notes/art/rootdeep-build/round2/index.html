Rootdeep Burrows: the build, rendered from the scratch map (round 2)

cc-P180, 2026-10-19. P141's approved layout built into a scratch copy of Trammel (outside both repos); nothing has touched the real map files. Drawn by notes/cc-P167-tools/isorender.py from the real tiles with the client's layout (statics on the land diamond's bottom, sloped land drawn with its texture; no hues, no lighting). Round 1 (2026-10-18) is kept in round1/.

Approve these renders, or say what to change.
What changed since round 1: (1) walls follow the rules measured on OSI buildings and house multis (notes/uo-wall-orientation.md): the piece that runs along x sits on its tile's south edge, the one along y on its east edge, the corner piece closes a room's south-east corner and a T-junction, the post marks the north-west corner; round 1 had the two pieces swapped and the corners inverted, which drew every run as a saw-tooth. (2) Variety: every zone draws its floor from all the stock variants of its texture by weight, the cave rings and ramps from eight rock tiles each, and crystals, webs, stalagmites, hay, rubble, mushrooms, logs, vines and bones are placed in seeded clusters with gaps, mixed sizes and no two identical pieces side by side; one seed per zone in plan-sets.json. (3) The renderer: round 1's drew every static half a tile too high and could not draw slopes, so the cave rings showed black seams; this one draws them as the client does. The walkable floor, the openings, the crawlway and every wall tile are exactly round 1's (checked by the builder).

2. An arch and webs at the lair mouth

The Terathan stone set's north-south doorway is two pieces over a 2-tile gap (0x01D8 passable half on the first open tile, 0x01D9 jamb half on the wall tile after the gap: Terathan Keep 5431,3132-3134 and the tower house multi 0x7A both do it that way). The approved mouth is 3 tiles (y 1246-1248), so (a) keeps all three open and puts the jamb one tile further south, leaving the arch top open in the middle; (b) is OSI's 2-tile doorway, which would close 5183,1248. (a) is what the Gloom render shows. Say (a), (b), or no arch.

3. The cave rings as they are

A cave ring as it is: rock at floor level, +12, then the void at +20 (Crawl Den NW corner). Impassable sloped land, as OSI's caves; no wall items on top (they would block sight and spells like the item-walled zones do).

5. The Vault doorway with no door

The Vault doorway at 5179,1137-1138 with no door. The region refuses non-trainees at the tile; a door item would have to do the same.

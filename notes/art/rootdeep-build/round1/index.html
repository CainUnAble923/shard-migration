Rootdeep Burrows: the build, rendered from the scratch map

cc-P167, 2026-10-18. P141's approved layout built into a scratch copy of Trammel (outside both repos), drawn by vseUOAware from the real tiles: the top-down overview in radar colours and an isometric render of each zone (classic art, no hues, no texture-mapped land, so flat land shows its art diamond, as the client draws it; the client adds lighting and the land textures). Nothing has touched the real map files.

Approve these renders, or say what to change.
Built as the mockup page proposed (its first set per zone, all stock EA tiles: no third-party art, so nothing new is registered): Landing L-A dungeon stone cave, Vault V-A marble strongroom (counters for the shelving, marble-walled pillars), Warren W-A dirt burrows, Tangle T-A rooted cave, Dawnseam D-A crystal seam, Gloom G-A Terathan dark stone, lair Q-A, crawlway C-A. Cave zones use sloped cave-rock land for their walls as OSI's own caves do; every floor is flat at Z 0. Things you might want changed: a zone's look, more or fewer rocks, mushrooms, crystals and webs, the pillars as walled blocks, the counters as shelving.

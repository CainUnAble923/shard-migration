Rootdeep Burrows: the build, rendered from the scratch map (round 4)

cc-P185, 2026-10-19. P141's approved layout built into a scratch copy of Trammel (outside both repos); nothing has touched the real map files. Drawn by scripts/mapbuild/isorender.py from the real tiles with the client's layout (no hues, no lighting). Round 1 (2026-10-18) is in round1/, round 2 in round2/, round 3 (the one you marked) in round3/.

Approve these renders, or say what to change.
Your marks on round 3 were: spaces next to the archways, torches too low, bad corners in the centre of the Vault, and the odd corners at wall ends, stubs and junctions in every zone. What changed: (1) Junctions: the piece a wall tile takes now comes from the runs meeting at it (which neighbours are wall tiles), measured on 9,744 OSI wall tiles in nine dungeons: a run's free end takes its plain piece, the four corners of an outline take post / ew / ns / corner, a T takes the through run's piece. 23 tiles changed; every mark you drew is one of them or a torch, pier or pillar. (2) Torches sit at Z 13 above the floor on the floor tile in front of the wall, as every OSI dungeon torch does. (3) Arches: the wall before a gap keeps its run piece, the arch top goes on the first open tile and the pier only on the wall tile after, which is OSI's bay (Nujel'm, Magincia); the half-tile gaps are gone. (4) The Strongroom pillars are solid plinths: the walled ring with a marble floor at wall height on top (seven variants below; f picked). The walkable floor, the 18 openings, the crawlway and all 1,614 wall tiles are exactly round 3's (the builder refuses to write otherwise).

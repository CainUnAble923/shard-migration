## What the real client found

Every fact was green while the first walk failed. Both bugs are fixed and the facts now cover them.

1. **No person could be ticked off.** A client click reaches `OnAosSingleClick`, and I had only overridden `OnSingleClick`. The facts called the wrong one.
2. **The prisoners and Mirren were solid gold.** TazUO paints a Blessed mobile gold unless its name is yellow. They are not Blessed now. They use `IsInvulnerable`, which pinned's `CanBeHarmful` already refuses.
3. **The window covered the hall**, on top of the people to click. It now sits at x 830, right of the default game view, 440 wide, with the ledger below it. On a window much narrower than the rig's 1280 it would need dragging; I did not test that.

## Where it differs from the plan

- **Warden Hale gives the warning at the crate**, not a separate guard. He wears armor and is the yellow name you just met.
- **The crate is a double-click** to take, and a double-click or a drop to put back, because the marked pouch is a lesson loan.
- **The raider is named Sarn.** The plan left him unnamed.
- **"Click yourself" is not a required step:** "Your name is gray" and your name in gray appear overhead on taking the pouch anyway.
- **Green (ally) is not taught.** The prompt listed five colors.

Findings:

1. **bug** | `server/customizations/ClusterFWoodBonuses.cs:206`, also `:236`: Both Apply overloads write DurabilityBonus even when the row adds zero, so ModernUO's unconditional unscale/scale setters round current and maximum hit points upward; the Ghostwood weapon probe went from 51/51 before the helper to 53/53 after it, despite neither row granting durability. Skip the durability assignment when `b.Durability == 0` in both overloads, and add hit-point assertions for woods with no durability bonus and Ironwood's exceptional second row.

2. **bug** | `server/customizations/ClusterFWoodBonuses.cs:220`; `pinned/Items/Weapons/ML Weapons/Artifacts/PhantomStaff.cs:20`: The helper accepts Phantom Staff and writes the elemental wood row, but that staff's GetDamageTypes override always returns 50 cold and 50 poison, so an Emberbark staff's stored 50 fire never reaches combat and the Frostbark and Runewood splits are likewise ignored. Make that override honor these extended-wood splits while retaining its stock split for other woods, and test GetDamageTypes on the normal-only Phantom Staff recipe.

3. **risk** | `server/tests/Items/ExtendedWoodBonusesVerification.cs:189`: Every craft uses a null tool, so the suite never checks overlapping runic and wood properties or their cap policy; simply substituting a stock wood runic tool would still miss those cases because the current wood runic attribute ranges are all zero. Add deterministic overlap cases with seeded attributes or explicitly configured test-only runic ranges, plus an actual runic-tool craft, and assert the approved stacking limits without changing P227's recorded exceptional-damage rule.

4. **risk** | `server/tests/Items/ExtendedWoodBonusesVerification.cs:209`, also `:242`: The weapon and shield checks stop after crafting, so there is no regression check that repair preserves the wood bonuses and Starwood's chosen extra. Add a damaged extended-wood weapon and shield repaired through the real Repair target, compare their bonus snapshots before and after, and cover both successful repair and the weakening branch without treating its intended maximum-hit-point loss as bonus loss.

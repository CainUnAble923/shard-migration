# Clean Up Britannia changes

Player guide draft, checked against source on October 24, 2026. Availability on the running shard and the published player package have not been checked.

Cleanup now pays Clean Up Britannia points without making civic waste bundles. You can redeem old bundles at the Sanitation Warden, and your Trash Bag has two options for clearing monster corpses.

## Redeem your old civic waste bundles

Drag your old **civic waste bundles** onto the **Sanitation Warden**. Each bundle gives **10 Clean Up points**. For example, a stack of 5 gives 50 points.

The Warden takes and deletes the bundles when you redeem them. Each bundle can be redeemed once; this is not a limit of one redemption per character. You can hand in other old bundles later. Custodian membership is not required for redemption.

Cleaning no longer creates new bundles. Your existing bundles remain redeemable.

## Civic Contracts now count points

Open the Guild Directory with `[guild`, choose **The Custodians**, and open **Contracts**. Accept a Civic Contract before you start earning the points you want it to count.

A contract tracks **Clean Up points earned after you accept it**. Earlier earnings do not count. Points from cleanup, trash barrels, your bag, and old bundles redeemed after acceptance can count.

The requirements are **50, 150, 400, 800, or 2,000 points**, depending on the contract. Refuse Collection, the first contract, asks for 50 points and pays 100 gold. Check your contract's progress in the ledger, then return to that page to turn it in.

Turning in a contract leaves your Clean Up points available to spend in the store. Spending points also leaves your earned contract progress intact. The same earnings cannot finish two contracts: when you turn one in, overlapping progress on other open Civic Contracts is adjusted.

If you accepted a contract before this change, open its ledger page before doing more cleanup. It starts tracking new earnings when you first view its progress under the new rules. Earlier points do not fill it, and you no longer need bundles to finish it.

## Clear monster corpses from your Trash Bag

Keep **your own Trash Bag in your backpack**, then right-click it. The corpse options do not require Custodian membership, but the bag must belong to your character. A borrowed bag or a bag in your bank will not show them.

| Menu option | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Clear a corpse** | Lets you target one monster corpse within 12 tiles. |
| **Clear corpses nearby** | Clears eligible monster corpses within 10 tiles of where you stand. It leaves loose ground items alone. |

You do not put the corpse into the bag. Select the menu option while the bag stays in your backpack.

**Loot anything you want to keep first.** Clearing permanently deletes the corpse and everything still inside it, including items inside other containers. If any contents remain, a confirmation window asks before clearing. Empty corpses clear without that question. The ordinary bag protections for loose items do not save belongings left inside a cleared corpse.

A player's corpse can never be cleared, including your own. A monster corpse is refused while clearing it would violate someone else's looting rights or destroy loot reserved for another attacker. Having rights to some of a shared kill's loot does not always let you clear the whole corpse. Loot first, or wait for those protections to expire.

**Clear corpses nearby** has a **60-second cooldown after a successful sweep**, shared with `[cleanupall`. Finding no eligible corpses or canceling the confirmation does not start that cooldown.

Clearing pays the usual Clean Up points for the remaining contents, plus an earned creature bonus when you or your party qualify for it. An empty corpse does not guarantee points, and clearing does not make civic waste bundles.

## Getting a bag and seeing the menu

Joining The Custodians gives you a free, blessed Trash Bag. The Sanitation Warden also supplies replacements to members. Empty your old bag before asking for a replacement: replacing it deletes the old owned bag and its contents wherever it is.

The two corpse labels require the player package update that includes them. If the menu shows blank rows, update your player package. Publication of that update has not been verified for this draft.

# New player FAQ

Draft, October 22, 2026. This page describes built features and decided plans, not verified availability on the running shard. Future features are marked (planned); conflicting details are marked (being confirmed).

## 1. How do I start?

In New Haven, find Rook Hallowell near the arrival plaza and double-click him to open orientation. Follow [Starting in New Haven](new-haven-start.md) for the core lessons and your next steps.

## 2. Do I have to finish every tutorial lesson?

Finish the required core to graduate; the wings are optional. The guided story through those wings is (planned), while lesson completeness is (being confirmed) in [Starting in New Haven](new-haven-start.md).

## 3. What does Young mean?

Young gives newcomers protection with exceptions, including tutorial fights. Its source duration is 336 hours of account play time, with voluntary renunciation and murder rules that can end it early. Graduation's effect on Young is (being confirmed); see [Levels, loops, and scrolls](levels-loops-and-scrolls.md).

## 4. When can I leave New Haven island?

Complete Rook's core and choose **Graduate**, then use an available travel route off the island. Permission to leave by skipping is (being confirmed). [Starting in New Haven](new-haven-start.md) explains graduation and travel.

## 5. Can I skip the tutorial and return later?

Rook offers **Skip the tutorial** with confirmations, and **Remedial training** if you return afterward. The skip policy and its progression rewards are (being confirmed). Check [Starting in New Haven](new-haven-start.md) before choosing.

## 6. What is a loop?

A loop is the shard's progression through repeated lives, with lasting accomplishments carried forward. Graduation records the first loop; later player resets and the Keeper and Breaker paths are (planned). Reset details are (being confirmed) in [Levels, loops, and scrolls](levels-loops-and-scrolls.md).

## 7. How do player levels and skills work?

The full player level and XP system is (planned), while skills improve through use. How skill caps connect to loops is (being confirmed). [Levels, loops, and scrolls](levels-loops-and-scrolls.md) separates player levels from the strength estimates used by other systems.

## 8. What do power scrolls and recipe scrolls do?

Power-scroll cap effects and their relationship to future loops are (being confirmed); loop-scroll progression and League merging are (planned). Recipe scrolls teach crafting designs instead. Compare [Levels, loops, and scrolls](levels-loops-and-scrolls.md) with [Crafting changes](crafting-changes.md) before using one.

## 9. Which guild should I join?

Choose a guild whose work interests you, using the Guild Directory's skill list. You can belong to several guilds, so start with the activities you want to try. These guides compare the paths.

## 10. How do I find a guildmaster and join?

Type `[guild`, choose a guild, and press **Show me the way** for directions. Joining requirements, membership scope, and Apprentice rank displays are (being confirmed) because the sources retain different descriptions. [Gathering guilds](gathering-guilds.md) explains the joining and Apprentice menus.

## 11. What is the League, and where do I register?

The League of Extraordinary Citizens connects the guilds and provides citizen ranks, services, and work. Visit Elara Voss, the League Registrar at New Haven's field office, using the League row in the Guild Directory. The [League guide](league.md) distinguishes her services from Rook's orientation.

## 12. Are AP, Renown, and guild standing the same thing?

AP is a permanent achievement score that unlocks rewards, Renown is the League's spendable balance, and guild standing measures reputation within a guild. Guild currencies are separate again. Renown spending descriptions and which earnings count toward Lifetime Renown are (being confirmed); see the [League guide](league.md).

## 13. How do I get my starter pet?

Normal graduation offers an evo starter animal if you did not already take the farm pet. Farm availability and the starter's companion terminology are (being confirmed). [Companions and evo pets](companions-and-evo-pets.md) explains the choices and follower space needed.

## 14. Can I hire a squire or humanoid companion?

Humanoid companion hiring, class development, and promotion are (planned). Their rank-to-loop rules and party limits are (being confirmed). [Companions and evo pets](companions-and-evo-pets.md) distinguishes that future party system from starter animals.

## 15. How does my pet grow?

An evo pet earns combat XP from creatures it helped damage and kill. Eligible ordinary tamed pets instead offer **Begin Animal Training** through Animal Lore, with follower-slot consequences. Owner-loop requirements and bonuses are (being confirmed), and the Bond Trial is (planned); read [Companions and evo pets](companions-and-evo-pets.md).

## 16. What should I do when I need healing or resurrection?

Use bandages on a living patient, with Healing and Anatomy for people or Veterinary and Animal Lore for animals. When dead, approach a healer and accept the resurrection window; [Rangers, Custodians, and Healers](rangers-custodians-healers.md) explains the conditions. The tutorial dungeon's resurrection destination is (being confirmed).

## 17. What is different about crafting here?

Crafting adds learned designs, guild work, and extended materials. Exceptional material-bonus details are (being confirmed). Levelable gear's normal player access is (planned), and deployment is (being confirmed); [Crafting changes](crafting-changes.md) explains the systems.

## 18. Why are recipes missing from my craft menu?

Higher designs can stay hidden until you learn their pattern book or individual recipe scroll. Learning teaches knowledge, so you still need the skill, tools, and materials to make the item. Merged-book access and promotion hints are (being confirmed); follow [Crafting changes](crafting-changes.md).

## 19. What are bulk order deeds?

Bulk order deeds, or BODs, request quantities of named crafted goods, sometimes with material and quality requirements. Ask an appropriate town vendor or your craft's guildmaster about orders. Direct large-deed filling, teaching rules, allowances, and payouts are (being confirmed); compare [Crafting changes](crafting-changes.md) and [Society of Smiths](society-of-smiths.md).

## 20. How are contracts, commissions, and requisitions different?

Guild contracts request supplies or deliveries, commissions request a client's piece or set, and requisitions request matching magic equipment. Moving client commissions to the League is (planned). Commission penalties, nonmember rewards, Lead chances, refresh rules, and gathering-contract skill checks are (being confirmed); see [Crafting changes](crafting-changes.md) and the [League guide](league.md).

## 21. Why did a rare resource turn into basic ore or logs?

Extended resource discoveries can require a report before you keep harvesting that material. Open **Field Report** in the Prospector's Logbook or **Discovery Report** in the Forester's Logbook. Harvesting and crafting requirements are (being confirmed); [Gathering guilds](gathering-guilds.md) explains their separate roles.

## 22. How do I start Imbuing?

Find the Artificers' Order through the Guild Directory and read its [Imbuing guide](artificers-order.md) before modifying equipment. Formula gates, book ranks, Unravel materials, and portable-forge descriptions are (being confirmed). Normal sources for Overcharged knowledge are (planned).

## 23. Can I fish and sail?

Start by double-clicking a fishing pole and targeting nearby water, or use a classic boat to reach open sea. [High Seas fishing](high-seas-fishing.md) covers catches and boat handling. Trap and sea-champion access are (being confirmed); galleon and naval-combat proposals are (planned), with ship direction (being confirmed).

## 24. Where do I see achievements and rewards?

## 25. How do I open a loot box?

Keep it in your backpack and double-click it in a guarded town with active guards, or a house where you are an owner, co-owner, or friend. Contents, naming, gear comparisons, and what happens to the opened box are (being confirmed). Use [Achievements and loot boxes](achievements-and-loot-boxes.md) for the opening rules.

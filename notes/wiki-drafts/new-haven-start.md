# Starting in New Haven

Draft from project notes and source checked October 22, 2026. Features still to come are marked (planned). Placement, settings, and availability on the running shard have not been checked.

## Arriving and finding Rook

New characters arrive in New Haven, on Trammel. Look for **Rook Hallowell, the League recruiter**, beside the plaza near the arrival point. Double-click him from nearby to open **New Haven orientation**.

The tutorial starts on the first login of an account that has not begun it. Its welcome messages point toward Rook when he is present. Progress is saved, so logging out does not erase completed stations. The tutorial normally runs once per account.

Rook handles orientation. **Elara Voss, the League Registrar**, handles League registration and services at the field office. The nearby training board opens the Guild Directory, with the older New Haven training quests on its second tab. Those quests are separate from Rook's core.

NPCs teach in their own voices. Messages beginning **System:** come from the Echo, the tutorial's dry commentator.

## The nine core trials

Complete the nine core entries on Rook's page to make **Graduate** available. The wings are optional.

Most core entries currently show a lesson preview and count as completed when opened. Their hands-on trials are (planned). The loop vision is built. Reopen Rook's orientation page to choose your next entry. A preview's listed location or promised tool does not mean a full lesson is available there yet.

| Core entry | Subject | Current form |
|---|---|---|
| Walking, running and the camera | Movement, scaling, hotkeys, and options | Preview; hands-on trial (planned). |
| Your paperdoll | Equipment, the alternate paperdoll, and durability display | Preview; hands-on trial (planned). |
| Your backpack | Containers and comparing items | Preview; hands-on trial (planned). |
| Speaking and the journal | Chat, journal filters, fonts, and nameplates | Preview; hands-on trial (planned). |

On a supported Shattered Legacy TazUO client, relevant previews can offer buttons to open the World Map, Journal, or options. Other clients retain the written hints.

The vision changes no skills, level, loop count, or Young status. It is an introduction to the story. Graduation records your first real loop later.

### Wren Tallow and the farm

The farm lesson is built, but its world placement and client walk remain unfinished in the cited farm notes. Where it is available, choose **Animals** for directions west of town, past the Bardic Guild. **Wren Tallow, the League farmhand**, waits at the field gate.

| Step | What to do |
|---|---|
| Receive a pet | Double-click Wren and choose a dog or cat. Keep a follower slot free. Your evo pet arrives bonded, follows you, and offers a hue choice. |
| Clear the pests | Fight the field's pests with your pet. The default target is 3 kills in which you or your pet dealt damage. This completes the Animals station. |
| Patch up | Return to Wren for bandages, then successfully heal yourself or another with Healing, or your pet with Veterinary. This completes Bandages. |

An unfinished pest count restarts if the server restarts. Completed farm steps are saved.

The farm is outside New Haven's guarded town. Tutorial creatures can fight trainees even while they are Young. Young status does not make every tutorial fight harmless.

### The Vault in Rootdeep Burrows

Rootdeep Burrows has a tutorial-only Vault beside its landing. The way down is in Old Haven's ruined Second Bank. The map route and Vault rules are built; current world placement has not been checked for this guide.

Active trainees and their pets can enter. In the Stacks, the Echo supplies practice nets and teaches you to hold a rat. Defeating the Overdraft opens a one-way crawlway into the Warren for trainees who helped damage it, including through their pets. The Vault then refuses reentry. Graduates cannot enter it.

The Vault and its net lesson are separate activities. Neither is required by the current nine-entry graduation check. Finish any farm or Vault activities you want before graduating, since they require an active trainee.

## Skipping and coming back

Choose **Skip the tutorial** at Rook. The System asks twice. Declining either confirmation keeps you in the tutorial.

Skipping earns the public achievement **Didn't Read the Manual**. Under the current default rule, it also records your first loop and opens island travel. That skip policy is provisional. An alternate rule requires remedial graduation before leaving.

Skipping does not give the normal Explorer graduation box or starter pet choice. Rook then offers **Remedial training**, the full tutorial with its normal graduation rewards. Starting remedial clears your station progress so you can work through the core again.

Graduating from remedial earns **Read the Manual Eventually**. If skipping already recorded your first loop, remedial graduation does not record or reward that loop again.

## Graduating, your first boxes, and your pet

Once all nine core entries are done, return to Rook and choose **Graduate**. You do not need to finish every wing or reach level 50 first.

| Graduation result | What it means |
|---|---|
| First real loop | Your character moves from loop 0 to loop 1. |
| First-loop reward | The first recorded loop awards its Loop box and fanfare once. |
| Tutorial reward | Normal graduation also awards an Explorer's tier-1 box, the Common graduation box. |
| Evo pet choice | Choose dog, cat, fox, or bird if you did not already take the farm pet. |
| Island travel | You can leave New Haven island, subject to ordinary travel rules. |
| Young status | Graduation leaves your remaining Young play time intact. |

Normal first graduation therefore has two box awards: the Loop box and the Explorer box. Other achievements may have given you boxes earlier.

After an account graduates, its later characters receive their own first loop at login. They do not repeat the tutorial or receive its Explorer box and pet offer through that login.

Keep a box in your backpack and double-click it in a guarded town with active guards. Boxes also open in a house where you are an owner, co-owner, or friend. They are bound to your account. Opening the first one in New Haven is a useful introduction to the reveal and rewards.

The evo starter uses 1 follower slot and arrives bonded. Pick its hue when offered. Taking a dog or cat at Wren's farm replaces the graduation pet choice; graduation does not add a second pet. These are ordinary evo pets, even though the graduation picker calls them companions. A squire from a later League quest is (planned).

The decided level ladder (planned) is 50 before graduation and 100 after the first loop, followed by later increases. Actual XP leveling, loop skill resets, and applying those level limits are (planned). Recording graduation currently changes no skills, stats, or XP. It also does not yet supply the owner-loop progress needed by higher evo stages.

## Leaving the island

Before your first loop, the default travel rule keeps you on Haven island, including its Rootdeep underground area. You can move around the island and use permitted local travel. Mainland exits are refused until graduation or the provisional skip allowance.

After graduating, choose your destination and use an ordinary available travel route. Graduation opens permission to leave; it does not automatically transport you away.

Young normally lasts for 336 hours of account play time. Graduation does not end that clock. Voluntary renunciation and the existing murder rule can still end Young early.

## Contradictions

These differences remain on record without deciding between the sources.

- **Story and opening:** F-35 describes a vision before waking and a continuous guided story. Current orientation begins at login near Rook; its loop vision is a selectable core entry. The October 14 decisions describe a core followed by optional story wings.
- **Trial completeness:** F-35 describes hands-on lessons, tools, and guildmaster offers. Current station previews list many of those activities without delivering their full trials. The farm, vision, Vault, and net lesson have separate working behavior.
- **Combat:** the station preview says Pick your weapon. The October 14 decision and F-44 require both melee and ranged teaching.
- **Pet choice and terminology:** F-35 and the farm use dog or cat. The graduation picker offers dog, cat, fox, or bird when no farm pet was taken, and calls them companions. The evo implementation is an ordinary pet.
- **Graduation rewards and Young:** older descriptions give only the tutorial box and say the first loop ends Young. P371 and current graduation retain that box, add the first-loop box, and preserve Young.
- **Skipping:** P371 provisionally allows skipping to open island travel, while retaining an alternate rule requiring remedial graduation. Its note leaves the final policy for a decision.
- **Levels and pet progression:** the design links loops to level resets, skill rules, and evo growth. Current graduation records loop 1, but level and skill reset work is unfinished and evo pets still use a separate owner-loop placeholder.
- **Dungeon death:** F-45 calls for resurrection at the landing. P141 reports that Young ghosts are instead moved to a surface death destination by the existing Young system; the landing ankh serves ghosts who remain below.
- **Built and placed:** P276 records the farm lesson built but its world placement and client checks unfinished. P188 records the Burrows map route built but leaves world seeding and the client walk unfinished. Neither record establishes current availability on the running shard.

# Town Houses (planned)

**Coming later.** Town Houses are planned. This page describes future housing, not current rental instructions.

Town Houses will let you rent a home in an existing town building. The plan includes homes inside guarded towns, with town guard protection continuing inside them. You will also be able to sublet a room, meaning you rent part of your town house to another player.

Your town house will count as your account's one house. You will need to choose between renting a town house and owning an ordinary house. It will not give your account an extra house slot.

Town Houses are separate from the planned inn rooms and guild halls. Inns and halls will not use the Town Houses system.

Locations, prices, payment schedules, storage limits, and room rental rules are still being decided. Outright purchases are also undecided. Details about ending a rental and recovering belongings will need confirmation before release.

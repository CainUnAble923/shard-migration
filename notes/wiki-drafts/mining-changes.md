# Mining changes: stone, ore quotas, and pickaxe restoration

Player guide draft, October 24, 2026. Prices below use the source defaults. Check the price shown in your game menu before buying or restoring.

The Miners' Compact now asks you to learn stone mining before taking Stone Contracts. Its raw ore orders track what you mine after accepting, and pickaxe restoration lets you cover missing Mining Vouchers with gold.

## Learn stone mining first

Read **Mining For Quality Stone** to learn stone mining. You need **100.0 base Mining** to read it. Put the book in your backpack and double-click it. A successful reading consumes the book and teaches that character the skill.

You can get the book through either route:

| Seller | Requirement | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Miners' Compact Liaison, **Learn Stone Mining** | Compact member at **Journeyman**, 5,000 Compact Standing | **50 Mining Vouchers** |
| Stone Crafter | No Compact rank required | Listed base price: **10,625 gold** |

Visit **Garrett Ashveil**, the Compact Liaison at the New Haven mine camp, for the Compact copy. You can buy it before reaching 100.0 Mining and save it until you can read it. After you learn stone mining, Garrett's dashboard shows **Stone mining: learned** in place of the book button.

Learning also opens Stone Contracts in **Work Orders** and your **Compact Dispatch Ledger**. Until then, the Available list hides them and shows a hint about the book. Individual contracts can still require standing or a reported vein discovery. A Stone Contract you already held before this change can still be turned in.

To gather granite after learning, enable mining both ore and stone through your mining tool's context menu, then mine mountainsides. Keep at least 100.0 base Mining. Stone is an occasional result alongside ore.

## Accept an ore quota before mining

Open Garrett's **Work Orders**, or double-click the **Compact Dispatch Ledger** in your backpack. Accept the quota first, then mine its metal with that character.

| Quota | Mine after accepting | Requirements to accept | Completion rewards |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mine Iron Ore** | **100 Iron Ore** | Compact membership; no extra standing or Mining skill gate | 75 Standing, 4 Mining Vouchers, 60 gold |
| **Mine Dull Copper Ore** | **50 Dull Copper Ore** | Journeyman, 5,000 Standing; 65.0 Mining; a reported Dull Copper vein | 350 Standing, 16 Mining Vouchers, 400 gold |

Each quota counts only its named metal. Iron from a Dull Copper vein still counts as iron. Ore mined before acceptance, bought, traded, or picked up adds no progress. Smelting stored ore adds no progress either.

Check **Active** for your progress, such as **60/100**. Once the target is met, use **Turn In** to claim the rewards. The quota takes no ore, so you do not need to carry it back. Ingot orders and Stone Contracts still require their listed materials.

### If you already held an older ore contract

Contracts accepted before quotas keep an alternative way to finish:

- **Raw Ore Sample** can be completed by turning in **100 Iron Ore**, or by mining **100 Iron Ore** for the new quota.
- **Ore Refinement Run** can be completed by turning in **150 Iron Ore**, or by mining **50 Dull Copper Ore** for the new quota.

The old material turn-in consumes the ore. Finishing through mining takes none. The Active tab identifies the old turn-in option. Newly accepted quotas require fresh mining.

## Restore your pickaxe when vouchers run short

Open Garrett's **Restoration (Jacob's Pickaxe)** page for your unlocked pickaxe tier. If you are short of Mining Vouchers, choose the offered gold option. It opens a page showing both prices before you confirm.

Each missing voucher adds **100 gold** to the normal restoration price. Your existing vouchers are still used, and the usual ingots and base gold are still required. This option covers a restoration's shortfall; it does not add vouchers to your balance.

For example, restoring **Jacob's Reinforced Pickaxe** normally costs **4 Mining Vouchers, 100 Iron Ingots, 15 Dull Copper Ingots, and 1,000 gold**. With **1 voucher**, you are missing 3. The gold option costs that **1 voucher**, the same ingots, and **1,300 gold** in total.

Gold and materials can come from your backpack and bank. If you lack enough gold, the Liaison tells you the total price and shortfall. With enough vouchers, use the regular restoration option. You still need the pickaxe tier on your account's record and no active copy.

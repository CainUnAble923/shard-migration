# New towns and keeps

Draft for Shattered Legacy's planned Siege of Sosaria world, October 27, 2026. These are map landmarks, not a list of verified services or open quests. Bank and moongate references below identify the author's mapped landmarks. Guards, vendors, travel routes, and housing limits still need placement and checks.

Names and locations come from **Siege of Sosaria Trammel Map 3.0 by Zigholtul**. Suggested local stories come from Shattered Legacy's quest drafts. They are marked as proposals and are not available quests.

## Towns and settlements

### Valunstead

A walled town with a moated keep in the northwest. Its mapped bank is at (484, 434, 15), and a gate landmark stands at (655, 397, 41). A proposed local story follows missing relief supplies and delivery records.

### Hammerhill

A new town with a mapped bank at (1530, 994, 5) and a gate landmark at (1541, 1081, -11). Hammerhill Sewers is a separate custom dungeon site, planned to remain sealed until populated. Repairing stolen pump equipment is a proposed local story.

### Kameron Kove

A new settlement with a mapped bank at (1297, 2470, 0) and a gate landmark at (1353, 2451, 12). The gate is planned to remain scenery initially. Local residents and services have not been verified for this draft.

### Haven, the planned New Haven

The author's rebuilt Haven occupies the former Old Haven site. Its mapped landmarks include Uzeraan's Mansion, a bank, docks, and a smithy. The plan moves New Haven here and gives Rootdeep a cellar-hatch entrance in town; final directions await placement checks.

### Elmhaven

A named settlement in the Trinsic Jungle area. The author's region reference places it within x 1891-2049, y 3135-3263. Its residents, services, and best approach remain unverified.

### Kettleburg

A named settlement south of Skara Brae's main town area. The author's region reference places it within x 427-733, y 2316-2618. Shops and arrival routes remain unverified.

### Elandrim Nur Shaz

A named town area in Yew Forest, near the Fort Whitestone landmark. Its region reference is x 860-920, y 987-1052. No inhabitants or local history are established by the name.

### Fort Yondall

A named settlement with an author's region reference of x 3366-3413, y 308-406. It is included in the plan for new town residents and services. Its entrance and garrison remain unverified.

### Plunderers Peak

A named town area in the new Nujel'm desert country. Its region reference is x 3939-4074, y 1544-1710. The name alone does not establish its occupants or whether it is safe.

### Seraphim Peak

A named town area in the eastern desert country, with a region reference of x 4593-4804, y 1699-1810. Its buildings, services, and approach have not been checked for this draft.

### Skaddria Naddheim

A named town area west of the Trinsic Jungle landmarks, with a region reference of x 1442-1582, y 2926-3037. Residents and services remain unverified.

### Viligant Valley

A named town area in the eastern country, with a region reference of x 4835-5018, y 1910-2012. The spelling follows the author's region file. No local story or service roster has been established here.

## Keeps, castles, and forts

### Fort Whitestone

A new keep in Yew Forest, listed at (890, 1055, 32). A proposed relief-post story follows an injured patrol and a missing scout. The quest and its residents remain proposals.

### Fort Viridian

A Yew Forest landmark at (1015, 1003, 0). Its proposed local story concerns forged forestry boundaries and a survey crew. It does not establish harvesting rights or a new resource site.

### Castle Dunbain

A castle north of Britain, listed at (1333, 1377, 0). A proposed investigation follows relief supplies that never reached their destinations. Its steward and guards are proposed characters.

### Fort Stormhammer

A fort north of Britain, listed at (1490, 1323, 2). A proposed armorer's story concerns a missing maker's pattern roll and equipment for a patrol. Quest access and rewards are not established.

### Fort Silverblood

A fort in southern Britain Forest, listed at (1561, 2652, -5). A proposed healer's investigation follows a poisoned water shipment. Its people and encounters still need implementation.

### Tigerclaw Fortress

A fortress in Covetous Forest, listed at (2595, 1085, 8). A proposed story concerns displaced pupils, a borrowed banner, and a lost crossing survey. Travel to Tokuno through that story is only a proposal.

### Khalahandra Keep

An island keep with a listed entrance at (2505, 1707, 50). A proposed garrison story follows unpaid watchkeepers and stolen relief supplies. The entrance coordinate is a reference point, not a verified landing or travel route.

### Red Dagger Keep

A named keep with a region reference of x 4406-4693, y 1944-2203. Its entrance has not been verified. A proposed story follows forged passage papers and stranded families; it does not establish a working route to Malas.

### Red Baron Stronghold

A named stronghold in the Nujel'm desert country, with a region reference of x 4067-4174, y 1173-1283. Its name supplies no confirmed ruler, faction, or garrison. Entry and services remain unverified.

### Yew Fort of the Damned

The author's dungeon landmarks list this fort at (972, 768, 0). Its occupants and access have not been checked. Being listed here does not announce it as open content.

## Other named sites

Camp Ironfang and Ivanguard Manor are southern Britain Forest landmarks with proposed animal-rescue and household-contract stories. Mistveil Ruins, Dhampire Ruins, Glimmerwood, Dragonstorm Isle, and the Skypoint Islands also have quest proposals. None of those proposals confirms an active quest or facet unlock.

Fortress Calcifina is cataloged as a custom dungeon, not a town service center. Like the other custom dungeon sites, it is planned to stay sealed until its encounters and access are ready.

## Reading these locations

Coordinates use the author's map and the order (x, y, z). Region ranges locate a named area; they do not describe an entrance, an exact boundary, a safe tile, or a guard zone. Trammel and Felucca will use the same geography with different facet rules.

This list covers the new town names in the author's Trammel location list, additional town and stronghold names in his Trammel regions, the rebuilt Haven, and named fort landmarks. Familiar towns such as Britain and Yew remain part of the [world overview](world-map.md). Names from the separate Sosaria region set, such as Dawn and the town of Moon, are omitted because that set may be leftovers from another project.

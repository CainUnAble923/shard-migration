# The world map: Sosaria

Draft for the Siege of Sosaria world planned for Shattered Legacy 1.0, October 27, 2026. This page describes the planned world. It does not confirm that the map swap, town services, travel changes, or closures are running yet.

## A familiar world with new ground to explore

Shattered Legacy's Trammel and Felucca will use geography based on **Siege of Sosaria Trammel Map 3.0 by Zigholtul**. Familiar towns remain, alongside new settlements, keeps, islands, and dungeon sites. Both facets will share the new geography while keeping their own rules. Check which facet you are on before traveling or fighting.

Old UO maps can help you recognize names, but their routes and coordinates may lead somewhere different here. Use this wiki for local geography and access. Outside guides can still help with standard content, but a familiar dungeon name does not confirm an unchanged entrance or layout.

## Regions and landmarks

| Area | What to look for |
| --- | --- |
| Yew Forest and the northwest | Valunstead's walled town and moated keep, Fort Whitestone, Fort Viridian, and Mistveil Ruins add landmarks around the familiar Yew country. |
| Britain and its forests | Castle Dunbain and Fort Stormhammer lie north of Britain. Hammerhill is another town in the northern country. The southern forest includes Camp Ironfang, Dhampire Ruins, Fort Silverblood, and Ivanguard Manor. |
| Compassion Desert and Covetous Forest | The desert has a pyramid landmark. Tigerclaw Fortress stands in the Covetous Forest area, alongside familiar Cove and Vesper country. |
| The western and southern mainland | Kameron Kove and Kettleburg join the western settlements. Elmhaven lies in the Trinsic Jungle area. Glimmerwood adds a named woodland farther southeast. Some of its rough edges are planned to remain closed at first. |
| Nujel'm and Moonglow country | A new desert landmass joins Nujel'm to Moonglow. The author's named areas include Plunderers Peak, Red Baron Stronghold, Seraphim Peak, and Viligant Valley. Their services and routes still need verification. |
| Eastern and southern islands | Red Dagger Keep, the Skypoint Islands, Dragonstorm Isle, and the Isle of the Avatar offer landmarks beyond the mainland. Dunbarrow Fissure, Echoback Estuary, and Darkfathom Hollow are also named on the map; nearby unfinished areas are planned closures. |

See [New towns and keeps](towns-new.md) for individual entries. Named landmarks are places on the author's map. Their presence alone does not establish a safe route, an active quest, or a working moongate.

## What changes from classic UO

New Haven is planned to move into the author's rebuilt Haven, on the former Old Haven site. That town has mapped locations for Uzeraan's Mansion, the bank, docks, and smithy. The old New Haven plaza becomes forest and shore. Follow the updated town directions when the swap opens.

Rootdeep's planned entrance is a cellar hatch in the rebuilt town. Older directions through the ruined bank's collapsed wall belong to the previous map. The hatch's exact building and route will be published after placement and a walk-through.

Public moongates and teleporters need destinations that fit the new land. The extra gate landmarks at Hammerhill, Kameron Kove, and Dragonstorm Isle are planned to remain scenery at first. A visible gate ring is not a promise that it is on the public destination list.

Classic dungeons remain part of the world plan, with entrances and placements checked against the changed terrain. EA's Blackthorn dungeon is proposed to stay out of the 1.0 world because the map uses its old space for other dungeons. This refers to the dungeon, not Blackthorn Castle in Britain.

## What stays sealed at first

The map was released unfinished. The 1.0 plan closes unfinished ground and keeps the author's custom dungeons sealed until their encounters and entrances are ready. A finished-looking doorway does not mean a dungeon has opened.

Planned surface closures cover:

- The unfinished rooster-shaped island, in the Poultry Paradise area.
- The northeast snow islands with unfinished settlement edges.
- Bare ground in the area described by the survey as east of Haven.
- Unfinished ground around Echoback Estuary and Darkfathom Hollow.
- The worst unfinished cliff seams in Glimmerwood and the northeast islands.

Closed surface areas are planned to turn travelers back with a fog message and prohibit housing. Boats should be able to sail past. The exact boundaries and behavior still need to be checked in play.

The author's custom dungeon sites, including Hammerhill Sewers, Haven Catacombs, Dragons Den, Azurite Caverns, and Fortress Calcifina, are planned to open individually after they are populated and checked. Later announcements will identify which have opened. There is no opening date promised here.

## Travel to other facets

The map swap covers Trammel and Felucca. Quest routes to Ilshenar, Malas, Tokuno, and Ter Mur are separate proposals. No quest chain, loop requirement, or new travel permission is established by this page. Check the published travel rules when those routes are ready.

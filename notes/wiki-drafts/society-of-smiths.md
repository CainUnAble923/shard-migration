# Society of Smiths

Draft checked against project notes and source code on October 21, 2026. Features still to come are marked (planned). Availability and settings on the running shard have not been checked. Configurable numbers below are the source defaults.

## What the Society is

The Society of Smiths is the guild for Blacksmithy and Arms Lore. Its work turns ingots into weapons, armor, shields, and supplies for Britannia. You build standing through orders and spend **Smithing Seals** on tools, recipes, and rewards.

Start at **Forge and Anvil in New Haven, Trammel**. Open the Guild Directory with `[guild`, find the Society, and choose **Show me the way**. Join through the guildmaster's guild page. Joining is free, needs no skill or tribute, and starts a new member as Initiate.

This membership is separate from the stock NPC guild's paid joining system. Society membership, standing, and Seals belong to your character. Learned recipes belong to your account.

Joining gives your trade tools, a blessed **Smithing Guild Book**, and a blessed **Smith Guild Salvage Bag**. On the **Starter items** page, request the **Hammer of Hephaestus**. The first copy is free, with no skill task. This shares its reward record with the **It's Hammer Time** training quest, so receiving it by either route uses the same first claim.

The separate **Apprentice task** asks for 50.0 base Blacksmithy or 10 Iron Ingots. The ingots are consumed only if you use the tribute option. Completing it pays 25 standing and 10 Smithing Seals. Promotion still checks the skill requirement below.

## Ranks and recipes

For a new member, promotion needs both standing and base Blacksmithy. Item skill bonuses do not meet the promotion requirement. Earned promotions are retained if skill later falls.

| Rank | Standing | Base Blacksmithy | Recipe access |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Initiate | 0 | None | Starter kit and services; may buy the Band I pattern book. |
| Apprentice | 1,000, or completed Apprentice task | 30.0 | Band I book grant; may buy Band II. |
| Journeyman | 5,000 | 50.0 | Band II book grant; may buy Band III. |
| Master | 15,000 | 70.0 | Band III book grant; may buy Band IV. |
| Grandmaster | 50,000 | 90.0 | Band IV book grant; daily Band V scroll shop rotation, up to 3 selections. |
| Legendary | 100,000 | 100.0 | Up to 2 unknown Band V starter scrolls; daily rotation grows to up to 5 selections. |

The Legendary guild trial is (planned), with its requirement waived by default until built. It is separate from the League's story trials.

Some Smith menus display rank from standing alone. That display can differ from the promotion and recipe system. This discrepancy is recorded under Contradictions.

Basic designs with a minimum crafting skill of 30.0 or less need no pattern book. Higher designs are hidden until learned. The ordinary Smith pattern books cover these bands:

| Book | Recipe's minimum skill | Price in Seals |
|---|---|---:|
| Band I | Above 30 through 50 | 100 |
| Band II | Above 50 through 70 | 200 |
| Band III | Above 70 through 90 | 400 |
| Band IV | Above 90 through 100 | 800 |

Band IV includes the Exodus Sacrificial Dagger design. Designs with their own recipe scrolls keep that requirement. Band V shop scrolls cost **1,500 Seals plus 10 Platinum Ingots** each, when an unknown design is available.

Read a book or scroll from your backpack to teach the account. An unknown one is consumed; one already known is kept. Unread copies can be traded. Promotion grants skip books the account already knows. Learning a design does not remove its crafting skill or material requirements.

Repairing, smelting, and enhancing do not need these pattern books. A completed large Smith BOD also has a default **2 percent** chance to award a Band V Smith scroll.

Smiths can also craft repair deeds and kits, and mule equipment. The Society's **Field Smelter Rig** uses Blacksmithy and Arms Lore. Its schematic tiers I, II, and III become available at Apprentice, Journeyman, and Grandmaster respectively, with separate purchase costs.

## Your Smithing Guild Book

Keep the book in your backpack and double-click it. It gives access to work without another walk to the guildmaster.

| Page or action | What it does |
|---|---|
| Bulk Orders | Stores Smith deeds, shows progress, requests small or large orders, and turns in completed deeds. |
| Commissions | Requests client work and submits matching pieces from your backpack. |
| Seal Catalog | Opens the reward shop. |
| Completed orders setting | Chooses Always bank, Always cash out, or Ask each time. |
| Orders that still teach me | Chooses training-focused Society orders; on by default. |

Drag Smith BODs onto the book to file them. Other items cannot be stored inside it. Carried loose deeds also appear in its order view.

The League Register can bind the Society by taking an empty Smithing Guild Book. Unbinding returns a fresh book. The Register's League services have their own League rank requirements; the separate Smithing Guild Book already provides the remote services listed above.

## Three kinds of work

### Bulk order deeds

Bulk order deeds, or BODs, ask for quantities of a specified item, material, and sometimes exceptional quality. Request one through the Society's **Bulk Orders** menu or your book, then accept the offered deed.

The current Society limit is **3 small deeds and 1 large deed carried at a time**, with no offer cooldown. Requesting and turning in a large deed requires **70.1 base Blacksmithy**, with no Society rank requirement. Larger allowances from Society and League ranks are (planned).

Small deeds take matching items. Large Smith deeds take matching crafted items directly and still accept the corresponding filled small deeds. The **combine with contained items** option helps fill an order from a container.

Crafting with either Hephaestus hammer can automatically put a matching result into a carried Smith deed. Small deeds are checked before large deeds. A piece used this way is consumed into the order.

**Orders that still teach me** favors work that can raise Blacksmithy. At higher skill, eligible extended metals help keep armor orders useful for training. If nothing suitable teaches, the system falls back to difficult work you can make. Turning the setting off allows ordinary choices among items you can make. A turn-in skill check is a chance to gain, not a guaranteed skill increase.

| Completed orders setting | What you receive |
|---|---|
| Always bank, the default | Smithing Seals saved toward catalog purchases. |
| Always cash out | Half the deed's usual old-style gold reward, its Fame, and a 1-in-40 chance at its old-style item reward. No Seals from that deed. |
| Ask each time | Choose bank or cash out for each completed deed. |

Both choices award the same Society standing and Blacksmithy turn-in checks. Small deeds give 1 check; large deeds give 2, or 3 for Gold and higher materials. Deed type, quantity, material, and quality affect Seal pay, so there is no single payment for every small or large order.

You can turn in at the guildmaster, through your book, or at a regular smith. Members use the Society payout setting at regular smiths too. A non-member's town-vendor BOD points are a separate reward system from Smithing Seals.

### Guild Contracts

Open **Guild Contracts** for supply work such as iron deliveries, tools for miners, weapons for guards, and armor sets. You can hold **3 active contracts for this guild**, separately from deeds and commissions.

Each contract lists its standing and skill requirements and pays standing, Seals, and gold. Requirements can differ from the promotion ladder. Materials for completion can come from your backpack and bank.

For example, **Iron Ingot Delivery** asks for 250 Iron Ingots and pays 100 standing, 5 Seals, and 50 gold. It has no skill or standing requirement beyond membership.

### Commissions

Commissions are named clients' requests. A small commission wants one piece; a large commission wants an armor set, delivered one piece at a time. You can hold **3 small commissions and 1 large commission**, separately from BODs and Guild Contracts.

Use the guildmaster's **Commissions** page or your book. Check the requested item, material, and exceptional requirement before crafting. Submit matching crafted pieces from your backpack. Store-bought pieces are refused. Completed work pays the displayed Seals and standing and gives a Blacksmithy skill check. A large commission pays when the set is complete.

Current Smith commissions have no completion deadline. The guildmaster's commission menu applies a default **5-minute wait** before new small requests after an abandonment.

Moving commissions to the League, adding Renown, recurring clients, deadlines, and Infamy penalties are (planned). Custom commission recipe rewards are also (planned); the current reward hook has an empty default selection.

## Hammer of Hephaestus

The Society's blessed legacy hammer remembers your work in each metal. Its skill bonus applies while you hold a usable hammer. Carrying it in a bag gives no skill bonus.

| Hammer | Maximum uses | Passive recharge | Familiarity limit per metal | Benefit |
|---|---:|---|---:|---|
| T1, Hammer of Hephaestus | 150 | 1 use per 30 minutes | 1,000 | +5 Blacksmithy while held; familiarity gives up to a 20 percent chance to refund a use in that metal. |
| T2, Reinforced Hammer of Hephaestus | 400 | 1 use per 15 minutes | 2,500 | +10 Blacksmithy while held, plus up to +2.5 from familiarity across the metal collection. |

Successful crafts can raise familiarity. A normal gain adds 1; an exceptional gain adds 3. Gains become less frequent near the limit. Use the hammer's **Metal Familiarity** context-menu entry to inspect your progress.

A spent hammer loses its skill bonus and can recharge. For an immediate refill, use **Hammer of Hephaestus Upgrades** at the guildmaster. Either tier costs **10 Seals and 200 Iron Ingots** to restore to full uses.

Upgrading T1 to T2 needs a usable T1 hammer, **5,000 Society standing**, and **80.0 Blacksmithy as shown with bonuses**. The cost is **25 Seals, 500 Iron Ingots, 100 Valorite Ingots, and 10,000 gold**. The old hammer is replaced and its familiarity carries forward. Materials and gold can be taken from your backpack and bank.

T3 resource efficiency and T4 exceptional-quality bonuses are (planned).

## Metals beyond Valorite

Blacksmithy continues through these extended materials. Mining, ore smelting, and salvage use the corresponding metal thresholds too, each against Mining rather than Blacksmithy.

| Metal | Blacksmithy to work it; Mining threshold | Crafting gain ceiling | Material character |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Platinum | 112.5 | 125.0 | Physical and cold resistance, durability. |
| Toxic | 125.0 | 137.5 | Poison resistance and damage. |
| Blaze | 137.5 | 150.0 | Fire resistance and damage. |
| Frost | 150.0 | 162.5 | Cold resistance and damage, with a fire-resistance penalty. |
| Obsidian | 162.5 | 175.0 | Physical resistance and durability. |
| Mythril | 175.0 | 187.5 | Energy resistance, energy damage, and luck. |
| Adamantium | 187.5 | 200.0 | Physical resistance and high durability. |
| Celestial | 200.0 | 200.0 | Mixed elemental bonuses and luck. |

The gain ceiling describes how far a metal can extend a recipe's training range. It does not guarantee gains or remove recipe requirements. Dawnstone is a separate starter metal worked at 45.0 Blacksmithy, outside this ladder.

Exceptional gear gets **1.5 times its positive material bonuses, rounded half up**. For example, a material's +5 resistance bonus becomes +8. Negative penalties stay unchanged. Runic properties and the separate bonuses for exceptional quality are not multiplied by this rule. Normal resistance caps still apply.

## Seals, salvage, and rewards

Standing measures progress; Seals are the balance you spend. Spending Seals does not spend standing.

The League's **Requisitions** also offer Smith-themed requests for magic armor, durable weapons, and shields. Matching found or crafted items pay Society standing, Renown, and gold rather than Smithing Seals. See the [League guide](league.md) for the board and its Leads.

The join bag recycles eligible metal gear near a forge, with a smithing tool in your backpack. Choose **Salvage Ingots** from its context menu. Mining controls material eligibility and ingot returns. Successful metal salvage also pays members Seals by material, from **1 for Iron to 30 for Celestial**. The item is consumed, and some of its material returns as ingots.

Open the **Seal Catalog** from the guildmaster or book. Current prices include:

| Reward | Seals |
|---|---:|
| Sturdy Shovel or Sturdy Pickaxe | 150 each |
| Gargoyle's Pickaxe | 300 |
| Prospector's Tool | 600 |
| Mining Gloves +3 / +5 | 900 / 1,800 |
| Powder of Temperament | 450 |
| Smith Guild Salvage Bag | 750 |
| Ancient Smithy Hammers, by catalog row | 300 / 600 / 1,500 / 3,000 |
| Dull Copper / Shadow Iron / Copper / Bronze Runic Hammer | 600 / 1,050 / 1,650 / 2,400 |
| Gold / Agapite / Verite / Valorite Runic Hammer | 10,800 / 16,200 / 27,000 / 45,000 |

The catalog currently checks Seal cost, without separate rank gates for these items. Recipe purchases have the rank requirements described earlier.

Post-Valorite runic hammers are (planned). Their catalog rows are placeholders, not available purchases. Guild-themed house add-ons are also (planned) for this reward catalog.

## Contradictions

These differences remain on record without deciding between the sources.

- **Name and membership:** F-9 and P14 retain Smiths' Fellowship and account-wide membership descriptions. P52 and current code use Society of Smiths; current guild membership and balances are per character.
- **Rank:** some Smith menus show rank from standing alone. Current promotion also checks base skill and recognizes the Apprentice task. Those displays can disagree.
- **Recipe inventory:** P78 reports no Smith Band IV book. Current Exodus crafting code registers a 95-skill dagger in that book band.
- **Large deeds and limits:** P32 required Journeyman as well as 70.1 Blacksmithy. P42 and current code require skill only. F-25 decides larger rank-based allowances and says 3 small / 1 large was temporary; current code still uses those fixed limits.
- **Turn-in checks:** F-18 approves one check per five items. Current code awards 1 for a small deed, 2 for a large deed, or 3 at Gold and higher.
- **Cash out:** F-25 describes gold, Seals, and an item chance. P55 and current payout code give reduced gold and an item chance, with standing and skill checks but no Seals in cash-out mode.
- **Economy and mining:** P56's prices and all-extended-ore-at-100 Mining description precede P57's tripled catalog prices and tiered mining requirements. Current code contains the latter rules.
- **Training orders:** P61 records many high-skill orders that no longer teach. P170 records a material-selection fix, present in current source and its high-skill verification cases.
- **Commissions:** F-37 decides a League move with Renown, deadlines, and penalties. P118/P122 and current source keep Smith commissions at the Society, without those deadline rules.
- **Book access:** P177/P179 mockups describe future book tiers and rank-gated remote services. The current Smith book already requests and turns in deeds remotely. P280's separate Register has League rank gates of its own.

# Guilds and orders

Draft assembled from the guild guides and glossary on October 22, 2026. Availability on the running shard has not been checked. Features still to come are marked (planned).

Start with the **Guild Directory** on the New Haven training board or open it with `[guild`. Choose a guild and **Show me the way**, then join at its guildmaster. Joining is free, with no skill or tribute required. You can join more than one guild. The separate **Apprentice task** comes after joining; crafting promotions can also require base crafting skill.

These memberships are separate from the stock NPC guilds' paid joining system.

The **League of Extraordinary Citizens**, or LXC, connects the guilds. Register with **Elara Voss** at the New Haven field office. League registration belongs to your account, while League rank belongs to each character. Individual guild membership, standing, and currency belong to your character.

## Which guild should I join?

| If you enjoy... | Start with... |
|---|---|
| Gathering and making your own gear | Miners' Compact for ore, Society of Smiths for metal equipment, or Foresters' Union for timber and wooden goods. These trades complement each other. |
| Crafting tools, clothing, or food | Tinkers' Union, Tailors' Circle, or Maritime Brotherhood. Use the crafting guide for each trade's patterns and deed rewards. |
| Magic and enchanted equipment | Arcane Society for casting and Inscription, Artificers' Order for Imbuing, or Order of the Alembic for potions and Glassblowing. |
| Animals and wilderness travel | Rangers' League for animal delivery work, pet tools, Archery, and Tracking. |
| Stealth, locks, and traps | Thieves' Den for its thief services and rank perks; Tinkers' Union for trap-kit patterns. |
| Healing or civic work | Healers' Covenant for Healing and Anatomy, or the Custodians for cleanup and its reward store. |
| Fighting, bard skills, or another magic tradition | Warriors' Brotherhood for melee and Throwing, Bards' Consortium for music, Twin Paths Dojo for Bushido and Ninjitsu, or Keepers of the Last Door for Necromancy and related skills. |
| Appraisal and Begging | Merchants' Exchange. Its specific reward path is not covered by the current guild guides. |
| Work across several interests | Register with the League and join the guilds that suit your activities. League rank and individual guild ranks are separate. |

## Contradictions

These differences remain on record without resolving the sources. The linked guides retain more detailed contradiction lists.

- **Joining and membership:** F-9's early descriptions use account membership and skill-or-tribute joining. The guild guides describe free character membership with a separate Apprentice task. Some guild help still calls the task a joining requirement.
- **Names:** F-9 retains Smiths' Fellowship, while the Smith guide uses Society of Smiths. The Healers guide reports that its contract ledger still says Healers' Circle rather than Healers' Covenant.
- **Alchemy's former home:** F-20 says it moved from the Arcane Society. F-9 and the Alembic guide record the Healers' Covenant as its former home.
- **Ranks:** several guild dashboards use standing alone, while general guild pages also recognize the Apprentice task. Craft promotions add base-skill requirements, so the displayed ranks can disagree.
- **Bulk order rewards:** F-20 describes guild-currency and teaching-order rewards across crafts. The crafting guide describes ordinary craft reward points outside the Society's separate Seal system. The gathering guide also records planned turn-in skill checks absent from current gathering and taming contracts.
- **Commissions:** F-37 places client commissions at the League with Renown, deadlines, and penalties. The Smith guide describes Society commissions without those deadlines. Their League move is (planned).
- **Requisitions and Renown:** F-47 and F-48 retain future-build descriptions, while the League guide records requisitions and Leads as built. Older F-13 text says Renown has no spending use; the League guide describes reward shops that spend it.
- **Halls:** F-49 calls Royal City already posted. The Artificers guide distinguishes its existing NPC post from a full headquarters (planned). The guides retain unsettled building and facet choices for several main halls.

# Your first companion

Your tutorial companion is an evo starter pet: an animal that follows you, fights beside you, and earns evolution XP by helping defeat creatures.

*Draft checked against source on October 24, 2026. Tutorial placement, shop stock, and settings on the running shard have not been verified.*

## Choose your pet

At graduation, Rook opens a window offering a **dog, cat, fox, or bird**. Press **Choose** beside the one you want. Keep **one follower slot free**. Your starter pet uses one slot even as it evolves.

Closed the window? Double-click Rook and select **Choose your companion**. The choice stays available until you take a pet. If you already took a dog or cat from the tutorial Farmhand, graduation does not offer another pet.

Your pet appears beside you and follows you. A second window lets you choose its color. That choice is permanent and carries into later forms. If you close the color window, double-click your living pet to reopen it.

## Bonding and basic care

**Your starter pet arrives bonded.** You do not need to wait a week or use a bonding potion. Bonding lets it become a ghost when it dies so it can be brought back. It can still be hurt and killed.

Say `all follow me` nearby to have your pets follow you. Say `all stay` to leave them in place, or `all attack` and choose a target to send them into a fight. These orders address all your pets nearby.

Bandages on your animal use **Veterinary and Animal Lore**. Start with easy fights and watch its health. Avoid **Release**: a released evo starter pet cannot be tamed again.

## If your pet dies

Keep its ghost with you. Do not leave it behind for a long time. For the tutorial vets and the elixir below, **wait until the pet's body has faded**. If you are told its spirit lacks cohesion, wait a few minutes and try again.

### Visit a tutorial vet

At the tutorial farm west of New Haven, **Wren Tallow, the Farmhand**, directs you to **Tobin the vet** beside her when your ghost pet comes near. Wren gives directions; Tobin performs the resurrection. There is also a tutorial vet by the New Haven stables.

Bring your ghost pet along, walk close to the vet, or double-click the vet nearby. Choose your pet in the resurrection window.

- The service is **free while you are Young**.
- After that, the window shows a fee paid from your **bank**. A starter evo pet costs **101 gold**.
- The pet loses **0.2 in each skill** when the vet brings it back.

Use these tutorial vets. Other vets do not currently offer this resurrection service.

### Use an elixir of rebirth

Your graduation box contains **one blessed elixir of rebirth**. Open the box in a guarded town or your own house, then keep the elixir in your backpack.

To use it, be alive, stand within **two tiles** of the ghost pet, double-click the elixir, and target the pet. Its body must already have faded. A successful resurrection consumes the elixir.

For replacements, animal trainers list the elixir for **500 gold**. **Rangers' League members** can buy it from their guildmaster's member menu for **375 gold**.

After either method, heal your pet before fighting again. It returns with only **10 health**.

## Stable your pet

Bring your living pet to an **Animal Trainer**. Say `stable`, then target your pet. Stabling costs **30 gold**, taken from your backpack or bank. Your pet must be out of combat, and you need room in your stable allowance.

Say `claim` near an Animal Trainer to retrieve your stabled pets. You need enough free follower slots for them to return. A stabled pet does not use your traveling follower capacity.

**Dead pets cannot be stabled.** Bring yours back first, then stable it if you want to travel without it.

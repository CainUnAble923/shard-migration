# Thieves' Den

Draft checked against project notes and source code on October 21, 2026. Features still to come are marked (planned). Availability on the running shard has not been checked.

## What the Den is and how to join

The Thieves' Den is Britannia's guild for thieves, scouts, lockpickers, and trappers. Membership opens monster stealing, and higher ranks add places to work, trap perks, and pack mule schematics.

Open the Guild Directory with `[guild`, choose **Thieves' Den**, and use **Show me the way**. Its New Haven guildmaster keeps the back room of the Bountiful Harvest Inn. At the guildmaster, open **Talk** and choose **Join the guild**. Joining is free, needs no skill, and starts you as an Initiate.

Membership, standing, and scrip belong to your character. Each character joins separately, and you can belong to other guilds too. The Den's membership is separate from the stock NPC trade guild's paid membership.

Return with **20.0 base Stealing** and complete the **Apprentice task** at the guildmaster. It pays **25 standing and 10 Den scrip**, once, and raises you to at least Apprentice. You do not need 1,000 standing if you have completed this task.

## Ranks and what they open

Standing sets your rank. Spending scrip does not spend standing. Higher ranks keep the earlier perks.

| Rank | Standing requirement | What opens |
|---|---|---|
| Initiate | Join, no standing required | Starter services; monster stealing in Felucca dungeons, champion spawn areas on any facet, and the Rootdeep Burrows outside the Vault. Skill requirements still apply. |
| Apprentice | Complete the Apprentice task, or reach 1,000 | Buy the Shadow Cloak I mule schematic. |
| Journeyman | 5,000 | Monster stealing in dungeons on other facets; one extra deployed trap; buy the Shadow Cloak II mule schematic. |
| Master | 15,000 | Steal from bosses in permitted places; damaging and holding traps can affect bosses, with half their rolled damage and half their hold duration; additional custom dungeon access (planned). |
| Grandmaster | 50,000 | Disarming another player's deployed trap always succeeds; buy the Shadow Cloak III mule schematic. |
| Legendary | 100,000 | The top rank. No additional Den-specific perk is built beyond the earlier ranks. |

To earn more standing, open **Requisitions** from the guild page or use the League's requisitions board. The Den requests magic goods, including general "no questions asked" items, Dexterity jewelry, fencing weapons with Hit Lower Attack, and leather armor with Night Sight. Match the actual request's properties and quality.

A turn-in consumes the item and pays Den standing, League Renown, and gold. Requests accept found or crafted goods. Requisitions do not pay Den scrip. See the League guide for the broader board and Leads system.

## Skills of the trade

| Skill | Its part in the Den's trade |
|---|---|
| Stealing | Take items and attempt the Den's monster steals. |
| Snooping | Look inside another creature's or person's pack. |
| Hiding | Conceal yourself. Nearby combat can prevent ordinary Hiding. |
| Stealth | Move while hidden. Hide first, walk carefully, and mind your armor. |
| Lockpicking | Open locked containers. |
| Detect Hidden | Search for hidden targets, including deployed traps. |
| Remove Trap | Disarm traps, set deployable kits, and pack your own traps away. |
| Poisoning | Apply poison and load poison into gas trap kits. |

Joining hands you the creation tools associated with the guild's skills. It does not raise those skills.

### Monster stealing

Use **Stealing** and target the creature itself to attempt its monster reward. You must be a Den member, have the rank for that location and target, and have at least **60.0 Stealing**. Bosses require Master even in the Burrows or a champion spawn area. Master rank does not make ordinary wilderness a monster stealing location.

| Stealing | A successful monster steal can give |
|---|---|
| Below 60 | No Den monster reward. |
| 60 to below 100 | Gold, 1 to 3 Den scrip, lockpicks, a small potion, or bandages. |
| 100 and up | Special consumables, stolen crafting components, recipe scrolls, or a rare loop scroll fragment. |

Special consumables include the Seed of Life, Mana Draught, balms, lotions, and Gem of Salvation. Some Burrows creatures can also yield an extra family part. Higher Stealing improves the success chance through 200, and more famous creatures can add a bonus. Legal attempts can train Stealing on a success or a miss; more famous creatures support higher skill gains.

A creature gives only **one successful monster reward**, shared across thieves. A miss adds a **5-second** wait on that creature, reveals you, and turns the creature against you. Normal skill delays still apply.

Pets, summons, pack mules, tutorial creatures, and creatures inside the Rootdeep Vault cannot give these monster rewards. The Vault's practice trap lesson is a separate activity.

### Hiding, Stealth, and trap placement

You can set traps openly or while hidden. Hidden placement takes **1 second**, adds concealment, and spends a Stealth step. A failed Stealth check reveals you, but the trap is still placed. The first trigger against an idle creature can gain an ambush damage bonus.

The owner and the owner's party can see deployed traps. Other players can reveal them with Detect Hidden. Some sharp-eyed monsters can spot a trap before triggering it.

## Crafts and stolen materials

The Den's recipes use Tailoring, Alchemy, and Tinkering. Learn their recipe scrolls before crafting; recipe knowledge belongs to your account. Monster stealing can supply the scrolls and components. The guild's **Mule module schematics** page sells its pack mule designs separately.

| Item | Craft menu minimum | Ingredients | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shadowsilk Cloak | Tailoring 100 | 14 cloth, 10 shadowsilk thread | Stealth +5 and Hiding +3 while worn. |
| Thief's Smoke Bomb | Alchemy 100 | 1 egg, 3 ember ash, 1 whisperleaf | A smoke bomb usable with Hiding 50 and 10 mana. |
| Pickpocket's Charm | Tinkering 100 | 2 gold ingots, 1 lucky fang, 2 shadowsilk thread | A ring with Stealing +5 and Snooping +5. |

Shadowsilk thread and ember ash can come from weaker monsters. More famous monsters add whisperleaf, thief's wire, and lucky fangs to the component pool. Thief's wire drops, but has no built recipe use in this set.

The **Thief's Smoke Bomb** uses Hiding instead of the ordinary smoke bomb's Ninjitsu requirement. Carry it in your backpack. It allows a Hiding attempt during combat, still respects the skill delay, and does not guarantee that you hide.

### Shadow Cloak for a pack mule

The mule's **Shadow Cloak** is a different item from the wearable Shadowsilk Cloak. Buy schematics at the Den guildmaster, or learn a schematic found through monster stealing. The purchase requirements below apply to buying it.

| Module tier | Rank to buy | Schematic price | Skills to craft | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I | Apprentice | 20 Den scrip and 5 Mining Vouchers | Tailoring 50, Hiding 30 | The mule hides when its nearby owner hides. |
| II | Journeyman | 50 Den scrip and 10 Mining Vouchers | Tailoring 70, Hiding 50 | It stays hidden while walking beside its hidden, unmounted owner. |
| III | Grandmaster | 120 Den scrip and 25 Mining Vouchers | Tailoring 90, Hiding 70 | It can hide when attacked, with a 30-second cooldown. |

Higher tiers also require the previous tier's module when crafting. Fitting a module needs a suitable mule tier and a free module slot. The schematic price buys knowledge, not a finished module or its crafting materials.

## Deployable traps

Tinkers craft the kits. **Remove Trap** sets them. You do not need Den membership to set ordinary traps, but Den rank adds the perks listed above. Learn any required Tinkering patterns through the Tinkers' Union.

These are the built default skill requirements:

| Kit | Tinkering to craft | Remove Trap to set | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Snare net | 30 | 0 | Holds one creature. |
| Caltrops | 40 | 20 | Damages, bleeds, and slows a creature. |
| Dart | 50 | 30 | Fires loaded fukiya darts; poisoned darts keep their poison. |
| Gas | 60 | 40 | Poisons eligible creatures in its area. |
| Alarm | 70 | 30 | Reports the creature and the trap's location to its owner. |
| Bear trap | 80 | 50 | Holds, damages, and bleeds a creature. |
| Blast | 90 | 60 | Deals fire damage in an area. |
| Shackle | 110 | 80 | Holds eligible creatures in an area. |

Drop a poison potion onto a gas kit, an explosion potion onto a blast kit, or fukiya darts onto a dart kit before setting it. Loading gas uses Poisoning, can fail, and consumes the potion either way. Poisoning limits the loaded poison's strength. The explosion potion's strength determines the blast payload.

Carry the kit in your backpack, double-click it, and target clear ground within **2 tiles**. Stand still while arming. You cannot set it while mounted or flying, in an active guarded town, in a house, or on obstructed spots such as doorways and travel entrances.

Your normal limit is **1 trap plus 1 for every full 50 Remove Trap**, with one more at Den Journeyman. Spent traps still count until packed away or decayed. A separate area limit also prevents crowded trap fields.

Under the default rules, traps catch monsters on every facet, including Felucca. Players, pets, summons, and vendors do not trigger them. Ordinary traps need their owner alive, online, on the same facet, and within **12 tiles**. The alarm ignores that distance but still needs its owner alive, online, and on the same facet.

Charges run out. Double-click a spent trap to reset it, or drop a fresh payload onto a spent gas, blast, or dart trap. Nothing resets itself on a timer. Deployed traps normally decay **30 minutes** after setting or resetting, or **2 minutes** after becoming spent.

Use **Remove Trap** on your own deployed trap to pack it back into a kit. Detect Hidden and Remove Trap let you find and salvage someone else's trap; Grandmasters always succeed at the disarm. Setting, resetting, disarming, and triggering traps can train Remove Trap.

Ground traps cannot catch flyers. Physical traps do not affect some incorporeal creatures. Bosses need Master for damaging and holding traps, but the alarm can report them at lower ranks.

In the Rootdeep Stacks, tutorial participants can receive two practice nets. Catch a Penny Rat or Hoarding Rat to complete the trap lesson. Practice nets need no skill, hold briefly, and are only for the Burrows. Some deployable trap artwork is temporary.

## Starter rewards and currency

At the guildmaster's **Starter items** page, members can take a **Bag of Smoke Bombs** and **Twilight Jacket** free, once each per character in the current life. Taking either closes its matching New Haven training quest. If the trainer already gave you that reward, the guild records it as received.

The bag contains ordinary smoke bombs, which need **Ninjitsu 50** to use. Den membership does not replace that requirement. The Twilight Jacket is blessed armor. It is separate from the crafted Shadowsilk Cloak.

A lost starter reward can be replaced for **10 Den scrip** if you no longer own a copy. Den scrip is kept in your character's guild balance. The Apprentice task pays 10, and the lower monster stealing tier can pay 1 to 3 on a successful scrip result. Replacements and mule schematics are built uses for it.

Standing promotes you; scrip buys Den services; League Renown is a separate reward from requisitions. A monster steal's scrip payment does not itself award standing.

## Contradictions

These differences remain on record without resolving the source notes or choosing new rules.

- **Joining:** P14 lists Stealing 20 as a joining task. F-9, P15, and the code make joining free and use Stealing 20 for the later Apprentice task.
- **Starter rewards:** P142 says the Bag of Smoke Bombs and Twilight Jacket do not exist. P15 and the starter code connect the Den to both rewards, and both item types and their quest rewards exist in pinned ModernUO.
- **Boss locations:** P142 says Masters can steal from bosses "anywhere." The code still requires a covered stealing location, with a separate exception for Exodus zealots. P195 also describes the zealot's rank as the first rank, while the code treats a zealot outside covered areas as requiring Journeyman.
- **Mule tier III:** P70 puts its schematic at Master. The current schematic code puts it at rank index 4, Grandmaster for the Den.
- **Scrip:** P49 says the Apprentice task is the only Den scrip income and replacements are its only use. P142 adds monster stealing income, and the mule code sells schematics for scrip and Mining Vouchers.
- **Trap training and perks:** P201 says Remove Trap requires Lockpicking 50 and Detect Hidden 50 and describes an Apprentice owner-name hover. The Endless Journey skill patch removes those prerequisites, and P275 records the hover as unbuilt. P142's earlier "nothing more" for Grandmaster also predates the trap salvage perk.
- **Trap details:** P201 includes payloads in crafting, cancellation on taking damage, and Tinkering gains reaching 160. P275 and the code load payloads afterward, cancel arming on movement or death, and cap these Tinkering craft ranges at 130. P275 describes the shackle's trigger reach as growing with Remove Trap; the code uses its fixed area radius.
- **Headquarters:** F-49 leaves the facet and building open. P151 proposes Buccaneer's Bath on Felucca as a judgment call. The guild location code lists the New Haven chapter, without that proposed headquarters.
- **Availability:** P217 labels monster stealing live and the Burrows map unbuilt. P275 records the map as built and traps as built but undeployed. These notes cover different snapshots; the running shard was not checked for this draft.
- **Recipe sources:** P142 also lists the general loot box recipe pool as a source of the new Den recipes. The current box code excludes their recipe IDs from that pool.
- **Consumable effects:** P142 says Balm of Protection has no effect because the Grizzle is absent. The current Grizzle code uses the balm when applying damage to players.
- **Fame examples:** P142's round-number Fame examples do not exactly match the code's rounded-down monster level calculation. This guide gives no numeric Fame cutoff for components or training.

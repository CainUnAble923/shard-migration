# Rootdeep Burrows

Player guide draft, checked against source on October 25, 2026. The entrance, encounters, and rules below are implemented in the checked source. Their availability on the running shard and the installed player map have not been verified.

## Who should go

Rootdeep is the starter dungeon beneath Old Haven on Trammel. The Vault introduces combat to active tutorial trainees. The ordinary Burrows beyond it offer progressively harder fights for new adventurers, with bosses best approached with friends and healing supplies.

You do not need to finish the Vault to explore the rest of Rootdeep. Graduates and other characters can take the south tunnel from the landing into the Warren. The Vault itself admits only active trainees and their pets, and closes to you after you earn credit for its boss.

Bring a weapon or spell supplies, healing supplies, and cures for yourself and your pets. Keep a following pet close when using the entrance, ladder, or crawlway. Pets on Follow, Come, or Guard orders travel with you only when within three tiles.

## Finding the entrance and getting out

From New Haven, head east into Old Haven's ruins and find the **ruined Second Bank of Haven**. Enter through the gap in its collapsed south wall. Inside is an ant-hill mound with a broad, dark mouth, around **3619, 2620 on Trammel**.

**Step into the dark mouth to go down.** The rim is not the entrance. You arrive on the dungeon's landing, where there is an ankh. The rope ladder on the landing's north wall is the way back up: walk to its foot. It returns you just outside the bank's broken south wall, clear of the mouth. Old Haven's ruins still contain undead, so watch your route outside too.

Use the physical entrance. Recall, Sacred Journey, gates, and teleporting from outside cannot bring you into Rootdeep. You cannot Mark a rune inside or cast a gate out. Recall out is allowed by the dungeon, subject to ordinary travel restrictions. Under the default tutorial rules, a character who has not earned the first loop must still graduate with the League recruiter before leaving New Haven island; Rootdeep counts as part of the island.

## The Vault: the tutorial route

The Vault is beside the landing. Visit it before graduating if you want its lesson.

| Area | What to expect |
| --- | --- |
| Counting Room | Ledger bats introduce fighting a swarm. |
| The Stacks | Penny rats and a Hoarding Rat. Hurting the Hoarding Rat calls more rats to its side. |
| Deposit Hall | Safe deposit boxes are hostile chest mimics. Watch the floor near them too. |
| Strongroom | **The Overdraft**, a slow living strongbox that hits much harder than the bats and rats. Leave room to retreat and heal. |

In the Stacks, the Echo gives eligible trainees practice nets. Place one in an aisle, step back, and let a rat approach. Holding a Penny Rat or the Hoarding Rat with your net completes the lesson. Listen for the System message.

When the Overdraft dies, trainees who helped damage it can receive credit, including through their pets. Simply standing nearby is not enough. Its wreckage contains a coin pouch, a Dawnstone ingot, bandages, and a trinket; these are shared corpse loot, not a separate guaranteed reward for every attacker.

The kill opens a crawlway in the Strongroom's south wall for **two minutes**. Credited living players can step onto it or double-click it from nearby to reach the Warren. It goes one way. You can return to the landing through the ordinary dungeon route.

**Loot before leaving.** The current Vault rules block reentry as soon as you receive Overdraft credit. Logging back in inside the Vault after that moves you to the landing. Dying or disconnecting before collecting your loot can therefore leave you unable to return to it. Keep the fight near the Strongroom; an existing issue can prevent the crawlway opening if the Overdraft dies too far from it.

## Beyond the landing

| Area | Creatures and dangers |
| --- | --- |
| The Warren | Burrow grubs, burrow scamps, tougher redcap scamps, burrow hogs, a rotworm, and a grubber. Start with manageable fights here. |
| Brood Pit | **The Burrow Mother** calls grubs during combat and has a poisonous bite. Her reinforcements make this more than a single-enemy fight. |
| The Tangle | Rootlings and poisonous sporecaps. The **Bramblehulk** in the Thicket can cause bleeding. |
| The Dawnseam | Dawnshards and the **Seamguard**, stone creatures with strong physical resistance. |
| The Gloom | Poisonous gloomlings, webbing gloomspinners, spellcasting gloomweavers, and a tough bloodworm. Webs can briefly paralyze you. |
| Queen's lair | Guards and **the Gloom Queen**, a spellcaster with poison and webs. Bring help and clear space to retreat. |

Watch the floor as well as the creatures. There are mushroom traps near the Vault's decoy chests, spikes on the Warren descents, a saw at the Thicket's gap, gas traps in the Gloom, and a stone-face trap at the Queen's doorway.

Loot includes Burrower Claws, Rootheart Sap, Dawnstone Shards, and Gloom Glands. The Mother and Queen also drop their own boss parts and jewelry. Some creatures supply starter materials, including Rootwood, Dawnstone, Gloomsilk, and Burrowhide from carving grubs or hogs. The proposed dungeon set upgrades that spend the parts are still planned.

## Young players: read this before going deeper

**Young status does not stop monsters attacking you in Rootdeep.** The dungeon limits hostile creatures attacking one Young player at once to three. Three attackers, a boss, spells, webs, bleeding, or a floor trap can still kill you. The limit is not a shield for your pet.

Young players normally retain immunity to being poisoned outside duels. That does not prevent other damage, and pets and characters without Young status still need cures. Graduation alone does not remove remaining Young time.

The landing's ankh is a resurrection point, but a Young death normally sends you to the New Haven healer area rather than leaving you beside that ankh. Plan for the walk back. The Vault's reentry warning above matters especially after an Overdraft kill.

For a first visit, learn the ladder route, try the Warren, and return while you still have supplies. Take friends for the Mother and Queen, and avoid rushing into unexplored rooms while retreating.

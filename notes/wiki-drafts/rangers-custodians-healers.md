# Rangers' League, Custodians, and Healers' Covenant

Draft refreshed for the Ranger elixir and Tracking Ping on October 25, 2026; other sections were checked on October 21. Features still to come are marked (planned). Availability and settings on the running shard have not been checked. Configurable numbers below are source defaults; planned Ping numbers are recommendations, not settled settings.

## Find your guild

These groups offer different ways to help Britannia: work in the wilderness, clean up unwanted items, or care for the injured.

Open the Guild Directory with `[guild`, choose a guild, and use **Show me the way** to find its guildmaster. The New Haven chapters are in Trammel.

| Guild | Skills or work | New Haven starting point |
|---|---|---|
| Rangers' League, also called the Outriders | Archery, Tracking, Animal Taming, Animal Lore, Herding, Veterinary, and Camping | Animal stables, with the Outriders' Guildmaster |
| The Custodians | Civic cleanup; no assigned skill | Sanitation Warden, upstairs in the civic hall |
| Healers' Covenant | Healing and Anatomy | Healer's Hall, with the Healer Guildmaster |

Joining through a guild page is free and requires no skill or tribute. You can join more than one guild. Membership, standing, and guild currency belong to your character. This is separate from the stock NPC guild's paid joining system.

Tools arrive when you join. Signature equipment is on the guildmaster's **Starter items** page, with the first claim free and no training task required. Taking a signature item shares its reward record with the matching New Haven training quest, so the two routes do not give two first copies. The guild claim does not grant the quest's temporary accelerated skill gain.

## Rangers' League

The Outriders are the Rangers' field company: hunters, scouts, animal tamers, and expedition guides. Their standing measures your progress, while **Trail Marks** are their currency.

Joining gives the guild's skill tools, a blessed **Outrider's Crook**, and a blessed **Hunter's Satchel**. Request **Heartseeker** for Archery and **Walker's Leggings** for Tracking from Starter items.

The separate **Apprentice task** asks for **30.0 base Archery or 25 Arrows**. Arrows are consumed only if you use the tribute option. Completing it awards **25 standing and 10 Trail Marks** and raises your general guild rank to at least Scout.

### Archery and Tracking

Use a bow or crossbow with its matching ammunition for Archery. Ordinary shots require a brief pause after moving. Weapon crafting and Fletching belong to the Foresters' Union, even though the Rangers use the finished weapons.

Use **Tracking** from the skills list, choose Animals, Monsters, Human NPCs, or Players, then select a target from the results. An arrow points toward the target. An empty search does not prove that nobody is nearby; tracking has skill and target checks.

Tracking can keep gaining through normal successful use above 100, up to your current skill cap. Its search radius counts at most **100 Tracking**, reaching **110 tiles**. More skill does not keep widening that search.

### Tracking Ping (planned)

The [Tracking Ping design](../cc-P563-tracking-ping-design.md) proposes a short radar sweep at **90.0 base Tracking**. It would count nearby creatures and show moving dots on TazUO's mini map, with a results window for choosing one quarry to follow with a tracking arrow. Clients without the Ping patch would receive a chat count and could open the results window. This ability is not implemented, and its numbers remain recommendations.

The proposed creature radius is **18 tiles at 90**, **24 at 100**, and **44 at 200**. A pulse would last **6 to 10 seconds**, cost **15 stamina**, and have a **30-second cooldown** after a successful one-second focus. Movement or damage during focus would cancel it. Equipment bonuses would not unlock or strengthen Ping. Ping itself would grant no skill gain or XP; normal Tracking would remain the training path.

The proposed sweep could sense creatures through dungeon walls only on the same mapped floor. Ping would be unavailable on unmapped dungeon floors and would exclude other players' private interiors. Visible players could appear from **120 base Tracking**, within **12 tiles and line of sight**, with a warning to the player being tracked. Hidden or invisible targets would stay excluded at every skill level. A player's dot would show their actual notoriety, not permission to attack.

### Field contracts and rank

Open **Field Contracts** from the member dashboard, or **Contracts** in the directory. Resource deliveries ask for goods such as leather, feathers, meat, wool, or camp supplies. Taming contracts ask for specified animals. Read the order's standing and skill requirements before accepting it.

For an animal delivery, keep your crook in your backpack, double-click it, and choose **Deliver Pet**. Target your own pet while it is following you. The animal is handed over permanently and counts toward an active matching contract. Choose animals you intend to give away.

Resource deliveries take the requested goods from your backpack and bank. Completed Ranger contracts pay the displayed standing, Trail Marks, and gold. You can hold **3 active contracts per guild**.

| Ranger rank | Standing |
|---|---:|
| Wanderer | 0 |
| Scout | 1,000, or completed Apprentice task |
| Outrider | 5,000 |
| Trailblazer | 15,000 |
| Beastmaster | 40,000 |
| Warden of the Wild | 80,000 |
| Legendary Outrider | 150,000 |

Some Ranger menus and contract gates read standing alone. Their display or access can differ from the Apprentice task's rank increase.

The starter crook adds **5 Animal Taming and 5 Peacemaking** while carried in your backpack. Its menu also offers shrinking a bonded, following pet into a figurine and instant bonding for your own unbonded, tamable pet. The starter crook's instant bond has a **48-hour cooldown**.

The Hunter's Satchel accepts leather, hides, bone, wool, feathers, and raw meat. It converts supported hides to their matching leather and reduces the weight of its contents by **30 percent**. Keep it in your backpack for automatic routing of supported resources.

Your Rangers' rank also improves a controlled pet's chance to gain skills. Animal Taming and Animal Lore above 100 contribute too. The Animal Lore window's skill pages show **Learns x...**. This raises the chance of a gain; it does not award free skill points. Loop contributions are (planned).

The League's **Requisitions** provide another source of Ranger standing. They request items such as magic bows and leather armor and pay standing, Renown, and gold. These are separate from Field Contracts and do not pay Trail Marks. See the [League guide](league.md).

### Bring back a dead bonded pet

Rangers' League members can open the Outriders' Guildmaster's **Member Dashboard** and choose **Buy an elixir of rebirth** for **375 gold**. Payment comes from your backpack or bank. Animal trainers sell the same elixir for **500 gold**, so the member price saves 25 percent. These purchases use gold, not Trail Marks.

Keep the elixir in your backpack. Once your dead bonded pet's body has faded, stand within **2 tiles** of its ghost, double-click the elixir, and target the pet. You must be alive, and the pet must be on a tile where it can be resurrected. A successful resurrection uses up the elixir. It cannot bring back an unbonded pet.

### The Rangers' Guild hall

The current chapter is at **New Haven's stables**. **Skara Brae headquarters (planned)** is the decided destination for the main hall. The site survey recommends the Counselor's Guild building; sharing the stock Rangers' Guild building remains an open choice. The survey placed neither.

New Haven remains a local chapter when the headquarters is built. The headquarters' full facilities, top-rank guild trial, and travel-network stop are (planned).

## The Custodians and Clean Up Britannia

The Custodians add portable and ground cleanup to **Clean Up Britannia**. They use **Clean Up points**, not the retired Civic Tokens.

Anyone can earn points through trash barrels and spend them in the Clean Up store. Joining the Custodians gives you the cleanup commands and a free, blessed, character-owned **Trash Bag**.

| Cleanup method | How to use it |
|---|---|
| Trash barrel, including a house barrel | Drop unwanted items into it. Points are paid when the items are destroyed. Use **Appraise for Cleanup** to check an item's value first. |
| Trash - Keep Britannia Clean barrel | Accepts items with turn-in value and pays immediately. |
| Your Trash Bag | Put items inside, right-click **Clean Up Britannia**, review the estimate, then choose **Dump Now**. **Return All Items to Pack** empties it without trashing its contents. |
| `[cleanup` | Members target one eligible ground item or monster corpse. |
| `[cleanupall` | Members target a point for a **10-tile radius** sweep, with a **60-second cooldown** after a successful sweep. |

Cleanup destroys the items. In the bag and ground commands, eligible litter with no point value is still destroyed. Loose blessed items, quest items, named items, exceptional weapons and armor, gold, bank checks, and nonempty containers are excluded. These bag and ground protections are not the trash barrel's rules.

A player's corpse is never eligible for Custodian cleanup. A monster corpse is eligible, and clearing it destroys everything still inside. Loot anything you want to keep first.

### Points and corpses

Item type, material, and supported magic properties determine point value. Fractional points count. Magic properties add value to eligible equipment that was not player-crafted; crafted equipment receives no extra points for its properties. Use appraisal rather than guessing a return.

Clearing a qualifying wild monster you or your party earned gives a base of **Fame × 0.001**, plus the Clean Up value of its remaining contents. For example, a dragon's base is **15 points**, before its contents. Pets and summons do not provide that base. A corpse without a qualifying kill record pays only for its contents.

You have two totals:

| Total | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Clean Up points | Your spendable balance |
| Lifetime points | Your Custodian rank; spending does not reduce it |

Points earned through barrels count toward lifetime standing too. Points you earned before joining are recognized when you join.

| Custodian rank | Lifetime points |
|---|---:|
| Volunteer | 0 |
| Junior Custodian | 25 |
| Custodian | 125 |
| Senior Custodian | 500 |
| Chief Custodian | 1,250 |

Use the directory's **Contracts** page for **Civic Contracts**. Each asks for an amount of **Clean Up points earned after you take it** (50, 150, 400, 800 or 2,000 by tier), and points earned before do not count. Turning one in takes no points: they stay yours to spend in the store. The same points never fill two contracts. The contracts pay gold (100 to 4,000), without extra standing or Civic Tokens. The Custodians do not post League requisitions.

**Civic waste bundles are retired.** Cleaning no longer makes them. If you still have some, hand them to the Sanitation Warden: each counts as **10 Clean Up points**, once.

### The Sanitation Warden and store

The **Sanitation Warden** handles membership, shows your balances and rank, replaces your Trash Bag, and opens the same store as a **Cleanup Officer**. You must be within **5 tiles** of one of these NPCs to use the store. Shopping does not require membership.

The shared catalog includes robes, equipment, decorative rewards, pigments, and charms. Its Custodian supplies include:

| Supplies | Clean Up points |
|---|---:|
| 100 Bandages | 15 |
| 5 Refresh Potions | 3 |
| 3 Greater Heal Potions | 5 |

The Trash Bag is free to members, with one owned bag at a time. Only its owner can dump it. Asking for a replacement deletes the old owned bag wherever it is, so empty it before replacing it.

The New Haven chapter is upstairs in the civic hall. **Britain headquarters (planned)** is the decided main-hall town; the survey proposes an empty brick hall near the First Bank. Its placement and a particular Cleanup Officer's location on the running shard have not been checked.

## Healers' Covenant

The Covenant's skills are **Healing and Anatomy**. Alchemy and Taste Identification belong to the **Order of the Alembic**.

Join at **Healer's Hall in New Haven**. The current kit includes **50 Bandages, Scissors, and a robe**. Request **Healer's Touch** for Healing and **Tunic of Guarding** for Anatomy from Starter items.

The separate **Apprentice task** asks for **50.0 base Healing or 25 Bandages**. Bandages are consumed only if you use the tribute option. Completion awards **25 standing and 10 guild scrip** and raises your guild rank to at least Apprentice.

League requisitions can earn more Covenant standing by accepting requested magic armor or jewelry. They pay standing, Renown, and gold. Check the request's actual properties before turning an item in.

### Healing with bandages

Double-click bandages and target yourself or a patient. Stay close until treatment finishes. Healing and Anatomy work together on people; Veterinary and Animal Lore serve animal and monster patients. Taking damage can make your fingers slip and weaken treatment.

| Treatment for people | Skill requirement |
|---|---|
| Restore hit points | Healing and Anatomy affect the result |
| Cure poison | At least **60.0 Healing and 60.0 Anatomy** |
| Attempt resurrection | At least **80.0 Healing and 80.0 Anatomy** |

These thresholds allow attempts, not guaranteed cures or resurrection. A poisoned patient receives a cure attempt instead of the ordinary hit-point heal. Healing can keep gaining through qualifying treatment above its stock ceiling, up to your current skill cap. High-skill bandage amounts use the shard's scaled healing curve.

### Resurrection policy

You do not need Covenant membership to receive an NPC healer's help or use bandages.

An ordinary healer and this shard's **Healer Guildmaster** can offer resurrection when your ghost walks from outside their **4-tile range** into it. You must be unfrozen, in line of sight, and on a tile that can hold you. Accept the resurrection window. If you are already beside the healer and nothing appears, walk out of range and back in.

The source defaults allow **criminals and murderers** to be resurrected by these healers. Being in war mode is not a refusal condition in this path. The policy does not remove criminal or murderer status.

Working ankhs offer resurrection too. The New Haven service code includes one beside the League Registrar's office. Some ankh graphics are decoration, so appearance alone does not establish that one works.

The player command `[SelfRes` is also built and enabled by default. It works while you are dead and requires a tile where you can be resurrected. Its availability on the running shard has not been checked.

The Covenant's **Empath Abbey headquarters near Yew (planned)** is the decided main-hall location. The survey recommends the Abbey church. New Haven remains the local chapter; the headquarters' full facilities, guild trial, and travel stop are (planned).

## Contradictions

These differences remain on record without deciding between the sources.

- **Joining and membership:** F-9 and P14 retain account-wide membership and older skill-or-tribute joining descriptions. P15 and current code use free character membership, with those requirements moved to the Apprentice task. The Outriders' information pages still describe the task rewards as joining rewards.
- **Ranger rank:** the general guild page recognizes the Apprentice task's Scout increase. The Outriders' dashboard and Field Contract gates use standing alone, so they can disagree.
- **Healers' name and kit:** P15's earlier kit includes Alchemy supplies. P77 and current skill ownership put them with the Alembic. The contract ledger still calls the Covenant "Healers' Circle."
- **Custodian currency and ranks:** F-3 retains the older Civic Token balance and 100 / 500 / 2,000 / 5,000 thresholds beside its later folding decision. P33 and current code use Clean Up points and 25 / 125 / 500 / 1,250 lifetime thresholds.
- **Corpse and property values:** P33 records a flat 0.10 corpse base and no extra value for magic properties. P175 and current code use earned Fame-based corpse credit and property value on non-player-crafted equipment.
- **Store coverage:** F-3 calls for the full OSI catalog. P33 records a smaller initial catalog, while P175 records an expansion with rewards still absent. The current store retains omitted rows.
- **Resurrection and ankhs:** P17 records missing New Haven healer spawns and a nonworking-healer report without verifying its cause in a client. P32 records the importer fix; P137 distinguishes decorative ankh art from working ankhs and adds a working one by the Registrar. This draft does not establish current world placement.
- **Hall identity:** F-49 decides Skara Brae for the Rangers. P151 proposes the Counselor's Guild and leaves sharing the stock Rangers' Guild open. No final building choice is established by these sources.

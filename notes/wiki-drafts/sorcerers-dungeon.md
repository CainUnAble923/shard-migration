# Treasures of Sorcerer's Dungeon

Player guide draft, checked against source on October 25, 2026. The event and rewards below are implemented in the checked source. The running shard's settings, event availability, and entrance route have not been verified in a client.

## Where and when

Treasures of Sorcerer's Dungeon is a Halloween event in **Sorcerer's Dungeon on Ilshenar**. The default annual schedule is **October 24 through November 2, inclusive**, using the shard's US Central date. That is ten calendar days. Automatic checks happen hourly, so the change can happen during the first hour of the opening or closing day. Staff can change the dates or switch the event on or off.

The dungeon entrance is around **546, 455 on Ilshenar**. Look for the pumpkins and flaming scarecrows. **The Researcher**, labeled as an Artifacts of Enchanted Origin Trader, stands nearby at **536, 456**. His personal name varies.

Bring healing supplies, cures, room in your backpack, and friends for the bosses. This is a sustained dungeon fight with spellcasters and dangerous melee enemies.

## What to do

1. Find the room where the event's undead are appearing. Zombies, skeletons, gargoyles, liches, and lich lords spawn there.
2. Help defeat them. After **80 event creatures die in the current room**, its boss appears. This is shared event progress, not an individual requirement of 80 kills per player.
3. Defeat the boss. After a pause of **one to three minutes**, the event begins spawning in the next room.
4. Watch for equipment **of Enchanted Origin** awarded directly to you. Return to the Researcher to exchange unwanted pieces for reward points.

The event moves through these bosses in order:

| Room | Boss |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Nightmare Fairy |
| 2 | Jack in the Box |
| 3 | Headless Elf |
| 4 | Abominable Snowman |
| 5 | Twisted Holiday Tree |
| 6 | Rabid Reindeer |
| 7 | Garish Gingerman |
| 8 | Stocking Serpent |
| 9 | Jack the Pumpkin King |

After Jack, the rotation starts again with the Nightmare Fairy. You can join a rotation already in progress.

**Jack throws pumpkins back at attackers when damaged.** He can also create unholy pumpkins nearby. They hatch into undead after five to ten seconds. Use a carving tool on them to try to destroy them before they hatch. A carving attempt can fail, so one attempt may not be enough.

## Artifacts and reward points

While the event is active, qualifying monster kills inside Sorcerer's Dungeon can award a random weapon, armor piece, shield, jewel, or hat of Enchanted Origin. You must be alive and have earned looting rights through combat participation. Simply standing in the room does not earn a roll.

An artifact is not guaranteed on each kill or boss. Your personal chance builds with qualifying kills, with higher monster fame and your Luck helping the tally. The tally resets when you receive an artifact. This hidden tally is separate from the points you spend at the Researcher.

Artifacts go into your backpack. If it cannot hold one, the game tries your bank, then places it at your feet if both are full. Watch the award message and keep space free. You can keep and use the equipment instead of trading it in.

To trade an artifact:

1. Put it directly in your backpack, outside any inner bag. The turn-in list only finds items at the top level.
2. Stand beside the Researcher and open his context menu. Choose **Turn In Artifacts of Enchanted Origin**.
3. Select the piece you want to surrender. **Each artifact is consumed for one point**, regardless of its properties.
4. Choose **Claim Rewards**, or double-click the Researcher, to open the reward list and spend your points.

The confirmation message may refer to artifacts of the Kotl. That wording is reused; these points belong to Sorcerer's Dungeon.

## Rewards

| Reward | Points | What it does |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Jack's Bag of Tricks | 75 | Gives a random treat or holiday oddity when used in October, with a 24-hour wait between successful uses. |
| Serpent's Jawbone | 50 | A house teleporter to a selection of Ilshenar destinations. Lock it down or secure it in a house, then double-click from nearby. Travel restrictions still apply. |
| Sterling Silver Ring | 50 | Hit Point Regeneration 3, Mana Regeneration 5, Damage Increase 75%, and a selectable +20 combat skill bonus. |
| Talons of Escaping | 50 | Gargoyle footwear with Dexterity Bonus 4 and Stamina Regeneration 1. |
| Boots of Escaping | 50 | Thigh boots with Dexterity Bonus 4 and Stamina Regeneration 1. |
| Jack in the Box | 20 | A wind-up toy. Double-click it; the jack pops out after nine seconds and closes five seconds later. Wait 20 seconds between uses. |
| Shackled Heart of the Pumpkin King | 20 | Double-click for a brief blood effect around your character. It causes no damage. |

To choose the ring's skill bonus, put the ring in your backpack and double-click it. Choose Archery, Fencing, Mace Fighting, Swordsmanship, Throwing, or Wrestling, then confirm. Treat this as a permanent choice. The client may label the ring **Stirling Silver Ring**.

**The Bag of Tricks works only during October.** It stops working in November even while the dungeon event is still running. Each use has a 10% chance of a Halloween treat; otherwise it gives rancid reindeer meat, a glass candy cane, an elf's severed ears, a suspicious gift box, an insulting doll, or spiked eggnog. The cooldown is a full 24 hours, not a midnight reset.

Hero of the Unloved, Savior of the Demented, and Slayer of the Pumpkin King title deeds are **not offered on this shard**.

## After the event

Under the default schedule, the event switches off on the hourly check after November 2 ends. New artifact awards stop, and ordinary dungeon spawning resumes. Do not assume every remaining enemy has disappeared.

The Researcher and entrance decorations stay after the season. You can still turn in saved artifacts and spend your points. Artifacts, reward points, and purchased prizes have no seasonal expiration in the checked implementation. The Bag of Tricks keeps its October-only use restriction.

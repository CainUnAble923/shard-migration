# Order of the Alembic

Draft checked against project notes and source code on October 21, 2026. Features still to come are marked (planned). Availability on the running shard has not been checked. Numbers below are the reviewed code's defaults; use the guild's displayed requirements and prices if they differ.

## What the Order is

The Order of the Alembic is the guild for Alchemy and Taste Identification, usually called Taste ID. Glassblowing also belongs here and uses your Alchemy skill. Its work covers cures, poisons, explosive potions, and the ingredients and vessels an alchemist needs.

Herbalism and reagent farming will expand the Order's work (planned).

## How to join

Find **Distiller Orla Vance** in the **Little Shop of Alchemy in New Haven, Trammel**. Open the Guild Directory with `[guild`, find the Order, and choose **Show me the way** for directions. Join at the guildmaster.

Joining is free. You need no gold or minimum skill to become an Initiate. Membership, standing, rank, and Alembic Drams belong to your character. Learned recipes belong to your account.

Your starter kit contains 4 empty bottles, a mortar and pestle, and a robe. Each character receives its kit once under the current starter rules.

The **Apprentice task** asks for either Alchemy 50.0 or 10 empty bottles. Complete it at the guildmaster to earn 25 standing and 10 Alembic Drams. If you meet the skill requirement, your bottles are kept. Otherwise the tribute is taken from your backpack, then your bank.

Completing the task satisfies the standing side of Apprentice promotion. Craft promotion also checks Alchemy: you need at least 30.0 to hold Apprentice. Handing over bottles at lower skill does not remove that requirement.

## Ranks and what they open

These are the names currently used by the Order. More distinctive names remain undecided.

Promotion needs both standing and your base Alchemy skill. Equipment bonuses do not count. Taste ID does not count toward this craft requirement. Once a promotion has been recorded, lowering your skill does not take it away.

| Rank | Standing needed | Base Alchemy | What it opens in the reviewed catalog |
|---|---|---|---|
| Initiate | Free joining | None | Starter kit and access to the pattern shop, including the Band II Alchemy book. |
| Apprentice | Apprentice task completed, or 1,000 standing | 30.0 | Band II Alchemy pattern book as a promotion gift, if your account has not learned it. |
| Journeyman | 5,000 | 50.0 | Band III Alchemy book becomes available to buy. |
| Master | 15,000 | 70.0 | Band III Alchemy pattern book as a promotion gift, if your account has not learned it. |
| Grandmaster | 50,000 | 90.0 | Access to the Band V scroll rotation service. The reviewed Alchemy catalog has no eligible Band V designs, so this currently adds no scrolls or new pattern book. |
| Legendary | 100,000 | 100.0 | The top guild rank. Its Band V starter gift needs eligible designs, so the reviewed catalog gives no Alchemy starter scrolls. The guild trial (planned) is waived by default until built. |

The book names reflect a merge rule: small recipe bands share the next book. In the current catalog, Band I folds into Band II, while Band III has its own book. There is no separate Band I or Band IV Alchemy book to collect from this catalog. Learning the shared book also covers the lower band it contains.

## Skills and crafts

| Skill or craft | How it works |
|---|---|
| Alchemy | Use a mortar and pestle to make potions from reagents and other ingredients. Many potion recipes also use an empty bottle. |
| Taste ID | Use the skill on food to check for poison. An unsuccessful check tells you that you cannot discern anything. Potions and filled potion kegs report their known contents. |
| Glassblowing | Learn from the Glassblowing book, then use a blowpipe near a forge with sand. It uses Alchemy, rather than a separate skill. |
| Herbalism (planned) | Forage, cultivate reagent plants, breed strains, and process harvests into reagent bundles. |

### Learning Alchemy patterns

Ordinary Alchemy designs whose minimum skill is 30.0 or less need no pattern book. Higher designs stay hidden until learned. A recipe with its own scroll keeps that separate requirement. Knowing a pattern does not replace the skill or materials needed to craft it.

At Orla's hall page, choose **Pattern books and recipe scrolls**. The current book prices are:

| Book | Price in Alembic Drams |
|---|---|
| Band II Alchemy | 200 |
| Band III Alchemy | 400 |

Promotion gifts offer another route to these books. Read a book from your backpack to teach your account; it is consumed when learned. An already known book is kept. Unread books and the account recipe scrolls can be traded.

The Alchemy menu also includes plant pigment, natural dye, color fixative, crystal granules, softened reeds, and specialized recipes. Some need additional knowledge or their own recipe scroll. Use the menu's hint lines for the patterns you have yet to learn.

### Learning Glassblowing

You need **100.0 base Alchemy** to read **Crafting Glass With Glassblowing**. Glassblower vendors sell the book and blowpipes. The Order's pattern shop does not currently sell this book.

After learning it, you can make bottles, flasks, vials, and other glassware. Glassblowing has its own blowpipe menu and keeps its book requirement instead of using Alchemy pattern bands.

The learned unlock is shared across your account. Each character still needs 100.0 base Alchemy to use the craft, along with a blowpipe, sand, and a nearby forge.

## Work, rewards, and currency

Keep these balances separate:

| Balance | What it does |
|---|---|
| Order standing | Helps determine guild rank. The Apprentice task and the Order's requisitions award it. |
| Alembic Drams | The Order's current currency name. The Apprentice task pays 10. Pattern books cost Drams. The name remains a placeholder. |
| Alchemy bulk order points | Buy rewards from the Alchemy bulk order list. They are separate from Drams and standing. |
| League Renown | Paid by requisitions and used by the League. It is separate from the Order's currency. |

### Alchemy bulk orders

With some Alchemy skill, members can right-click Orla for **Bulk Order Info**. Town Alchemists also issue Alchemy deeds. Fill a small deed with the requested potions; large deeds combine matching completed small deeds. Return a completed deed to the appropriate giver.

Turn-ins pay gold to your bank, Fame, and Alchemy reward points. Use **Claim Rewards** to view the reward list. Banking points keeps a reduced portion for later purchases; check the confirmation before choosing.

Available rewards include a mortar and pestle with 250 uses, special natural dyes, and Alchemy Master Craftsman talismans. Rows labeled **not on the shard yet** cannot be purchased. Bulk order turn-ins do not currently award Alembic Drams or Order standing.

### Requisitions

Open **Requisitions** at the guild hall page to see requests for magic items. The Order's interests include Enhance Potions jewelry, poison-resistant armor, Intelligence jewelry, and lucky hats.

A matching item is consumed on turn-in. You receive Order standing, League Renown, and gold at the Appraiser's price. Guild rank and League rank affect the requests available. This is separate from potion bulk orders.

### Herbalism and reagent growing (planned)

The decided design adds wild herb patches and cultivated reagent plants. Garlic, Ginseng, Mandrake Root, Nightshade, and Blood Moss are intended for the growing and foraging path. Other reagent sources include shoreline gathering or fishing, volcanic mining, and creature drops (planned).

Bowls will keep the standard plant pace. Garden beds, Herbalism, and crafted garden equipment will support larger harvests (planned). Breeding will improve strains, with the best strains coming from player breeding. Some herbs will also serve Cooking and Healing (planned).

An Alembic town farm will rent plots for weekly guild currency, with plot access tied to rank (planned). A quest will offer a choice of mandrake sprout or garden gnome familiar to help tend a home garden, with further tasks unlocked through upgrades (planned).

Herbalism is not implemented in the reviewed code. Do not expect these plant, rent, familiar, or harvest-order features yet. Vendors remain a source of reagents, and existing world reagent pickups are separate from the planned Herbalism system.

## Contradictions

- **Former skill owner:** F-20 says Alchemy moved from the Arcane Society. F-9 and P77 say Alchemy and Taste ID moved from the Healers' Covenant.
- **Apprentice promotion:** P77 describes completing the task as lifting rank to Apprentice. The later promotion code also requires base Alchemy 30.0; the bottle tribute can be completed below that skill.
- **Alchemy books:** P78 records Bands II and IV and says its merged Band IV is sold at rank 3 and granted at rank 4. The current craft registrations and merge code yield Bands II and III, and the shop and gift code check merged bands while processing earlier ranks.
- **Bulk orders:** P77 and P78 report no Alchemy issuer. P123 and the current code provide Alchemy deeds at town Alchemists and Orla. The broader guild-reward design should not be read as an implemented Drams payout for these deeds.
- **Glassblowing menu:** P77 calls it a book unlock inside the Alchemy menu. The craft code provides a separate blowpipe menu using Alchemy skill.
- **Reagent sources:** F-51 starts from reagents being available only through vendors. P260 records existing wild reagent spawners and creature drops, separate from Herbalism.
- **Herbalism decisions:** F-51 says there are no open design questions. P260 retains code-driven questions about crossing, upkeep, reagent spawners, promotion, and plot rules. This page assigns no values to those unresolved details.

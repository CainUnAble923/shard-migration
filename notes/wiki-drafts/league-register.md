# The League Register

Draft checked against the source on October 24, 2026, including the P451 fixes. Prices, capacities, and crafting requirements below are the built-in defaults. Running-shard settings and availability have not been checked. Use the requirements and prices shown in game.

The League Register brings your guild records, remote contracts, and League goods into one book. As your League rank rises, it can turn in ready orders and repair your equipped gear.

## Getting your Register

At **Dull Copper Citizen** or higher, visit Elara Voss, the League Registrar at the New Haven field office. Choose **League Register** beside **Citizen Status** on her main menu. The Register is free, blessed, and bound to your character. Keep it in your backpack and double-click it to open it.

If you lose it, ask for another. A replacement invalidates the old copy and keeps your bound guilds. Leave room in your backpack for the new book.

## Binding guilds

Open **Guilds** and choose a guild under **Bind a guild you belong to**. You must already be a member. Binding adds that guild to your Register; it does not join the guild for you.

The Society of Smiths requires its **Smith Guild Book**, and the Miners' Compact requires its **Dispatch Ledger**. Put the book directly in your backpack, outside any bags. Empty any deeds out first. Binding consumes the book. Other guilds currently bind through membership alone.

| League rank reached | Guild slots |
|---|---:|
| Dull Copper | 2 |
| Bronze | 4 |
| Verite | 6 |
| Blaze | 9 |
| Mythril | 12 |
| Celestial | 18 |

These capacities continue through the ranks between milestones. Choose **Unbind** to free a slot at no charge. If the guild requires a book, you receive a fresh one while you remain a member. If there is no room for that book, the guild stays bound.

If you leave a bound guild, the Register shows it grayed out once, then removes it and frees its slot. Leaving does not return a book or refund anything.

## Finding your way around

| Tab | What you can do |
|---|---|
| **Guilds** | Bind or unbind guilds, see bound guild standing, and open their contracts once unlocked. |
| **Ledger** | Review guild rank, standing, currency, and the next standing threshold for guilds you belong to. |
| **League** | Use combined turn-ins and League repair when your rank allows them. |
| **Bank** | See totals for banked gold and common crafting materials. Bank checks count toward the gold total. |
| **Goods** | Buy backpack upgrades, a League Field Satchel, or a Combined Logbook. |

The Bank tab is a summary. It has no withdrawal button and grants no bank-crafting upgrade.

## Contracts and combined turn-ins

At **Bronze**, use **Contracts** beside a bound guild on the Guilds tab to open its contract ledger from anywhere. The guild's own requirements still apply.

At **Verite**, open **League** and choose **Turn in every ready order**. This submits eligible active work orders for guilds you have bound and still belong to. Each order must meet its normal requirements. Required materials can come from your backpack and bank, and successful orders consume their materials and pay their usual rewards. Unready orders stay active.

With the **Society of Smiths** bound, the same button also turns in completed small and large smith bulk order deeds from your backpack, including deeds inside bags and guild books. Banked deeds are excluded. It uses your Society payout preference: **Always cash out** cashes out; **Always bank** and **Ask each time** bank the Seals without a choice window.

This button handles active guild work orders and completed smith deeds. Use the separate turn-in routes for requisitions and commissions.

## Repairing gear

At **Blaze**, open **League** and choose **League repair**. It repairs your own damaged equipped gear for free, with no cooldown. Gear in your backpack, inside bags, or in your bank is excluded. Equip the piece you want repaired before pressing the button.

Repair deeds and kits offer other options, with no League-rank or user-skill requirement:

| Repair option | What it reaches | Uses |
|---|---|---|
| Field repair deed | One damaged item you wear or carry, including inside a backpack bag | One successful repair |
| Field repair kit | One damaged item you wear or carry per charge | 10 charges by default |
| Remote repair deed | All damaged gear you wear, carry, or keep in your bank, including inside containers | One successful repair pass |

Keep the deed or kit in your backpack. Double-click a field deed or kit, then target your item. The deed or kit must remain in your backpack until you finish targeting. A Remote Repair Deed works immediately when double-clicked in your backpack. It can be used at any League rank, including Blaze and above.

With the default settings, these repairs and the free Blaze action restore current durability to the item's existing maximum without lowering that maximum. They do not restore maximum durability already lost. An undamaged or unsuitable target consumes no field deed or kit charge. A Remote Repair Deed is kept if nothing needs repair.

Only items with durability can be repaired. A tool's remaining uses are separate; these repairs do not refill use counters.

### Making repair items

Blacksmiths find these recipes under **Repair Items** in the smithing menu. The skill values below are the recipe minimums, not guaranteed-success levels. The person using the finished item does not need those skills.

| Item | Blacksmithy minimum | Arms Lore minimum | Materials |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Field repair deed | 60 | 90 | 5 iron ingots |
| Field repair kit | 80 | 110 | 30 iron ingots |
| Remote repair deed | 90 | 120 | 20 iron ingots and 1 star sapphire |

## League goods and backpack upgrades

Buy these on **Goods**. Purchases spend your account's Renown balance and gold. They leave Lifetime Renown and your League rank intact. Gold comes from your backpack first, then your bank.

| Purchase | League rank required | Renown | Gold |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Combined Logbook | Dull Copper | 500 | 50 |
| League Field Satchel | Bronze | 5,000 | 500 |
| Backpack capacity: 150 items | Bronze | 2,000 | 200 |
| Backpack capacity: 175 items | Verite | 6,000 | 600 |
| Backpack capacity: 200 items | Blaze | 15,000 | 1,500 |
| Backpack capacity: 250 items | Mythril | 30,000 | 3,000 |

Backpack upgrades belong to the character. Buy each step in order, starting from the normal 125-item capacity. Each listed price pays for that step, and the Goods tab shows your next step. These upgrades raise item capacity only. They do not increase the weight you can carry or enlarge other containers.

If your pack cannot receive a purchased satchel or logbook, you see **Make room in your pack first.** The purchase takes no payment. Logbook replacement also keeps your old books if delivery fails.

### League Field Satchel

The Field Satchel holds one existing guild satchel from each family:

| Family | Guild currency used for insertion |
|---|---|
| Ore satchel | Miners' Compact |
| Lumber satchel | Foresters' Union |
| Hunter's Satchel | Rangers' League |
| Essence satchel | Artificers' Order |

Drop your guild satchels into it. Each successful insertion costs **50 of that family's guild currency** by default. A refused insertion costs nothing. Taking a satchel out is free, and repositioning it inside the same Field Satchel is free. Taking it out and inserting it again costs the fee again.

Put resources into the appropriate guild satchel inside, rather than dropping loose resources directly on the Field Satchel. You supply the guild satchels separately.

Keep the Field Satchel **directly in your character's backpack**. There, it and its contents count as one item toward your backpack limit. Inside another bag, in the bank, or in house storage, its contents count normally. Your backpack's weight limit still applies.

Current limitation: a loaded Field Satchel may be refused when placed back into your backpack, even though it would count as one item once inside.

### Combined Logbook

Double-click the Combined Logbook in your backpack to open the **Prospector's Logbook** or **Forester's Logbook**. Each option requires membership in its guild.

Buying it removes the old Prospector's and Forester's logbooks from your backpack, including those in bags. Your discovery records remain: the books read the same saved records. You do not need to bring the old books to buy the combined one.

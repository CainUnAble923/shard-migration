# Mounts and riding

Draft checked against source code on October 24, 2026. Availability, spawns, settings, and the player package on the running shard have not been checked. Numbers marked as defaults may differ there.

For a first mount, buy a **horse from an Animal Trainer**. Wild mounts need taming. The League and achievement mounts below arrive as statuettes that call bonded living pets. Veteran reward mounts use reusable figurines and account age.

The custom reward routes, prices, and horse-level stats below are the current source values and still await final confirmation.

## Riding and caring for a mount

Stand next to your living mount and double-click it. You must own it, and it cannot be poisoned or a dead pet. Use your client's dismount action to get off. Dismount before changing mounts.

Living mounts keep using follower capacity while ridden. Pets and companions share that capacity. Use Animal Lore to check a particular animal: training can change its follower cost and taming requirement.

Gargoyles cannot ride animals, including ethereal mounts. Some transformed bodies, areas that forbid mounts, temporary mount blocks, and an open trade also prevent riding. A mount's appearance does not remove these rules.

Feed living mounts suitable food and keep them safe when dismounted. Bonding allows a dead pet to be resurrected; it does not prevent death. To store a living mount, dismount and ask an Animal Trainer to **stable** it. Use **claim** to retrieve it. The trainer needs stable space and payment, and will refuse dead pets, summoned creatures, and pets busy fighting. Unload a giant beetle before stabling it.

## The red riding dragon

The **red riding dragon** and its colorable riding-dragon companion are **staff-only for now**. Players cannot tame them, and the current code gives them no shop, achievement, loot-box, or wild-spawn route.

Their current stats are horse-level placeholders, with one follower slot. A future player reward route and final abilities have not been decided. Do not hunt ordinary dragons expecting to tame one of these riding dragons.

The **riding drake** below is a separate reward mount. It is not the red riding dragon.

## League reward mounts

Open **League Shop** from `[achievements` or through **Elara Voss, the League Registrar in New Haven**. You need **1,750 Achievement Points (AP)** to unlock each of these rows, enough Renown to pay, and room in your backpack.

AP is a permanent score and is not spent. The Renown price is spent each time you buy a statuette.

| Mount | Renown |
|---|---:|
| Chestnut draft horse | 750 |
| Bay draft horse | 750 |
| Grey draft horse | 750 |
| Palomino draft horse | 750 |
| Barded warhorse | 1,000 |
| Gilded charger | 1,500 |

The shop's Bronze, Silver, and Gold loot boxes do not include these mounts. Their reward pools stop below the mounts' AP requirement.

## Achievement reward mounts

These statuettes are direct rewards for newly earning the listed account achievement. They are separate from any loot box the achievement awards. Your characters share the achievement record, so earning it again on another character does not give another statuette.

| Mount | Achievement | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| Moonlit unicorn | Frequent Flyer: Unlimited Edition | Earn the facet visits for Trammel, Felucca, Ilshenar, Malas, and Tokuno |
| Bonewalker steed | They Were Already Dead Once | Slay 50 undead |
| Emberhoof | Extremely Normal Behavior | Slay 10,000 creatures, after the earlier kill achievements |
| War wolf | Deeply, Embarrassingly Invested | Reach 500 AP, after the earlier League achievement prerequisites |

Visit Trammel before Felucca for the facet achievement chain. Ter Mur is not part of this particular five-facet goal. Some of these achievements stay hidden until earned.

The moonlit unicorn has no male-rider restriction. It uses the reward-mount rules, rather than the ordinary unicorn's rules.

Accounts that earned an achievement before its mount reward was added are not automatically paid a replacement by the current award code. A catch-up policy is unconfirmed.

## Loot-box reward mounts

These pools are built, but a normal player source for eligible Tamer boxes has not been verified. The default achievement-to-box mapping does not award Tamer boxes. Boss boxes have award code, but project notes report trouble with awards during ordinary boss kills. Treat these routes as **unconfirmed availability**, not a farming promise.

| Eligible box | Possible mount statuettes | Default chance of a mount statuette |
|---|---|---:|
| Tamer, tier 5 | Riding drake, riding bear, riding wolf, saddled horse | 10% |
| Tamer, tier 6 | Riding drake, riding bear, riding wolf, saddled horse | 20% |
| Boss, tier 6 | Marsh raptor, dusk raptor, sky raptor, bog strider | 15% |

The chance is for a mount from that pool, not for each named mount. A qualifying box can give no mount. Lower-tier boxes and other themes do not use these pools. Contents are fixed when a box is awarded; waiting to open it does not improve the roll.

For box ownership and opening locations, see [Achievements and loot boxes](achievements-and-loot-boxes.md).

## Using a League, achievement, or loot-box mount statuette

1. Keep the statuette in your backpack.
2. Be alive and leave **one follower slot** free.
3. Double-click the statuette. It calls a living mount beside you, bonded to you and following you.
4. Double-click the mount to ride it.

A successful call **consumes the statuette**. Being dead or having too many followers leaves it unused. It is not a reusable summon button, and dismounting does not turn the animal back into a statuette.

These reward mounts cannot be tamed. Each uses one follower slot and currently has horse-level stats. Names such as raptor, drake, unicorn, and warhorse do not give them the combat abilities of similarly named monsters. They still need ordinary living-pet care and can be stabled.

Draft horses, the barded warhorse, gilded charger, moonlit unicorn, bonewalker, emberhoof, and saddled horse eat fruit, vegetables, grain, and hay. Raptors, the bog strider, war wolf, riding drake, and riding wolf eat meat. The riding bear accepts meat or fish.

Use the shard's updated player package for the custom mounts. Built art does not establish that the matching package has already been published to players.

## Veteran reward figurines

Veteran rewards depend on **real account age**, not League rank, AP, or Renown. The source defaults enable them and use **30 days per reward level**. When you have an unused reward choice, the login reward notice offers the selection menu. Claiming a figurine spends a choice.

| Reward level | Account age at the default interval | Mount choices added |
|---:|---:|---|
| 3 | 90 days | Ethereal horse, ostard, llama |
| 4 | 120 days | Ethereal ki-rin, unicorn, ridgeback |
| 5 | 150 days | Ethereal swamp dragon, beetle |
| 9 | 270 days | Rideable polar bear figurine |
| 10 | 300 days | Ethereal cu sidhe |
| 11 | 330 days | Ethereal reptalon |
| 12 | 360 days | Ethereal hiryu |

Carry the figurine and double-click it to summon and ride, with one follower slot free. Summoning takes a short cast that can be interrupted. Dismounting returns the reusable figurine. These are blessed items, not living pets to feed, stable, or resurrect.

Reward-marked figurines also check the user's account age. Receiving one from someone else does not remove that requirement. Their old menu labels may say years even though the source default interval is 30 days.

## Other reward mounts

The **Compact Mule** is a living pack mount with its own League errand and upgrade path. At Dull Copper rank, take **The Compact's Mule** from the New Haven Registrar and deliver **50 Iron Ore and 25 Iron Ingots**. See [Companions and evo pets](companions-and-evo-pets.md) for its progression.

Boss loot also includes **Paroxysmus' Swamp Dragon** and the **Grizzled Mare**, each through its own statuette. Chief Paroxysmus and Monstrous Interred Grizzle respectively have a 5% statuette drop in source. Encounter access on the running shard is unverified. Use the statuette from your backpack and confirm to release a bonded living mount; a successful release consumes it. These two mounts disappear if released from your control.

An evo pet that looks like a horse, raptor, unicorn, or other mount does not become rideable just because of its appearance. Evo pets and the reward mounts on this page are separate creatures.

# Next test release: patch notes

Draft covering changes merged since player package 2026.10.23.1001, through October 26, 2026 (server version 2026.10.26.8). These changes are pending the next test release. Update through the launcher for the new art, menu labels, and client features. Features marked **test shard only** need staff setup or activation.

## Tutorial: test shard only

- Rook shows the core lessons first, then the optional lessons. Brighter markers, readable windows, and clearer hints explain what to click and where to go. Missing practice clothes, bags, or tokens can be replaced through Hint and arrow.
- Combat, healing, and all six magic lessons are playable in the training yard. The trades yard adds gathering, camping, all nine crafts, bard and thief practice, and walking tours about houses, travel, reputation, and shard systems. The farm teaches herding, taming, and pet care. You keep what you craft.
- Lesson equipment is loaned and collected afterward. Cloth, logs, bows, and arrows can no longer be cut up or kept, and temporary Hiding and tithing changes are restored after a restart.
- Each account gets one starter pet, from either the Farmhand or graduation. Graduation shows pictures of the choices, and Rook keeps an unchosen pet waiting through logout or a restart. The farm vet revives pets free while you are Young; graduation also includes an elixir of rebirth.
- The Farmhand supplies up to three stacks of ten bandages per account. A full pack no longer spends an allowance. The patch-up lesson requires a successful heal, and players outside the tutorial cannot attack its creatures.
- Lessons stay with the original trainee. If that character is deleted or moved to another account, your next character can continue with lessons and rewards kept.

## New skills and features

- **Persuasion replaces Begging**, keeping your existing skill points. Use it on a person to open an asking menu. The client skill list may still say Begging until a later client update.
- Ask a banker about work to receive a sealed courier letter. Drag it onto the named recipient to deliver it and train Persuasion, up to 200. Beginners deliver within town; harder jobs go farther. You may hold one job at a time, including a letter in your bank, and have three hours to deliver. Letters currently train skill without paying gold.
- Ask townsfolk for help to receive a few coins or bread without losing karma. Each account can receive up to 50 gold and two loaves per day. Successful charity teaches only below 20 Persuasion; courier work trains beyond that.
- **Tracking Ping** unlocks at 90 trained Tracking. Use Tracking and choose Ping, or type `[TrackingPing`, to sense nearby creatures on your floor. Chat shows their numbers and the danger of hostile creatures; `[TrackingPing results` lists them. A ping costs 15 stamina, reveals you, and has a 30-second wait. Its range and duration grow through 200 Tracking.
- At 120 trained Tracking, Ping can also sense visible players within 12 tiles, warning them that they feel watched. Hidden players and the pets of hidden owners stay hidden. Dungeons and mini map dots are not supported yet.
- Animal trainers now sell elixirs of rebirth for dead bonded pets. Rangers' League members pay less at their guildmaster. Wait for the pet's body to fade, then use the elixir beside its ghost.

## Mounts and evolving pets

- New mount statuettes call a bonded mount and are used once. The mounts can be stabled and shrunk, use one follower slot, and currently have horse-level stats. The updated player package is required to see them.
- At 1,750 Achievement Points, the League shop unlocks chestnut, bay, gray, and palomino draft horses for 750 Renown each, a barded warhorse for 1,000, and a gilded charger for 1,500.
- New achievement rewards include a bonewalker steed for 50 undead kills, an emberhoof for 10,000 kills, a moonlit unicorn for visiting every facet, and a war wolf for 500 Achievement Points. Previously earned achievements do not automatically grant these new mounts yet.
- Top-tier Tamer boxes can contain a riding drake, bear, wolf, or saddled horse. Top-tier Boss boxes can contain a marsh, dusk, or sky raptor, or a bog strider.
- Horse and lizard evolution lines are ready for staff-supplied tests, with foal and hatchling growth stages. They are not tutorial choices. Later warhorse, raptor, and final forms still wait on unfinished owner loops and the Bond Trial. The dog's final appearance list adds a great gray wolf alongside the windrunner.

## Guilds, crafting, and cleanup

- **Masterwork Hammer of Hephaestus:** Society of Smiths Masters with 15,000 standing and 100 Blacksmithy can upgrade a Reinforced Hammer. The third tier has 800 uses, +15 Blacksmithy while held, and up to 5,000 familiarity per metal. Familiar metals return some ingots after successful crafts, up to a quarter at full familiarity.
- Hold the Masterwork Hammer at an anvil and forge, choose Craft This Order, and target a smith bulk order in your pack. It crafts and fills the order through normal attempts, spending your materials and hammer uses. It stops when finished, interrupted, or out of supplies.
- Large orders first use matching completed small deeds. **For a partly filled large order, put small deeds you want to keep in a bag first: the hammer can consume a whole deed for a partial requirement.** Hammer exhaustion and charge recovery still have known issues. Tier four is not available.
- Bulk order windows and tooltips show guild members their guild's payout. Smiths see standing and Seals or cash-out gold. Reopen the deed for a fresh quote after changing guilds or trading it.
- Abandoning a smith commission through the guild book starts the same wait as abandoning it at the guildmaster. Small and large commissions share that wait. Fletching, carpentry, and tinkering bulk orders now reject shop-bought items.
- The Miners' Compact sells Mining For Quality Stone from Journeyman rank for 50 Mining Vouchers. Reading it needs 100 trained Mining. Stone Contracts appear after you learn stone mining; contracts already held can still be turned in.
- Mine Iron Ore and Mine Dull Copper Ore are now quotas for 100 iron or 50 dull copper ore mined after accepting. You keep the ore. The dull copper job needs Journeyman rank, 65 Mining, and a reported Dull Copper discovery. Older ore contracts keep their original turn-in requirements.
- When restoring Jacob's Pickaxe, the Liaison can cover missing vouchers for 100 gold each. The confirmation shows both prices.
- Civic Contracts now count Clean Up points earned after accepting, instead of civic waste bundles. Turning one in keeps your store points available to spend. Give leftover bundles to the Sanitation Warden for 10 points each.
- Right-click a trash bag to Clear a corpse or Clear corpses nearby and earn Clean Up points. Clearing deletes everything still inside, with confirmation when loot remains. Player and bonded pet corpses are protected, and other players' loot rights are respected. Nearby clearing uses only the corpses you approved; moving away or putting the bag away cancels it.
- The Artificers' welcome and guildmaster pages now explain that the Apprentice task pays the first standing and Essence Shards, and learned formula tiers determine your imbuing limits.

## High Seas fishing

- Fish delivery orders are ready for staff to place fish mongers at Britain, Moonglow, Trinsic, Vesper, Jhelom, Skara Brae, Papua, and the Sea Market. Bring your boat near a monger to request an order, fill its shipping crate, and deliver it to another port. `[FishMongerStatus` shows your standing.
- Current orders award first-tier rewards only: some bait, lava lobster traps, Fishing Guide volumes 1 or 2, Fishing power scrolls, or plain poles. Higher standing does not unlock the later rewards yet.
- Apply fish bait to a pole, or crab and lobster bait to a trap. It doubles the chance of the matching catch; enhanced bait triples it. Each catch spends one use.
- Magic hooks, lava-pole fishing, and the Oracle of the Sea's Charybdis route are built, but normal orders cannot supply hooks, the Oracle, or Charybdis bait yet. These need staff supplies to test. Lava hooks allow lava catches, including cracked rocks you can split with an axe and stone footwear that prevents movement while worn.
- SOS wrecks can bring up oars, fish heads, unfinished furniture, a yellow polka dot bikini, or a rare ship's strongbox. Charybdis drops two to five fish pies. New fish-pie recipes still lack normal ingredient supplies, and some pie effects remain unfinished. Corgul's island access is not open.
- Fish orders still have item-handling and delivery issues, and no usable player cancellation option. An unfinished crate can block dry docking. Ask staff for help with an order you cannot finish.

## Fixes

- Companions cannot be traded away or go wild from neglect. Their weapon damage survives a restart, and companions left out at logout or restart are safely put away and returned at login.
- Unraveling a container affects only the items you approved. Later additions, blessed items, and items behind locked containers are left alone.
- Guildmaster purchases check that you are nearby and have pack space before charging. Failed deliveries are refunded. The tutorial vet no longer charges for a pet you walked away from.
- League Register purchases and guild removal no longer drop replacements at your feet when your pack is full. Blaze repair covers worn gear; remote repair deeds still cover carried and banked gear.
- The League Field Satchel accepts filled guild satchels, routes resources into them, and charges no fee for refused insertions or rearranging contents. It counts as one item only directly in your backpack; its contents count in bank and house storage. Ready-order turn-in finds smith deeds inside bags and books.
- Hunter's Satchels accept only their intended resources through every drop route. Hides dropped into the satchel's open window are cut into leather.
- First-time boss loot boxes now reach qualifying participants. Eligible boss and paragon loot can receive its rare Self Repair roll. Pet, summon, and provoked-creature kills count toward the League bestiary; killing pets no longer gives levelable gear experience or Butcher's Eye rewards. Some hidden achievements now check their conditions more carefully.
- Exodus exits resume after a restart without creating another boss. Both Asclepius staves help bandage healing, Exodus rewards have cleanup value, and revealed encounter chests close out after restart.
- Void Pool cauldron charges and battle records survive a restart, every improving player's best score is recorded, and old waves are cleared before a new battle.
- Repeated wrong guesses no longer break Underworld puzzle boards or trap-training kits. Disabled mini champions stay off after restart, and the magic key retrieves its puzzle pieces from nested bags.
- Old Haven's Rootdeep entrance is now a wide burrow in the ruined bank, with the ladder returning you outside the broken wall. It needs the matching map update and staff setup. Missing server map data closes the way down safely; the exit still works. Young players receive a protection warning.
- Extra League jewelry can always be removed, follows equipment rules, and counts toward levelable gear, imbuing effects, Fortune's Favor, and jewelry achievements.
- Eodon's cliff vines refuse climbing while paralyzed. Corrected wildlife placement restores rare white and black tiger proportions when staff refresh the spawners. Staff-supplied dragon turtle hatchlings now keep their stats and lose skill on first tame instead.
- Treasures of Sorcerer's Dungeon removes all event undead when it ends, including pumpkin hatchlings and survivors in earlier rooms. Jack's event pumpkins stay inside the dungeon and stop with the event. Killing tamed pets gives no artifact credit.
- Tracking Ping refunds stamina and its wait if too many creatures arrive during a scan. Old menus respect later skill waits, duel-hidden players and their pets stay hidden, and expired pings stop sensing even during server lag.
- Persuasion checks your location, sight, and condition again when you choose an answer. Banked letters still count as active jobs. Bankers in town subregions, gypsy bankers, and minters now work correctly with courier letters.

## UI and launcher

- Options > Interface > Appearance adds personal looks for backpacks, bags, metal chests and bank boxes, crates, and spell scrolls. Classic remains the default; only you see your choices.
- Guild and League menus use a stone and bronze frame in the updated Shattered Legacy client. Other clients keep their classic frame.
- Exhausted trees leave stumps at ground level. With the updated shard client, the chopped tree disappears until its wood returns; some trees may still leave floating leaves. Polearms now give the correct no-wood message at an exhausted stump.
- The launcher shows progress while preparing shard art and maps, prepares them during updates, and uses the previous art if preparation takes too long. Unreadable files no longer discard the art list, simultaneous starts share a rebuild, interrupted swaps recover, and an update timeout no longer causes a second wait.
- Play Shattered Legacy now defaults to the test shard.

## Early features: test shard only

These are off by default or require staff-created items and sites. Ask staff to arrange a test.

- **Item identification:** new magic loot weapons and armor can start unidentified. Appraisal names magic from 30 and reads values from 60; Arms Lore assesses resistance, damage types, and condition from 30 and measures them from 60. Difficult items need more skill. Discoveries are shared by everyone looking at the item. Existing and crafted gear stays known, and protection labels remain visible.
- **Guard witnesses:** a pilot in Trammel's Cove records who could see crimes and which guards could hear calls. Guard responses are unchanged; walking patrols are not enabled.
- **League warrants:** staff can supply account-bound investigation warrants for the Siegebreaker and Hollow Oak. They give directions, but the encounters and warrant shop are not open.
- **League Echo:** staff can test a blue Stygian Dragon Echo with a Mythril League character. It uses two follower slots, lasts 20 seconds, fights wild creatures only, and has a 30-minute account wait. It fades if you disconnect, die, leave it behind, or enter a protected place; Felucca is excluded. No Tome or Registrar reward is available yet.
- **Townsfolk:** staff can place Mara and Ellis in New Haven. Double-click, choose Talk, or greet them nearby for private directions and conversation. Inn rest is not built yet.
- **Weapon milestones:** at item level 100, a crafted levelable weapon can spend 100 points and one property slot on Stormstrike or Emberstrike through `[ItemLevel`. Damaging hits against wild creatures have a 5% chance to deal 12 energy or fire damage, with a shared 10-second wait. Player-level restrictions and the full imbuing integration are still pending.
- **Town houses:** staff can assign free test leases in empty town buildings, with house doors, lockdowns, friends, town guards, and the account's one-house limit. Player leasing, rent, and sublets are not ready. **Empty the building before staff end a lease: belongings are not packed for you yet.**
